# 03 — Email 02: Why This Result Suits You

## Send timing

Approximately one day after completion, unless the customer has purchased, entered checkout or changed their recommendation.

## Job to be done

Explain the recommendation clearly enough that the customer understands the match instead of treating it as a mysterious algorithmic answer.

## Core message

> Your recommendation reflects the result, format and timing preferences you selected.

## Primary CTA

**SEE WHY IT MATCHES**

Destination: the recommendation page anchored to the explanation section.

## Content structure

1. Short headline connecting expertise to the customer’s answers.
2. Three explanation modules at most:
   - desired depth;
   - preferred application format;
   - development time or control preference.
3. A concise “What to expect” summary.
4. Approved product image or texture detail.
5. Primary CTA.
6. Quiet link to retake or adjust answers.

## Explanation language

Use transparent cause-and-effect language:

> Because you selected [answer], this recommendation prioritises [relevant benefit].

Avoid:

- “Our AI analysed your skin” unless a real, documented AI system performed that task;
- dermatological or medical claims;
- absolute guarantees;
- assumptions not present in the stored result.

## Suggested subject lines

- Why this is your Sunless match
- The thinking behind your recommendation
- A closer look at your matched result

## Preview text

See how your preferred depth, format and timing shaped the recommendation.

## “What to expect” content

State only verified product facts, such as:

- expected depth range;
- buildability;
- development window;
- recommended number of coats;
- finish or format characteristics.

Do not promise an identical outcome for every customer.

## Design direction

This email should be more explanatory than Email 01:

- narrow editorial text column;
- up to three simple live-text explanation rows;
- one supporting product or application image;
- no decorative pseudo-science diagrams;
- no crowded comparison table.

## Dynamic data

- questionnaire answers used in the recommendation;
- approved explanation strings tied to each answer;
- product facts from the current catalogue;
- result URL and retake URL.

If fewer than two reliable reasons are available, shorten the email rather than inventing additional personalisation.

## Plain-text version

List each answer and its recommendation consequence in plain language, followed by the result and retake links.
