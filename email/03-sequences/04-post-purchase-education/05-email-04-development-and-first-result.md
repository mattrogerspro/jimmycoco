# Email 04 — Development and First Result

## Role

Set realistic expectations for how the colour develops, what the customer may see during the process and how to assess the finished result.

## Default timing

Send according to the approved development profile, usually one to three days after confirmed or estimated delivery. Timing should be delayed when the product has not arrived or a service issue is open.

## Primary objective

Prevent normal development behaviour from being mistaken for product failure and help the customer understand the first result.

## Content hierarchy

1. What to expect during development
2. Product-specific timing
3. Guide-colour or initial appearance explanation where applicable
4. Rinse, shower or finish instructions
5. How the final colour may settle
6. Calm troubleshooting route

## Expectation standards

Only use approved product guidance. Where relevant, explain:

- the difference between guide colour and developed colour;
- when colour begins to appear;
- when the result is reasonably assessed;
- rinse temperature and cleanser guidance;
- temporary transfer or odour expectations when substantiated;
- how a second application or additional coat changes depth;
- when not to reapply immediately.

Do not guarantee an identical colour outcome for every customer.

## First-result branch

When the customer has explicitly reported a result, adapt the message:

- **happy:** reinforce aftercare and invite the customer to save the routine;
- **lighter than expected:** offer approved buildability guidance;
- **deeper than expected:** provide approved fade and future-application guidance;
- **uneven or unclear:** prioritise support and troubleshooting, not a review request;
- **adverse reaction:** stop automation and route to the approved safety process.

Do not infer satisfaction from an email open or site visit.

## Subject-line territories

- What to expect as your colour develops
- Your first Sunless result, explained
- From application to finished colour
- When and how to assess your result

## CTA

Primary CTA options:

- `See the development guide`
- `Understand your result`
- `Get application support`

## Proof restraint

Relevant result imagery may be used only when it clarifies realistic outcomes and follows the proof, consent and disclosure standards. Do not use manipulated before-and-after imagery.

## Validation

Confirm exact product development profile, delivery state, no support issue, current approved instructions and functioning destination links.

## Success signals

Development-guide engagement, reduced expectation-related contacts, appropriate support routing and positive self-reported first-use confidence.
