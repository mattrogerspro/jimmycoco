# Claims register

**Read this before writing anything.**

The spray tanning industry states a great deal as fact that is not established.
Much of it is repeated by brands considerably larger than us. This register
exists so that nobody writes it into an article — and so that we can do the
thing no competitor does, which is say plainly what is and isn't known.

Three sections: what must not be published, what is genuinely disputed, and what
is solid.

---

## 1. Do not publish without further verification

Widely repeated. No supporting evidence found. **These do not appear as fact
under any framing, including hedged framing** — "some therapists find that…" is
still publishing it.

| Claim | What we found |
|---|---|
| **"DHA resistance"** as a physiological phenomenon | No evidence it exists. Draelos explains the same observation better: colour depth is proportional to stratum corneum thickness. |
| **Hormonal effects** (pregnancy, PCOS, HRT) on DHA development | No peer-reviewed evidence. Every claim traces back to salon blogs. |
| **A "15% FDA limit"** | **Does not exist.** 21 CFR 73.2150 contains no numeric cap. This one is repeated almost universally. |
| **Erythrulose "makes tans last longer"** | Sources contradict each other. |
| **Green or grey cast on olive skin** as a verified mechanism | Asserted everywhere, verified nowhere. Note: green *underarms* from aluminium in deodorant **is** verified — different thing. |
| **Any specific PSI or CFM figure** for spray tan equipment | No published standard exists. |
| **Any ventilation ACH or CFM standard**, or implying a legal requirement exists | No such standard. No such requirement expressed numerically. |
| **Shaving / waxing interval** as an evidenced figure | The intervals everyone quotes have no evidenced basis. |
| **Benzoyl peroxide's effect on a DHA tan** | Unevidenced. |
| **Acetone as a gun cleaner** | One source recommends it. It will destroy Teflon seals. Do not repeat under any circumstances. |

**The correct treatment** is to address these head-on as unevidenced beliefs.
That's not a limitation — it's the most valuable content in the programme.
Example:

> "DHA resistance" is widely discussed in the profession. We could find no
> published evidence that it exists as a physiological phenomenon. What is
> documented is that colour depth is proportional to stratum corneum thickness
> (Draelos) — which produces the same observation, with a different cause and a
> different fix.

## 2. Genuine industry disagreements

Two or more credible sources contradict each other and nothing settles it.
**Present as disagreements. Name who holds which position. Do not pick a side.**

| Question | The disagreement |
|---|---|
| **Violet base and skin tone** | goGLOW: violet suits **cool** skin. Sjolie: violet suits **warm** skin. Directly opposite advice from two credible trainers. No study either way. |
| **Higher DHA %** | Darker, or faster? Sources split. |
| **Patch test timing** | 24h vs 48h. The training standard says 48. Practice varies. **Your insurer's wording is the requirement that binds you** — that's the honest practical answer. |
| **Pregnancy** | Tommy's: unsafe. MotherToBaby: no data. UK training: 16-week rule. Three positions, three different bases. |
| **Application sequence** | No standard exists. Every trainer teaches their own. |

## 3. Solid — build on these

Strong, under-used, and almost entirely absent from trade content. Full
citations in [`source-library.md`](source-library.md).

| Source | Established finding |
|---|---|
| **ACS Omega kinetics paper** (PMC9753197) | The Maillard mechanism; the effect of pH and temperature. **Temperature is the single most influential variable on browning intensity.** |
| **UK SAG-CS 2024 opinion** | The only regulator modelling of spray booth exposure, with actual margins of safety by booth type. **UK limits: leave-on self-tan 14%, rinse-off 22.5%. EU limit 10%.** The strongest safety asset available to us. |
| **Draelos** | Colour depth is proportional to stratum corneum thickness. Explains dark knees, light faces, and why exfoliation matters — replaces an entire genre of folklore in one sentence. |
| **Jung et al.** | DHA-treated skin generates **>180% more free radicals under UV**. A DHA tan gives roughly **SPF 3–4** and is not sun protection. |
| **21 CFR 73.2150** | The actual FDA position — and the document that disproves the "15% limit". |

## 4. Blocked — commercially significant, unresolved

> **Professional rapid solutions are sold across the industry at 18% DHA, which
> exceeds every published regulator figure for leave-on self-tan (UK 14%, EU
> 10%).** The UK rinse-off figure is 22.5%, and whether a booth application is
> correctly categorised as rinse-off is precisely the unresolved point.

**This does not affect our own product.** The Malibu professional litre is 10% DHA (confirmed 8 Aug 2026) — at the EU limit and comfortably inside the UK leave-on figure of 14%. The unresolved question is about the 18% rapid solutions sold elsewhere in the industry, which is a point in our favour rather than a risk to us.

**Nothing about DHA percentage compliance publishes until this is resolved.**
Not in the compliance pillar, not in the craft pillar, not in an FAQ answer.
Resolving it is research task 2 in [`README.md`](README.md#what-still-needs-doing--where-new-research-effort-should-go).

## Adding to this register

Any time you research a claim and can't source it, add it here rather than
quietly leaving it out of one article. The register is the institutional memory
— its value is that the next person doesn't repeat the search.

Format: the claim, what you looked for, what you found, and the date.
