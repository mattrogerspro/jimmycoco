# Email 01 — We Have Missed You

## Role

Reopen the relationship gently and remind the customer what Sunless can help them achieve.

This is not a guilt message. It should not mention the exact duration of inactivity unless that wording has been explicitly approved and is useful.

## Default timing

Send when the customer first crosses the approved lapse threshold and all eligibility checks pass.

## Primary objective

Generate a relevant return visit without relying on urgency or a discount.

## Message hierarchy

1. Warm reintroduction
2. One relevant product, routine or shade-guidance route
3. A concise reminder of the brand’s expert method
4. One primary CTA
5. Optional preference-management link

## Subject-line territories

- A little Sunless inspiration for you
- Ready to rediscover your glow?
- Your Sunless routine, whenever you are ready
- A fresh look at effortless colour

Avoid:

- Where have you been?
- We noticed you disappeared
- Your tan must have run out
- Last chance to come back

## Personalisation

Use only reliable context:

- previously purchased product;
- established routine category;
- completed shade-match result that remains current;
- relevant new alternative when the old product is unavailable;
- first-time versus repeat-customer status.

Do not state that a customer loved a product merely because they purchased it.

## Content routes

### Known repeat-product route

Show the previously repurchased product or its approved successor, with a current CTA such as `Return to your routine`.

### Choice-reset route

When previous preference may be stale, direct to `Find my match` or `Explore the collection`.

### First-time-customer route

Focus on making the next experience easier: preparation, application confidence or a simpler product path.

## Visual structure

1. Header
2. Editorial headline and short introduction
3. One dominant product or guidance module
4. One expert reassurance
5. Primary CTA
6. Preference link and footer

## Asset rules

Use approved source assets only. Product packaging, customer skin, celebrity photographs and result imagery must not be generatively altered.

## Success event

A qualified return session, shade-match start, product view, cart creation or purchase.

## Exit and handoff

Purchase exits win-back. Cart or checkout transfers to the relevant recovery flow. Support or complaint activity transfers to service ownership.
