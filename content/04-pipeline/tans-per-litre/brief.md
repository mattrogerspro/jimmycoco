## Primary reader

**The owner-operator**, working out what to order and whether her cost per tan is
right. Equally **the mobile therapist**, for whom solution is a larger share of a
smaller cost base.

## The question she typed

> "how many spray tans in a litre"

Variants: "how much solution per spray tan" · "ml per spray tan" · "spray tan
solution per client" · "how many litres do I need a month"

## What she gets in the first hundred words

28 to the litre, about 36ml a tan, £2.14 of solution — and the honest note that
her own number will differ, with the method to find it.

## Why us, why now

The gap the strategy document records as "forum threads": four of the top six
results for this query are SalonGeek threads dating from 2008–2018. Where the
forum does not rank, US brands do, in dollars.

Nobody states the figure *and* what it rests on. The 36ml is derivable from the
rating and nobody has bothered to derive it.

## Claims this piece makes

- [x] 28 full-body tans per litre — **product figure, confirmed by Matt 8 Aug 2026**
- [x] 10% DHA — product figure, matches `pro-site/app/lib/specs.ts`
- [x] 1,000ml ÷ 28 = 35.7ml per tan — arithmetic
- [x] £60 ÷ 28 = £2.14 — arithmetic
- [x] 7.1% of a £30 ticket — arithmetic
- [x] +1 tan per litre ≈ £46/year at 624 tans — arithmetic
- [x] 26→30 spread ≈ £223/year — arithmetic

**No unsourced numeric range for yield.** The earlier draft of the cost article
quoted 24–32; that was invented and has been removed everywhere. 28 is the
product figure and the article tells her to measure her own rather than trust any
range we could publish.

## Open question — product naming

The article refers to "Sunless by Jimmy Coco professional solution" and links to
`/products/malibu-professional-spray-1l`. Three names are in play across the
repository and at least one picture is wrong:

- `pro-site/app/lib/specs.ts` — "Malibu Professional Spray Tan Solution 1L"
- `content/` and the brand notes — "Malibu 1 Ltr Professional Spray"
- `website/11-content-hub/` — three litres: Laguna (Light/Medium), Malibu
  (Medium/Dark), Malibu (Dark/Extra Dark)

The article is written to survive any answer — it names the brand rather than the
variant in the body, and the only variant name appears in the citation. Confirm
before publishing so the citation and any product link are right.
