# 10 — Final AI Results Gallery Prompts

## Production rule

AI is used to design the interface concept and surrounding editorial system. It must not invent, retouch, recreate or alter customer evidence photographs.

Insert approved result images into the final composition as unchanged source assets. Preserve original pixels, aspect ratio and colour. Do not generate synthetic before-and-after people.

---

# Prompt A — Desktop Results Gallery Landing Page

Create one ultra-premium 1700px-wide desktop ecommerce Results Gallery page for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**.

Use the attached approved Sunless homepage, design-system documents and real result photographs as strict references. This is an evolution of the existing brand, not a redesign. Preserve the warm ivory, linen, stone and champagne palette; elegant editorial serif headlines; refined sans-serif interface copy; matte-black calls to action; restrained luxury; generous negative space; and natural skin-focused photography.

The page must feel like a credible luxury beauty evidence library rather than Instagram, Pinterest, a testimonial wall or a generic Shopify gallery.

## Page architecture

Create the following sequence:

1. Existing Sunless navigation and utility system
2. Restrained editorial introduction
3. Clear filter and sort toolbar
4. One large featured transformation
5. A regular three-column results grid
6. A quiet shade-match conversion module
7. Evidence disclosure and refined footer transition

## Hero direction

Use the headline:

**“Real skin. Real colour. Real results.”**

Supporting copy:

**“Explore verified Sunless results by starting skin tone, desired depth, product and development time.”**

Add a primary text or matte-black CTA reading **“FIND RESULTS LIKE MINE”** and a quieter secondary action reading **“Match My Shade.”**

Anchor the copy in a narrow left column. On the right, place one approved real before-and-after image pair at large scale. Insert these photographs unchanged. Do not retouch, recolour, regenerate, crop destructively or reinterpret the people.

## Filters

Create a calm, contained toolbar with controls for:

- Starting skin tone
- Desired depth
- Product format
- Product or shade
- Coats
- Development time
- Sort

Show a result count and a restrained active-filter state. Avoid oversized chips, glassmorphism or dashboard styling.

## Featured result

Design one large editorial feature using an approved before-and-after pair, inserted unchanged. Include:

- starting profile;
- achieved depth;
- exact product and shade;
- number of coats;
- development time;
- verification label;
- short quote;
- **“View Full Result”** CTA.

Show the exact approved product image as a small, grounded commercial cue. Do not redraw or alter the packaging.

## Results cards

Create a consistent three-column grid. Every card must contain:

- clearly labelled Before and After images;
- starting tone;
- result depth;
- product and shade;
- coats and development time;
- provenance label;
- `View Result` action.

Use aligned image ratios and calm editorial metadata. Do not use irregular masonry, social-media hearts, floating reaction controls, promotional stickers or decorative gradients.

## Visual rhythm

The page should progress from spacious editorial introduction to useful controls, then into a denser evidence grid. Use strong but restrained chapter separation. Avoid one continuous cream slab by varying scale, width and density while retaining one coherent brand language.

## Integrity constraints

- Use only the supplied approved people and result photographs.
- Insert all human photographs unchanged at the pixel level.
- No generative face alteration.
- No body reshaping.
- No skin smoothing.
- No artificial warming of After images.
- No fabricated customer names, reviews, statistics or results.
- No invented product labels or packaging.
- Keep essential text as editable interface content in final production.

The result must feel like Vogue beauty editorial discipline combined with Apple-level information clarity: photorealistic, elegant, commercially useful, accessible and unmistakably Sunless by Jimmy Coco.

# Prompt B — Desktop Result Detail Page

Create one ultra-premium 1700px-wide desktop result-detail page for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**, using the approved website system, exact product assets and one approved real before-and-after result.

The page must explain precisely how the result was achieved and provide a direct but restrained route to purchase.

## Above-the-fold composition

Use an asymmetrical two-plane layout:

- Left 7 columns: large labelled Before and After comparison using the supplied photographs unchanged.
- Right 5 columns: result title, provenance label, starting profile, achieved depth, product and shade, coats, development time and one dominant purchase CTA.

Suggested title format:

**“Medium neutral skin to soft golden colour.”**

Primary CTA:

**“RECREATE THIS RESULT”**

Secondary action:

**“View Product Details”**

Add a quiet link:

**“Not sure this shade is right for you? Match My Shade.”**

## Evidence summary

Present concise factual metadata:

- Starting tone
- Undertone, when verified
- Target depth
- Product and exact shade
- Number of coats
- Development time
- Lighting context
- Verification category

Do not use invented precision or pseudo-clinical scoring.

## Method chapter

Create a visually distinct editorial section explaining:

1. Prepare
2. Apply
3. Develop
4. Rinse or finish
5. Maintain

Use only recorded method information. Show approved product and application-tool images unchanged.

## Product purchase module

Include:

- exact product image;
- product name;
- selected shade;
- concise suitability statement;
- price placeholder styled as live commerce data;
- stock state;
- quantity;
- matte-black Add to Bag CTA.

The product module must feel clearly purchasable but not like a generic marketplace card.

## Lower-page content

Include:

- short customer context or quote;
- three similar results;
- a restrained routine module with no more than three supporting products;
- a final shade-match invitation;
- disclosure that results vary by starting tone, preparation, application, development time and lighting.

## Integrity constraints

The supplied person, before-and-after and product images must be inserted unchanged. Do not alter faces, bodies, skin tone, product packaging, colour temperature or image content. Do not generate replacement humans. Do not fabricate evidence.

Use warm ivory and mineral-stone backgrounds, refined serif headings, clean sans-serif metadata, matte-black CTAs, generous whitespace and clear commercial hierarchy. The page should feel credible, human, editorial and immediately usable.
