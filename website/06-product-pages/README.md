# Sunless Product Page System

This folder defines the complete product-page experience for Sunless by Jimmy Coco.

The product page must balance two responsibilities:

1. Create emotional desire for the finished result.
2. Make the buying decision unmistakably clear.

It must never become a product brochure that hides price, suitability, shade logic or purchase controls. Equally, it must not collapse into a generic ecommerce template.

## Governing principle

The visitor should understand within seconds:

- what the product is;
- what result it creates;
- whether it suits them;
- how much it costs;
- what to select;
- and how to buy it.

The editorial experience should increase confidence, not delay purchase.
