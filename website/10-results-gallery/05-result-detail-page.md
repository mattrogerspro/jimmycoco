# 05 — Result Detail Page

## Purpose

The result-detail page turns one transformation into a complete, credible and commercially useful story.

It must explain the starting point, product choice, method and outcome before asking the visitor to purchase.

## Page sequence

1. Breadcrumb and gallery return
2. Large before-and-after comparison
3. Result summary
4. Product and shade used
5. Starting profile and target result
6. Application method
7. Development and capture details
8. Customer quote or context
9. Recreate this result
10. Similar results
11. Shade-match CTA
12. Footer

## Above the fold

### Left side

- large, accurately labelled before-and-after comparison;
- optional static thumbnails for alternate angles;
- no decorative overlays covering relevant skin.

### Right side

- result title;
- concise transformation summary;
- verified provenance label;
- starting profile;
- achieved depth;
- product and shade;
- coats and development time;
- primary `Recreate this result` CTA.

## Result title format

Use useful, descriptive titles rather than promotional claims.

Examples:

- Fair neutral skin to soft golden colour
- One-coat medium bronze result
- Deep bronze face result using Malibu Beach

## Method section

Explain the sequence clearly:

1. Preparation used
2. Application tool
3. Product quantity or coat count
4. Development duration
5. Rinse or finishing step
6. Maintenance guidance

Only include information recorded for that result. Do not fill missing evidence with assumed best practice.

## Product module

The product module should contain:

- exact product image;
- product name;
- selected shade or variant;
- short suitability statement;
- current price from live commerce data;
- stock state;
- Add to Bag;
- link to product details.

The result evidence should explain why this product is relevant before the CTA appears.

## Customer context

Where consent and data allow, include concise context such as:

- first-time or experienced self-tan user;
- desired event or everyday use;
- primary concern;
- what they liked about the result.

Do not reveal unnecessary personal data.

## Similar results

Show no more than three to four relevant results based on:

- similar starting tone;
- same product or shade;
- similar desired depth;
- similar coat count.

Avoid generic `You may also like` logic.

## SEO and structured content

Each result detail should have:

- unique descriptive title;
- concise meta description;
- indexable method and product information where appropriate;
- image alt text based on visible content;
- canonical handling for duplicate campaign variants.

## Accessibility

- static comparison must remain available if interactive controls fail;
- all images need meaningful alt text;
- accordions must not hide all essential method details by default;
- Add to Bag feedback must be announced;
- labels cannot rely on left/right position alone.

## Success criteria

The page succeeds when the visitor can understand why the result occurred, judge whether it is relevant and purchase the correct product without returning to the gallery for missing information.
