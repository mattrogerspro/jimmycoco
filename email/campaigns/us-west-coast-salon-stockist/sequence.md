## Approved commercial boundaries

Eligible U.S. professional partners may request a complimentary 100 ml professional trial sample, subject to current individual availability review before shipping. The sequence deliberately contains no fixed U.S. trade price, retail price, margin, volume discount, state-wide availability promise, stock promise or shipping lead-time claim.

Retail is framed as client care: Buff & Glow Mitt for even-looking maintenance, Self Tan Soufflé for a controlled top-up, and A-List Glow Kit for a complete routine or gift option. Current availability, order route and partner terms are confirmed for the individual business before a retail plan is set.
