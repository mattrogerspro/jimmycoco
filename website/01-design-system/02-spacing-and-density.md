# 02 — Spacing and Density System

## Purpose

This document defines how spacing creates luxury, hierarchy and editorial rhythm across the Sunless website.

Spacing is not simply empty area. It controls pace, perceived value, comprehension and emotional emphasis.

## Core principle

Do not apply one repeated vertical spacing token to every chapter.

The homepage should feel composed like a magazine or film sequence, with deliberate changes in pace.

## Base spacing scale

Use a flexible scale built around the following approximate values:

- 4px — optical adjustment only
- 8px — tightly related inline elements
- 12px — labels and compact metadata
- 16px — standard small-component spacing
- 24px — card and field relationships
- 32px — internal grouping
- 48px — major component separation
- 64px — compact chapter spacing
- 80px — standard editorial breathing room
- 96px — major visual separation
- 120px — large desktop pause
- 160px — major chapter opening or closing
- 200px+ — intentionally expansive editorial space

The scale is not a requirement to use every value. It provides proportion and consistency.

## Spacing hierarchy

### Micro spacing

Used within:

- Labels
- Product metadata
- Rating groups
- Form controls
- CTA reassurance

Micro spacing must feel precise and functional.

### Component spacing

Used between:

- Product image and product title
- Heading and body copy
- Form questions and answer controls
- Review quote and customer context

Component spacing must clarify relationships.

### Chapter spacing

Used to separate major narrative scenes.

Chapter spacing should create a visible emotional reset. It should not merely prevent elements touching.

## Recommended chapter ranges

- Hero internal spacing: 96–140px
- Hero-to-authority transition: 32–72px depending on integration
- Recommendation engine: 150–210px top and bottom
- Hollywood method: 180–240px
- Real-results photography: 80–130px around image planes
- Best sellers: 140–190px
- Founder story: 120–180px
- Comparison: 180–240px
- Reviews: 130–200px
- Routine: 150–210px
- Final CTA: 170–240px

These are directional. The rhythm should be judged at full-page scale.

## Density cadence

The approved broad sequence is:

Dense → structured → sparse → visual → moderate → editorial → minimal → human → light → cinematic.

This prevents fatigue and creates contrast.

## Breathing moments

At least two homepage chapters should contain intentionally low information density.

A breathing moment may contain:

- One headline
- One short statement
- One dominant image
- One quiet link or no CTA

The purpose is to restore attention and increase the perceived importance of surrounding content.

## Whitespace around premium objects

Product renders, founder portraits and large quotes require more space than utilitarian interface elements.

Do not crowd premium objects with:

- Badges
- Multiple CTAs
- Dense metadata
- Decorative icons
- Repeated borders

A product should feel curated, not stocked.

## Card padding

Directional desktop guidance:

- Small utility card: 20–24px
- Standard product card: 28–36px
- Recommendation card: 48–72px
- Editorial testimonial surface: 40–64px

Internal padding should increase with importance and card size.

## Text spacing

- Eyebrow to heading: 12–18px
- Heading to supporting copy: 20–30px
- Body paragraph spacing: 16–24px
- Supporting copy to CTA: 28–40px
- CTA to reassurance: 10–16px

Do not compress body copy against CTAs. The action should feel like the natural conclusion of the message.

## Density by intent

### Decision-heavy scenes

Use stronger grouping and tighter internal relationships, but generous outer whitespace.

### Emotional scenes

Use lower text density and larger image scale.

### Proof scenes

Use enough metadata to establish credibility without turning the scene into a dashboard.

### Product scenes

Use standardised, concise information so comparison feels easy.

## Mobile spacing

On mobile, reduce absolute spacing but preserve relative hierarchy.

Recommended ranges:

- Standard component gap: 16–24px
- Major group gap: 32–48px
- Chapter top/bottom: 72–120px
- CTA separation: 24–32px

Do not compress mobile so aggressively that the premium rhythm disappears.

## Common spacing failures

Reject designs that:

- Use identical padding around every chapter
- Create long blank areas without purpose
- Place cards too close together
- Use tiny gaps that make unrelated elements appear grouped
- Apply oversized whitespace around low-value utility content
- Make dense information feel decorative rather than readable
- Allow the final CTA to feel cramped

## Full-page rhythm test

View the page at 10% zoom.

The spacing should produce recognisable pauses and changes of pace. If chapter boundaries are visible only because of thin dividers, the spacing system has failed.

## Success criteria

The spacing system succeeds when:

- Important scenes feel more valuable
- Related content is clearly grouped
- The page never feels mechanically repetitive
- Dense chapters are followed by relief
- The user can scan without fatigue
- Whitespace supports conversion rather than delaying it
