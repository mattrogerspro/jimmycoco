## Cadence & sending notes (summary)

- **Timeline:** Day 0 → 3 → 8 → 13 → 20. Stop the sequence the moment someone replies.
- **Send days/times:** Tue–Thu, 8–10am salon-local time. Avoid Mondays and weekends.
- **Format:** Send Emails 1, 2 and 5 as **plain text** (best inbox placement). Emails 3 and 4 can go plain text *or* the branded HTML versions once there's engagement.
- **One ask per email**, and for cold, make the ask a **reply** ("send it", "yes") — reply-based CTAs lift both conversions and deliverability.
- **Compliance (AU Spam Act 2003):** include accurate sender identification and a working unsubscribe in every send, and only email business roles the message is genuinely relevant to. (Practical guidance, not legal advice — confirm with your own adviser.)
- **Primary metric:** reply rate and sample requests, not opens.
