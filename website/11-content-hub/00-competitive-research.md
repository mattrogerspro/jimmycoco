# Competitive Content Research — Global Tanning Benchmark

**Research date:** 17 July 2026  
**Purpose:** internal strategy only; competitor names must not be copied into customer-facing Jimmy Coco articles.

## Method

There is no single audited, public global sales table covering consumer self-tan and professional spray-tan brands. This is therefore a strategic benchmark set, not a claim of exact worldwide revenue rank.

The ten brands were selected using four signals:

1. repeated inclusion in current beauty-editor testing and category coverage;
2. multi-market distribution or strong professional adoption;
3. breadth and clarity of the current tanning range;
4. maturity of education, shade guidance, routines and editorial content.

The research distinguishes consumer self-tan from professional spray-tan solutions while covering both, because Jimmy Coco operates across both markets.

## Category patterns confirmed by the research

### 1. Education is part of the product

The best-performing content does not merely announce products. It reduces the visible-risk questions that stop a purchase:

- Which shade should I choose?
- Will it look orange?
- How do I prepare?
- How do I tan my hands, feet and face?
- How long should it develop?
- When should I apply it before an event?
- How do I keep the fade even?

Jimmy Coco should treat the answer as part of the formula’s value, not as generic blog filler.

### 2. Face tanning is becoming a separate routine

Current category coverage increasingly treats facial mist, drops and serum formats as skincare-adjacent daily or weekly routines rather than miniature versions of body tan. The A-List Face Tanning Mist gives Jimmy Coco an immediate route into this demand.

### 3. “Skinification” is changing self-tan language

Hydration, barrier awareness, skincare actives, lighter formats and natural-looking radiance are replacing the older obsession with maximum darkness. Jimmy Coco’s A-List Face Mist and Malibu Soufflé already contain product stories suited to this shift, but claims must remain tied to approved product data.

### 4. Natural, controllable colour is more current than maximum depth

The category is moving toward believable daylight colour, lighter layers and greater control. This aligns directly with Jimmy’s stated professional preference for a natural glow and supports content about shade, layers, face/body balance and event timing.

### 5. Professional brands build authority through training

Professional content performs a different commercial job. It must help a salon choose a solution, consult a client, control usage, protect results and understand service economics. Jimmy Coco’s one-litre Laguna, Malibu and Sunset solutions create a clean three-part education system.

## Jimmy Coco content opportunity

Competitors largely occupy one of three positions:

- mass, accessible tanning advice;
- premium skincare-infused self-tan;
- technical professional solution and training.

Jimmy Coco can credibly unite all three through one editorial promise:

> Professional colour judgement, translated into a clear at-home or in-salon method.

The content should therefore make Jimmy’s expertise operational. Each article must tell the reader what to choose, what to do, what to expect and what to avoid.

## Research limitations

- Market-share and revenue data are not consistently public, so the rank must never be presented externally as an audited sales league table.
- Search visibility was assessed qualitatively from current search results and content architecture, not from paid keyword-volume software.
- Product pages change. All Jimmy Coco product facts used in the article set were checked on 17 July 2026 and require a final pre-publication check.
