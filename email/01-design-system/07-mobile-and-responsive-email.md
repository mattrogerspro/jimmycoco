# Mobile and Responsive Email

## Objective

Design for the inbox most customers actually use. Mobile is the primary composition, not a reduced desktop afterthought.

## Mobile principles

- Single-column reading by default
- Minimum 16px body text
- Touch targets at least 44px high
- 20–24px side padding
- Clear proposition before scrolling deeply
- One dominant CTA in the opening section
- No essential side-by-side comparison that becomes confusing when stacked

## Stacking rules

Two-column modules should stack in the order that preserves meaning:

1. proposition or product image
2. product name and suitability
3. proof or detail
4. CTA

Do not automatically preserve desktop source order if it creates image → image → text → text on mobile.

## Image behaviour

- Use responsive width: 100%
- Design safe crop zones for faces and packaging
- Avoid text embedded near image edges
- Provide dedicated mobile crops when a desktop panoramic image loses meaning
- Keep product scale consistent across related modules

## Buttons

- Full-width or nearly full-width primary buttons are preferred on narrow screens
- Stack adjacent actions vertically
- Keep at least 12–16px between touch targets
- Avoid tiny inline CTAs for primary actions

## Content length

Mobile emails should be aggressively edited. Long educational content should use concise steps and link to the website for depth.

## Navigation and footer

Keep header navigation minimal. Footer links may wrap into grouped rows or a vertical list. Unsubscribe and preferences must remain easy to find and tap.

## Testing widths

Review at minimum:

- 320px
- 375px
- 390px
- 414px
- 480px
- 600–640px desktop container

## Failure modes

Reject designs with:

- text smaller than 14px for important content
- side-by-side products squeezed into unreadable cards
- buttons requiring precision tapping
- cropped faces or labels
- excessive email length caused by mechanical stacking
- horizontal overflow
- order summaries or tables that break at narrow widths

## Success criteria

The mobile email succeeds when the proposition, product, proof and CTA are understandable with one-handed scanning and no zooming.
