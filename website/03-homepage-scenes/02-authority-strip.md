# Scene 02 — Integrated Authority Strip

## Role in the experience

The authority strip confirms credibility immediately after the hero without interrupting momentum.

It should feel like an elegant editorial colophon, not a row of floating ecommerce cards.

## Commercial objective

Reduce early scepticism by proving:

- Customers approve of the products
- Reputable media has recognised the brand
- Jimmy Coco has genuine professional authority

## Core proof set

Use three distinct proof types:

1. **Customer validation** — rating and verified review volume
2. **Editorial recognition** — selected fashion and beauty press
3. **Professional authority** — Jimmy’s role and relevant experience

The proof types must complement rather than repeat one another.

## Recommended copy

### Customer proof

> 4.8/5 from 1,642 verified customers

Use only current, verified figures.

### Editorial proof

> Featured in Vogue, Cosmopolitan and Harper’s Bazaar

Only show publications supported by genuine coverage or approved brand evidence.

### Founder authority

> Created by celebrity tanning artist Jimmy Coco

Optional supporting line:

> Professional colour judgement, translated for home.

## Composition

Preferred treatment:

- Full-width transitional band integrated into the lower edge of the hero
- Three proof groups separated by whitespace or fine vertical rules
- No independent raised cards
- Quiet background shift from hero ivory toward the next chapter
- Consistent baseline across all three proof groups

The strip should be visually lighter than the hero and recommendation engine.

## Architectural silhouette

A shallow, horizontal bridge between two larger scenes.

It should create a pause and strengthen trust without becoming a standalone destination.

## Hierarchy

1. Rating or primary proof figure
2. Short proof statement
3. Supporting logos or credentials

Media logos must not overpower customer proof or founder authority.

## Visual language

- Warm ivory or soft white field
- Deep charcoal type
- Muted bronze stars where accessible
- Fine hairline dividers
- Small but readable sans-serif
- Controlled logo sizing
- Generous horizontal breathing room

## Trust principles

### Specificity

A precise verified rating is more credible than “Loved by thousands.”

### Diversity of proof

Customer, editorial and expert proof reduce different forms of anxiety.

### Proximity

Proof appears directly after the hero promise and before the larger commitment of the recommendation engine.

## Accessibility

- Keep supporting text at a readable size
- Do not use logo imagery without accessible text equivalents in production
- Ensure star ratings are accompanied by numeric text
- Avoid low-contrast gold text
- Stack proof groups clearly on mobile

## Mobile behaviour

On mobile, display one proof group per row or use a simple swipe pattern with visible pagination. Do not compress all three into tiny columns.

## Common AI failure modes

Reject concepts that:

- Create three large floating cards over the hero
- Invent review counts or publication logos
- Use illegible miniature text
- Make the strip taller than necessary
- Add excessive badges, seals or award icons
- Present generic trust claims without specificity
- Use bright gold or glossy effects

## Success criteria

The authority strip succeeds when:

- Credibility increases without slowing the scroll
- Three different proof types are understood instantly
- The transition into the recommendation engine feels natural
- The treatment remains editorial and restrained
- The strip does not resemble a generic trust-badge widget

## Final AI image prompt

Create one shallow, premium authority transition for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**, positioned immediately beneath the existing hero and above the shade recommendation engine. Preserve the current warm ivory palette, editorial restraint and refined sans-serif typography.

Do not create three detached floating cards. Instead, build one integrated full-width editorial band with three proof groups separated by generous whitespace and subtle fine rules: **4.8/5 from 1,642 verified customers**, selected approved fashion and beauty press recognition, and **Created by celebrity tanning artist Jimmy Coco**. Use restrained bronze star accents, deep charcoal text and carefully controlled logo scale.

The band should feel like a luxury magazine colophon and a visual bridge between chapters. Keep it shallow, calm, highly legible and production-ready. No glossy badges, award seals, glassmorphism, invented statistics, crowded logos or generic Shopify trust widgets.
