# AI Copy Production Workflow

## Purpose

Define how AI-assisted email copy moves from brief to approved production content.

## Workflow

1. Confirm lifecycle owner and eligibility
2. Validate source data
3. Select approved prompt version
4. Generate structured draft
5. Review facts and dynamic states
6. Review brand voice and hierarchy
7. Render inside the actual template
8. Test plain text and fallbacks
9. Complete legal, service and claim review
10. Approve, version and archive

## Generation boundary

AI may draft, rewrite, compress, expand, classify and audit copy. It may not determine whether a customer is eligible, invent a claim, change an offer, approve legal wording or send the message.

## Structured draft output

Require:

- subject options;
- matching preview text;
- recommended pair;
- headline;
- body in module order;
- primary and secondary CTA labels;
- plain-text version;
- dynamic-field table;
- unresolved source requirements;
- claim dependencies.

## Review order

Review in this sequence:

1. Customer state and necessity
2. Factual accuracy
3. Offer and policy accuracy
4. Main proposition
5. CTA and destination
6. Dynamic-field fallbacks
7. Tone and readability
8. Subject and preview relationship
9. Accessibility and plain text
10. Grammar and final proof

## Dynamic-state testing

Render at minimum:

- complete customer data;
- missing name;
- no shade result;
- unavailable product;
- changed price;
- unknown market;
- expired offer;
- suppressed customer;
- higher-priority service state.

## Approval rule

The final approved copy must be stored as a fixed production version. Production must not call a generative model at send time to create unreviewed copy.
