# Composition and Rhythm System

## Purpose

This document prevents the homepage from collapsing into a repetitive stack of cream containers and card grids.

## Governing rule

Every homepage chapter must have a unique architectural silhouette.

No two consecutive chapters may share the same combination of:

- Width
- Height
- Density
- Alignment
- Image scale
- Background tone
- Card structure
- Text-to-image ratio

If two adjacent chapters could be swapped without materially affecting the page rhythm, at least one composition is too generic.

## Chapter archetypes

Use a deliberate sequence of contrasting archetypes:

1. **Cinematic split scene** — dense hero with layered people, product and copy.
2. **Contained decision workspace** — structured recommendation experience on a dedicated tonal field.
3. **Airy explanatory spread** — sparse, bright and typography-led.
4. **Photography-dominant proof scene** — near full-bleed imagery with minimal interface.
5. **Curated product gallery** — calm return to the grid with limited products.
6. **Asymmetrical founder editorial** — oversized portrait, quote and offset text.
7. **Minimal comparison spread** — quiet, precise and almost diagrammatic.
8. **Human testimonial environment** — portrait-led, warmer and more intimate.
9. **Linear ritual scene** — three-step routine with directional flow.
10. **Dark cinematic ending** — strong visual closure and conversion.

## 10% zoom test

At 10% zoom, the complete homepage must read as a sequence of recognisably different blocks. The eye should detect chapter changes before it can read any copy.

Failure indicators:

- More than three consecutive pale backgrounds
- Repeated four-card rows
- Identical container widths throughout
- Uniform vertical padding
- Similar heading placement in every chapter
- Every scene centred
- Every image confined to a card

## Background cadence

Approved sequence:

- Warm ivory
- Soft mineral stone
- Bright white
- Photography-dominant tonal field
- Clean white
- Natural linen
- Bright white
- Warm taupe
- White
- Deep charcoal

Background changes must be clear at distance but restrained at full size. Avoid arbitrary stripes, harsh dividers or decorative gradients.

## Vertical rhythm

Use a composed rhythm rather than one repeated section token.

Suggested chapter padding ranges:

- Hero: 96–140px internal breathing room
- Decision chapter: 150–210px
- Airy editorial chapter: 180–240px
- Photography-led chapter: 70–120px around dominant imagery
- Product gallery: 140–180px
- Founder editorial: 100–170px
- Comparison: 180–230px
- Reviews: 130–190px
- Routine: 150–200px
- Final CTA: 160–230px

These are directional, not mechanical. Optical balance overrides numeric uniformity.

## Grid behaviour

Use an invisible 12-column desktop grid, but do not force every element into the same central container.

Allowed behaviours:

- Full-bleed image planes
- Images extending beyond the content grid
- Oversized product renders overlapping negative space
- Narrow editorial text columns
- Offset captions and quotes
- Alternating left and right anchors
- Intentional blank columns

## Density cadence

Recommended rhythm:

Dense → medium → sparse → visual → medium → editorial → minimal → human → sparse → cinematic.

The viewer should periodically receive relief from reading and choice.

## Card discipline

Cards are tools, not the default layout.

Use cards only when they clarify selection, comparison or grouping. Avoid cards for:

- Founder biography
- Editorial quotes
- Large photographic proof
- Chapter introductions
- Emotional storytelling

Card rows should never dominate more than two consecutive scenes.

## Success criteria

A successful page:

- Feels composed rather than stacked
- Contains obvious visual pauses
- Alternates information and emotion
- Uses photography as architecture, not decoration
- Builds toward a memorable dark ending
- Remains unmistakably one brand despite compositional variety
