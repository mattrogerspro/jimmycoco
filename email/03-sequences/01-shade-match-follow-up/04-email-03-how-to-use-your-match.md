# 04 — Email 03: How to Use Your Match

## Send timing

Approximately three days after completion, unless suppressed by purchase, checkout or recommendation change.

## Job to be done

Reduce fear of streaking, over-application, poor preparation and an unpredictable result.

## Core message

> Your recommended product is designed to be controllable. Here is the simplest way to use it well.

## Primary CTA

**SEE THE APPLICATION GUIDE**

## Content structure

1. Reintroduce the recommended product and variant.
2. Present a concise three-stage method:
   - Prepare
   - Apply
   - Develop and finish
3. Include product-specific quantities, number of coats and timing only where verified.
4. Add one expert tip attributed to Jimmy only when the wording is approved.
5. Link to the complete application guide.

## Suggested subject lines

- How to use your Sunless match
- Three steps to your matched result
- Your personalised application guide

## Preview text

Prepare, apply and develop your recommended product with confidence.

## Content rules

Instructions must be sourced from the current approved product guidance. Do not generalise one product’s method to another format.

Examples of product-sensitive differences:

- mousse versus soufflé;
- face product versus body product;
- instant versus developing colour;
- professional spray solution versus consumer application;
- brush, mitt or hand application.

## Design direction

Use one linear three-step module with generous spacing. Each stage may include one small approved image or icon, but the email must remain readable when images are blocked.

Keep the recommended product visible near the opening, then let live text carry the method.

## Dynamic data

- recommended product and variant;
- preparation instructions;
- application tool;
- coat guidance;
- development time;
- aftercare guidance;
- application guide URL.

If product-specific instructions cannot be verified, do not send this message until the content is resolved.

## Safety and claims

- Include patch-test or sensitivity guidance where required by approved product instructions.
- Do not provide medical advice.
- Do not imply guaranteed transfer-free, streak-free or identical results unless the claim is substantiated and approved.

## Plain-text version

Provide the complete three-stage method in text, followed by the guide URL and support link.
