# Product Page — Suitability and Result Explanation

## Purpose

This section translates formula characteristics into a clear customer outcome. It should answer: who is this for, what will it look like, and when should I choose something else?

## Core content

Present the following in a concise, visually legible format:

- Desired depth
- Undertone behaviour
- Skin-tone suitability
- Finish
- Development time
- Buildability
- Experience level
- Best use cases
- Important limitations or exclusions

## Recommended structure

### Result statement

A direct one-sentence summary:

> Creates a deep, neutral bronze with controlled warmth and buildable intensity.

### Best for

Use specific scenarios:

- Customers seeking a deeper event-ready result
- Medium to deep skin tones wanting added richness
- Experienced users comfortable with controlled layering

### Consider another product when

Provide honest routing:

- You want only a subtle everyday tint
- You prefer a shorter development window
- You are new to self-tan and want maximum forgiveness

Honest disqualification increases trust and reduces unsuitable purchases.

## Visual treatment

Use one airy editorial chapter with a strong product or skin-result image and a narrow information column. Avoid turning every attribute into a separate card.

A concise result spectrum may show:

Subtle → Medium → Deep

The active product position must be labelled in text, not colour alone.

## Undertone explanation

Explain undertone in practical language. Connect the formula base to the result without making unsupported scientific claims.

Example:

> The violet-toned base is designed to reduce excessive orange warmth and support a more neutral-looking bronze result.

Use “designed to” unless stronger claims are verified.

## Realistic expectation setting

State that final depth varies with:

- Natural skin tone
- Preparation
- Number of coats
- Development time
- Application consistency
- Aftercare

Do not promise an identical result for every customer.

## Product comparison route

When useful, link to a concise comparison against adjacent products. The current product must remain visually primary.

## Copy rules

Prefer specific, observable outcomes. Avoid:

- flawless
- perfect for everyone
- universally flattering
- zero transfer
- streak-proof
- guaranteed

unless legally and operationally substantiated.

## Accessibility

- Use text labels for all scales and spectrums.
- Keep explanatory copy readable and concise.
- Do not embed essential guidance only inside images.
- Ensure comparison links have descriptive labels.

## Failure modes

Reject sections that:

- use poetic copy without suitability information;
- imply all skin tones receive one identical outcome;
- hide limitations;
- use a dense wall of icons;
- overstate formula claims;
- rely on unlabeled skin-colour swatches;
- confuse undertone with depth.

## Success criteria

A visitor should be able to decide confidently whether this product matches their desired result and know when another product would be more suitable.
