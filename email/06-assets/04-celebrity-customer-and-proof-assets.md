# Celebrity, Customer and Proof Assets

## Purpose

Define strict handling rules for celebrity photography, customer-result imagery, testimonials and other proof assets used in email.

## Core rule

These assets are documentary evidence, not generative material.

They must remain faithful to the approved source and may not be regenerated, retouched into a different result, or used to imply an association that has not been approved.

## Celebrity assets

Celebrity imagery requires:

- verified source;
- explicit usage approval;
- approved channel and market;
- approved start and end dates;
- permitted crop and placement;
- approved accompanying copy;
- endorsement or association limitations;
- credit requirements;
- named legal or rights owner.

Possession of a photograph does not establish permission to use a name, likeness or endorsement claim.

## Pixel-faithful handling

Celebrity photographs must be inserted from the original approved file.

Do not:

- regenerate the person;
- alter the face, body, hair, skin tone or identifying features;
- retouch skin or body shape;
- change wardrobe;
- replace or extend the background using generative processing when it risks changing the person;
- apply filters that materially change appearance;
- distort aspect ratio;
- use face enhancement or AI upscaling that recreates features;
- create a synthetic group image from separate people.

Allowed operations, when approved:

- proportional scaling;
- non-destructive crop;
- placement within a layout;
- masking that does not alter the person;
- loss-controlled compression;
- colour-profile conversion that preserves appearance.

## Celebrity strips and galleries

For horizontal strips:

- use every approved photograph as an independent immutable image;
- preserve each original aspect ratio unless an approved crop exists;
- never stretch images to equal widths;
- use contain, letterboxing or carefully approved crops rather than distortion;
- keep consistent visual spacing through layout, not image alteration;
- provide an intentional mobile presentation;
- do not add a name, quote or product association unless separately approved.

## Customer-result imagery

Before-and-after or result imagery must record:

- consent reference;
- capture date;
- product and routine used;
- development time where relevant;
- lighting and camera conditions where available;
- whether images are customer-supplied or professionally captured;
- permitted cropping and retouching;
- market and channel rights;
- expiry or withdrawal process.

## Result integrity

Do not:

- change skin tone;
- smooth, reshape or beautify the result;
- change lighting independently between before and after;
- remove application errors;
- composite a result from different people;
- create synthetic before-and-after evidence;
- use generated skin as customer proof;
- imply typicality without approved evidence.

## Testimonials and reviews

Store:

- exact approved wording;
- source record;
- review date;
- product or service context;
- permission state;
- editing record;
- attribution rules;
- market limitations.

Edits must not change meaning. Ellipses or shortening require review.

## Professional and founder imagery

Classify professional, founder and behind-the-scenes photography separately from celebrity and customer proof. Usage still requires rights and source approval, but transformation limits may differ.

## Proof pairing

Every proof asset must be paired only with copy it genuinely supports.

Examples:

- a celebrity portrait does not prove product use unless that use is approved and evidenced;
- a customer result does not support a universal performance claim;
- a professional image does not establish professional endorsement of every product;
- a review for one variant must not be attached to another without approval.

## Withdrawal and expiry

When permission expires or is withdrawn:

1. change status to `EXPIRED` or `BLOCKED`;
2. remove the asset from new sends;
3. identify scheduled campaigns and templates;
4. replace hosted assets where contractual obligations require it;
5. preserve audit history;
6. document the effective date and owner.

## Release blockers

Do not use when:

- rights or permitted context are unclear;
- the original cannot be located;
- the image has been generatively altered;
- accompanying copy implies unsupported use or endorsement;
- customer consent is missing or withdrawn;
- result conditions are misleading;
- crop distorts identity or evidence;
- asset or permission has expired.
