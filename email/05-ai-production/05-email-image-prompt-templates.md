# Email Image Prompt Templates

## Purpose

Provide controlled prompt structures for creating Sunless email imagery without altering protected source assets or inventing product truth.

```text
Create one premium editorial email hero environment for Sunless by Jimmy Coco.

CHANNEL
- Email module:
- Desktop dimensions:
- Mobile dimensions:
- Required safe area:

PROTECTED ASSETS
- Exact source files:
- These files must be inserted unchanged.
- Do not redraw, regenerate, retouch, recolour, reshape, extend or reinterpret them.
- Do not alter faces, bodies, skin tone, product packaging, labels, logos or product colour.

GENERATIVE AREA
- Environment:
- Surface:
- Lighting:
- Architecture:
- Mood:
- Negative space:
- Camera position:

COMPOSITION
- Subject position:
- Product scale:
- Copy-safe region:
- Desktop crop:
- Mobile crop:

EXCLUSIONS
- no invented products;
- no fake labels;
- no synthetic celebrity likeness;
- no altered customer results;
- no beauty retouching;
- no excessive glow, HDR or generic AI luxury styling.

OUTPUT
Generate the environment and composition plan. Protected assets remain separate for deterministic compositing.
```

## Product still-life prompt

```text
Create a restrained editorial still-life environment for the supplied Sunless product cut-outs.

The product files are immutable and will be composited after generation.
Generate only the background, surface, lighting and natural shadow environment.

Use warm ivory, linen, stone and subtle champagne tones.
Lighting must feel natural, soft and cinematic.
Leave clear negative space for email copy.
Do not generate bottles, packs, labels, logos, brushes or product shadows that conflict with the supplied products.
```

## Celebrity strip environment prompt

```text
Create a full-width black editorial film-strip environment for a horizontal row of supplied celebrity photographs.

The celebrity photographs are immutable.
They must be inserted pixel-for-pixel from the original files with no generative processing, retouching, colour change, crop distortion, face alteration or body alteration.

Generate only:
- black background;
- restrained spacing system;
- subtle frame dividers;
- optional film-strip edge language;
- typography-safe heading area.

Do not generate, redraw or extend any person.
Do not imply a quotation or endorsement not supplied in the approved brief.
```

## Educational diagram prompt

```text
Create a clean editorial instructional illustration for this approved application step: [STEP].

Use simplified non-identifiable hands or abstract motion only where appropriate.
Do not reproduce a real customer or celebrity.
Do not invent product packaging.
Use the supplied exact product cut-out separately.
Prioritise clarity at mobile email width and ensure the instruction remains understandable in accompanying text.
```

## Decorative texture prompt

```text
Create a non-documentary abstract background inspired by warm skin-adjacent neutrals, soft linen, diffused sunlight and subtle cosmetic texture.

It must not resemble an exact tan result, skin swatch, ingredient proof or before-and-after claim.
No people, products, logos, labels or readable text.
Designed for email use with low visual noise and generous copy-safe space.
```

## Prompt output requirements

Every image prompt must return or record:

- intended module;
- desktop and mobile crop plans;
- protected source list;
- generated element list;
- prohibited transformation list;
- output dimensions;
- alt-text purpose;
- QA checks required before approval.
