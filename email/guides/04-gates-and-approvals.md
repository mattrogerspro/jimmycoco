# From draft to send — gates and approvals

A campaign draft cannot send anything. This guide explains what stands between a finished draft and a live send, and who holds the keys.

## The nine QA gates

Every campaign is checked against the canonical gates before it can even be called ready for review:

1. **Repository compliance** — required playbooks actually read; comparable reviewed; campaign registered.
2. **Strategy** — one audience, one outcome; entry, exclusions, cadence, stop rules, handoff; lifecycle-collision check.
3. **Truth and claims** — every commercial statement sourced or tokenised.
4. **Copy** — subject and preview match the body; approved voice; fallbacks; plain text; no false urgency.
5. **Template and rendering** — HTML generated by the master template; clean build; mobile, Outlook and blocked-image review.
6. **Accessibility** — headings, contrast, text-not-image, alt text, reading order.
7. **Assets** — approved, traceable, correct rights, markets and expiry; protected people unmodified.
8. **Delivery and compliance** — correct ESP tokens, approved sender, monitored reply, working unsubscribe, suppression, legal review stated.
9. **Consistency** — README, sequence, data, studio metadata, HTML and registry all agree.

Any failure means `CHANGES REQUIRED` or `BLOCKED` — never "production-ready".

## The four statuses

`DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR SEND` · `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW` · `CHANGES REQUIRED` · `BLOCKED`

`READY TO SEND` does not exist until a **named human** approves the current copy, assets, commercial facts, recipient basis and rendered output.

## The double lock

Even a fully approved campaign cannot send unless **both** locks open:

1. Its entry in `shared/campaign-registry.js` is set to `enabled: true` — new campaigns are always registered disabled.
2. The environment has `EMAIL_LIVE_MODE` switched on.

## Release actions that always need fresh approval

Enabling an automation or broadcast · importing contacts · enabling a campaign or `EMAIL_LIVE_MODE` · changing Supabase production data · deploying Vercel production changes · sending any live or test email. A routine git push never writes to Resend; `npm run templates:check` is a safe, read-only runtime-contract validation.
