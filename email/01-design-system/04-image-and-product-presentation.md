# Email Image and Product Presentation

## Purpose

Define how people, results and products are shown in email without sacrificing accuracy, loading performance or trust.

## Source-asset rule

Approved celebrity, customer, Jimmy Coco and product images must be inserted from the original source files. Do not use generative tools to alter faces, bodies, skin results, packaging, labels, proportions or colour.

Cropping for an email slot is permitted only when:

- the original pixels remain unedited
- the crop does not misrepresent the subject or result
- no face or important product detail is cut awkwardly
- the crop is documented in the asset system

## Image roles

### Hero image
One decisive image supporting the main proposition. It should establish result, authority or product desire immediately.

### Product image
Use accurate packaging, clear silhouette, consistent scale and enough negative space for robust responsive cropping.

### Result image
Include factual context nearby: product, shade/depth, coats, development time and disclosure where required.

### Founder/professional image
Show Jimmy working, advising or demonstrating expertise rather than posing as a generic celebrity endorsement.

### Editorial image
Use sparingly to establish mood, ritual or aspiration. It must still support the email's message.

## Product modules

A product presentation should normally contain:

- accurate image
- product name
- concise suitability or result statement
- price where relevant
- one CTA

Avoid grids of near-identical bottles without decision guidance.

## Image ratios

Recommended source crops:

- Hero: 3:2 or 4:3 desktop, with safe mobile centre crop
- Product: 1:1 or 4:5
- Portrait/story: 4:5
- Horizontal editorial strip: 16:9 or 3:1 only where the detail survives mobile

## Performance

- Export responsive, compressed assets
- Avoid animated GIFs as a default
- Use concise alt text
- Specify dimensions to reduce layout shift
- Keep decorative images optional when images are blocked

## Text over imagery

Avoid critical live text over background images because support is inconsistent. Prefer a separate text cell or bake only non-essential decorative lettering into the image.

## Success criteria

Imagery succeeds when it is authentic, accurately represents the product or result, loads quickly and supports the message even when displayed at narrow mobile width.
