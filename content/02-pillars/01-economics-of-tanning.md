## Why this is first

It's the widest gap and the clearest authority play. Every operational costing
query in this category currently resolves to one of three things: a forum thread
from 2011, a US brand's calculator in dollars, or a 2017 blog post that ends by
asking you to phone. The one UK page carrying actual £ figures ranks #1 for
several queries — which tells us precisely what wins here.

It's also the pillar where the audience is most receptive. 20% of UK salons are
operating at a loss; average margin is 8.2%; 72.6% expected to raise prices
within three months of February 2026. Costs went up in April — employer NIC
threshold cut to £5,000, National Living Wage to £12.71, business rates
overhauled with salons excluded from the relief pubs got. Everyone in this
audience is doing sums right now.

## Scope

**In:** cost per tan · pricing · margin · yield per litre · retail contribution ·
mobile-versus-salon economics · utilisation and chair time · the tanning room's
worth against alternative uses of the space · VAT threshold effects on a tanning
business.

**Out:** whole-salon P&L and general small-business finance (pillar 5) ·
anything that requires us to give financial advice rather than a model.

### 4. What the retail shelf is really worth — `retail-attach-maths`

Retail is 4% of UK salon revenue against a 15–25% benchmark, and 70% of clients
who don't buy say they'd like to. That gap is the single largest unclaimed
margin in the reader's business and it needs no extra chair time.

Bridges into pillar 4.

**Pattern:** A · **CTA:** the range · **Uses:** £18 soufflé, £15 mitt

### 5. Is mobile spray tanning actually profitable? — `mobile-spray-tan-profit`

Nobody has written this. ~40% of self-employed beauty professionals work mobile
or home-based, and every existing costing model ignores travel time, fuel,
equipment amortisation and the fact that a no-show costs a mobile therapist the
journey as well as the slot.

**Pattern:** A · **Reader:** mobile therapist · **CTA:** profit calculator

## Standing rules for this pillar

- **Every article contains at least one complete worked example with visible
  arithmetic.** A costing article without a cost in it is the failure mode of
  every competitor page in this category.
- **Every model carries its Assumptions block** with ranges, so a reader can
  substitute her own figures.
- Retail figures are £59 / £18 / £15 — see
  [decision 5](../00-strategy/decisions.md#5-retail-pricing-the-lower-prices-are-correct).
- Solution cost is the £60 litre, named as ours.
- Every model article ends with the financial-model standing note.
- Cost inputs that changed in April 2026 (NLW, employer NIC, business rates) are
  dated inline, because this pillar will need a refresh each April.

## Commercial route

Calculator → trade account → the litre. Retail attach content routes to the
range. Nothing in this pillar sells before it has delivered its number.
