# How to Help Your Jimmy Coco Self-Tan Fade Evenly

An even fade begins before the tan develops: prepare the skin consistently, apply one controlled layer, rinse gently and avoid creating extra friction on selected areas. Maintain the face separately from the body and remove an old result evenly before beginning a completely fresh application.

Jimmy Coco’s Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé is currently described as buildable and designed for a long-lasting, even fade. Technique still matters. No formula can make constant rubbing, hot soaking or heavy patch repairs invisible.

## Start with an even application

Fade exposes the original application. If one elbow, ankle or wrist received more product, it may remain darker as the surrounding colour softens.

Use the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) across broad areas and finish hands, feet and joints with less product. Blend edges with [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush). Allow the full development period and complete the first rinse gently.

The aim is a thin, consistent layer—not the largest amount the skin can hold.

## Treat the first rinse as part of the result

At the end of the 6–8 hour Malibu development window, rinse according to the product directions. Avoid scrubbing off the guide colour. The rinse is intended to reveal the developed tan, not exfoliate it immediately.

Use comfortable water, keep the process gentle and pat the skin dry. Rough towelling creates unnecessary friction, particularly around shoulders, hips, knees and ankles.

## Reduce uneven wear

Daily life fades some areas faster than others. Hands are washed frequently; clothing rubs the waist and underarms; shoes create friction around feet and ankles; facial cleansing affects the face separately.

You do not need to avoid normal hygiene. Instead:

- keep showers efficient rather than soaking for long periods;
- pat instead of rubbing the skin dry;
- avoid aggressive exfoliation while the result is still even;
- keep the skin comfortably moisturised with products known to work in your routine;
- notice where clothing or footwear repeatedly creates friction.

The face will usually need its own maintenance plan because it is cleansed more frequently. Refresh with the [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) only after assessing the developed facial colour against the neck and body.

## Do not chase every pale area

Patch-applying more body product to every fading section can create layers of different ages. The temporary repair may look acceptable in one light and more obvious when the older colour continues to fade.

If a small area needs attention, use a familiar, controlled method and blend far beyond the visible edge. If the whole result has become uneven, allow it to fade and remove it consistently before beginning a new full application.

## Know when to refresh and when to reset

### Refresh

Refresh when the overall colour remains even but has become lighter. A controlled new application can restore depth without targeting random patches.

### Reset

Reset when there are multiple dark and pale zones, clear friction marks or several layers on top of each other. Prepare the full area evenly and start again rather than adding another coat to an unstable base.

The Buff & Glow Mitt is described as a three-in-one tanning tool in the current range. Use its exfoliating function only according to approved care and application instructions.

## Face and body will not fade at the same speed

That difference is normal. Keep the two routines connected by colour, not by calendar. The body may remain even while the face needs a light refresh; the face may look balanced while hands have faded through washing.

Before reapplying, check three transitions:

- face to neck;
- wrist to hand;
- ankle to foot.

Correct the visual relationship rather than making every area equally dark.

## Common aftercare mistakes

- Scrubbing during the first rinse
- Rubbing hard with a towel
- Using a new, untested correction method before an event
- Recoating only the darkest or palest visible spot
- Applying a full fresh tan over several uneven old layers
- Refreshing the face without checking the neck
- Forgetting sun protection because the skin looks tanned

## Frequently asked questions

### How long will my Jimmy Coco tan last?

The live product copy describes the result as long-lasting, but exact wear varies with skin preparation, application, cleansing, friction and aftercare. Publish a fixed duration only when approved product evidence supports it.

### Can I exfoliate while wearing self-tan?

Exfoliation will accelerate removal. Use it when you are deliberately preparing to reset the result, not as an automatic daily step during maintenance.

### Should I reapply the face and body together?

Not necessarily. Assess each area and the transitions between them. The A-List Face Mist allows facial colour to be maintained separately.
