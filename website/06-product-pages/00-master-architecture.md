# Product Page — Master Architecture

## Purpose

The product page converts interest into confident purchase. It should feel like a premium consultation organised around one product, not a long brochure and not a generic Shopify template.

## Primary commercial objective

Enable the visitor to choose the correct product or variant and add it to cart with minimal uncertainty.

## Secondary objectives

- Explain the result in concrete terms.
- Connect the product to Jimmy Coco’s professional method.
- Resolve concerns about undertone, depth, streaking, transfer, dryness and fading.
- Increase average order value through a relevant routine.
- Preserve a clear path back to shade matching when suitability is uncertain.

## Required page sequence

### 01 — Breadcrumb and context

Keep this quiet. It supports orientation without competing with the product.

### 02 — Above-the-fold purchase zone

This must contain:

- Product gallery
- Product name and category
- Concise result statement
- Rating and verified-review count
- Price and size
- Variant or depth controls where relevant
- Suitability summary
- Primary Add to Bag action
- Delivery, returns and payment reassurance
- Shade-match rescue path

### 03 — Result-at-a-glance strip

Communicate three to five high-value attributes such as:

- Undertone
- Colour depth
- Development time
- Finish
- Recommended experience level

This should be visually concise, not a row of marketing badges.

### 04 — Product story and mechanism

Explain why the product produces a more natural result. Connect formula characteristics to visible outcomes.

### 05 — Who it is for

Clarify suitable skin tones, desired depth, use cases, confidence level and any important exclusions.

### 06 — Real results

Show real skin outcomes with the product, number of coats, development time and lighting context.

### 07 — Application method

Present preparation, application, development and maintenance in a clear ritual.

### 08 — Formula and product detail

Include ingredients, texture, fragrance, finish, size, care and factual claims. Avoid burying essential information in decorative accordions.

### 09 — Reviews by concern

Allow visitors to understand how the product performs for customers with similar needs.

### 10 — Complete the routine

Recommend only the products that materially improve the result.

### 11 — Final purchase reminder

Repeat product identity, current selection, price and Add to Bag action without creating a second competing sales concept.

## Conversion hierarchy

### Primary action

**ADD TO BAG**

### Secondary action

**Find My Match** or **Check My Shade** when suitability is uncertain.

### Tertiary actions

- View real results
- Read application method
- Read reviews
- Explore ingredients

Tertiary actions must never be styled like primary purchase controls.

## Page rhythm

Recommended cadence:

Dense purchase zone → concise facts → editorial explanation → visual proof → instructional clarity → detailed reassurance → routine → decisive close.

Alternate density and background tone. Do not use repeated cream card grids.

## Non-negotiable clarity rules

- Product name, price and purchase action must be visible without scrolling on standard desktop.
- The selected variant must always be explicit.
- Never make the visitor infer who the product suits from poetic copy.
- Never hide stock status or delivery expectations.
- Never use vague labels such as “Option 1” or colour swatches without names.
- The page must still work when photography is unavailable or slow to load.

## Accessibility

- Maintain logical heading order.
- Provide descriptive image alt text.
- Variant selectors must use real controls with visible selected, hover, focus, unavailable and error states.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate depth or availability.
- Keep body copy at a comfortable reading size and line length.
- All essential product facts must be available to assistive technology.

## Success criteria

The page succeeds when a visitor can accurately answer:

- What will this product do for me?
- Is it suitable for my skin and desired result?
- Which version should I select?
- What will it cost?
- How do I use it?
- Why should I trust it?
- What should I buy with it?

—and can add the correct selection to bag without hesitation.
