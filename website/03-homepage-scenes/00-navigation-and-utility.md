# Scene 00 — Navigation and Utility

## Role in the experience

The navigation provides orientation without competing with the hero. It must feel calm, premium and immediately understandable.

## Commercial objective

Support three primary visitor behaviours:

1. Find the right shade
2. Shop products directly
3. Learn why Sunless is different

## Primary navigation architecture

Recommended consumer navigation:

- Shop
- Find Your Shade
- Results
- Jimmy’s Method
- About Jimmy

Utility actions:

- Search
- Account
- Bag

Professional shopping should remain accessible but visually secondary to the consumer journey. It may sit in a utility area or under Shop, depending on final information architecture.

## Priority rule

`Find Your Shade` is the most valuable navigational action and should receive subtle visual privilege.

Approved treatments:

- Fine outlined pill
- Slightly stronger weight
- Restrained underline
- Distinct spacing

Do not make it look like a loud promotional button in the navigation.

## Header composition

- Slim utility bar above the main navigation where required
- Logo with generous breathing room
- Navigation positioned with clear grouping
- Utility icons aligned consistently
- No crowded mega-menu visible in the hero concept

The header should feel integrated with the hero environment rather than placed on a detached white strip unless legibility requires it.

## Utility bar

Use only high-value, factual assurances.

Potential messages:

- Free UK delivery over the verified threshold
- Clear returns or shade-support message
- Delivery cut-off when operationally accurate

Limit the number of messages. Tiny rotating promotional copy should not create cognitive noise.

## Interaction principles

- Clear hover and focus states
- Generous pointer targets
- Keyboard-accessible menus
- Predictable dropdown behaviour
- No hover-only critical information
- Search should open quickly and support product, shade and concern terms

## Mobile behaviour

Mobile navigation should prioritise:

1. Menu
2. Logo
3. Bag
4. Sticky or persistent `Find My Shade` action after the hero

Do not crowd the mobile header with all desktop utilities.

## Visual language

- Refined sans-serif
- Deep charcoal text
- Minimal iconography
- Fine lines
- No heavy shadows
- No glassmorphism
- No oversized promotional banners

## Common AI failure modes

Reject concepts that:

- Replace the logo with invented lettering
- Use generic ecommerce navigation
- Add oversized sale banners
- Make all navigation items equal when `Find Your Shade` is strategically primary
- Use tiny unreadable utility text
- Add excessive icons
- Separate the header from the hero with a harsh unrelated background

## Success criteria

The navigation succeeds when:

- The user understands the primary routes immediately
- The shade journey is visibly privileged
- The header feels premium and restrained
- The hero remains the dominant emotional scene
- Search, account and bag remain easy to locate

Design a slim, elegant utility bar with only concise factual reassurance, followed by a calm main navigation containing **Shop**, **Find Your Shade**, **Results**, **Jimmy’s Method** and **About Jimmy**, plus minimal search, account and bag icons. Give **Find Your Shade** subtle visual privilege through an understated outline, weight or spacing treatment without making it look promotional.

Integrate the navigation naturally into the warm ivory hero environment. Use generous horizontal spacing, deep charcoal typography, fine rules and clear alignment. Avoid a generic Shopify header, crowded mega-menu, discount banner, glassmorphism, excessive icons or tiny unreadable text.
