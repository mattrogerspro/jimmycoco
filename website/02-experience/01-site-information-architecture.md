# Site Information Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the minimum website structure required to move a visitor from discovery to confident purchase without creating a large, generic ecommerce catalogue.

The architecture must support two equally important behaviours:

1. Visitors who want expert guidance before choosing.
2. Visitors who already know what they want and need a fast route to purchase.

## Primary navigation

Use a calm, restrained navigation with no more than six primary destinations:

- Shop
- Find Your Shade
- Results
- The Method
- About Jimmy
- Journal

Utility actions:

- Search
- Account
- Bag

Do not overload the header with every product category. Product taxonomy belongs inside Shop and search.

## Core page hierarchy

### Home

Role: introduce the promise, establish authority, reduce uncertainty and direct visitors into shade matching or curated products.

### Shop

Role: provide structured product discovery without forcing visitors to understand internal product naming.

Recommended routes:

- Shop All
- Best Sellers
- Face
- Body
- Gradual Tan
- Instant Tan
- Preparation
- Application
- Sets and Routines

### Product Detail Page

Role: convert product interest into confident purchase by explaining suitability, result, application, proof and complementary routine.

### Find Your Shade

Role: replace guesswork with a guided recommendation based on undertone, desired depth, experience and use case.

### Shade Match Result

Role: present one primary recommendation, one alternative and the exact application plan.

### Results

Role: prove outcomes across real skin tones, undertones, product types, coat counts and development times.

### The Method

Role: explain Jimmy Coco’s professional logic: preparation, undertone matching, controlled layering and finish.

### About Jimmy

Role: connect authority to product decisions rather than provide biography for its own sake.

### Journal

Role: answer practical self-tan questions, support search visibility and provide expert guidance.

### Cart and Checkout

Role: preserve confidence, reduce friction and prevent unexpected cost or delivery uncertainty.

## Product taxonomy principles

Products should be organised by customer need before internal category.

Preferred taxonomy:

- Desired result
- Application area
- Development speed
- Experience level
- Skin depth or undertone suitability
- Format

Avoid requiring the customer to know the difference between every mousse, mist, serum and lotion before they understand the result.

## Navigation behaviour

### Desktop

- Header remains visually quiet.
- Shop may open a structured mega menu with a maximum of three columns.
- Find Your Shade is visually privileged but not promotional.
- Bag count is visible without becoming a badge-heavy interface.

### Mobile

- Use a simple full-height menu.
- Keep Find Your Shade near the top.
- Place utility actions in the header.
- Do not reproduce a desktop mega menu as nested complexity.

## Breadcrumbs

Use breadcrumbs on:

- Collection pages
- Product pages
- Journal articles
- Results detail pages

Breadcrumbs should aid orientation and search structure without becoming visually prominent.

## Search architecture

Search should recognise:

- Product names
- Product types
- Desired outcomes
- Concerns such as streaking, transfer or fading
- Use cases such as face, holiday, event or first-time tanning

Search results should prioritise products, followed by guidance content.

## Footer architecture

Group links into a limited set:

- Shop
- Guidance
- About
- Help
- Legal

The footer is utility, not another promotional section.

## Governance rules

- Every primary page must have one clear role.
- Avoid duplicate pages that answer the same customer question.
- Do not create campaign landing pages that conflict with core product or shade-match logic.
- New pages must identify their position in the customer journey before approval.
- Product pages remain the primary commercial destination.

## Success criteria

The architecture succeeds when a new visitor can answer these questions quickly:

- What does Sunless sell?
- Which product is right for me?
- Why should I trust the result?
- How do I use it?
- What should I buy next?
