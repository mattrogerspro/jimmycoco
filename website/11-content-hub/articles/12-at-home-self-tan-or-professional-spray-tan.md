# At-Home Self-Tan or Professional Spray Tan? A Jimmy Coco Guide

Choose at-home self-tan when you want a repeatable routine, control over timing and the ability to maintain face and body colour separately. Choose a professional spray tan when you want an artist to assess the result, select a solution and apply it evenly for you—particularly before an important event or when you find full-body application difficult.

Both routes use Jimmy Coco’s tanning method. The difference is who controls the consultation and application.

## The quick comparison

| Decision | Jimmy Coco at home | Jimmy Coco professional spray tan |
|---|---|---|
| Who applies it? | You | Trained spray-tan artist |
| Main format | Tinted Tan Soufflé and facial mist | HVLP/airbrush professional solution |
| Shade/depth route | Malibu Medium or Dark; Original Soufflé in relevant markets | Laguna Light/Medium, Malibu Medium/Dark or Sunset Dark/Extra Dark |
| Best for | Repeat routines, privacy, flexible timing, separate face maintenance | Consultation, hard-to-reach coverage, event preparation, artist control |
| Application tools | Buff & Glow Mitt, Face Brush, Body Brush | Professional equipment and artist technique |
| Current development guidance | Malibu and A-List Face Mist: 6–8 hours | Professional solutions: 6–12 hours with additional approved guidance |

Always follow the exact product or salon directions. These timing ranges are not interchangeable.

## Choose at home when you value repeatable control

The at-home route works well when you want to learn one routine and repeat it on your own schedule. [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) has a visible guide colour, Medium and Dark shade options and a buildable approach. The [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) covers the main body areas, while a brush adds precision around hands and feet.

The [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) separates facial maintenance from the body routine. That is useful because the face is cleansed more often and may fade differently.

At-home tanning asks more of your technique. You need to prepare the skin, reach every area, control edges and reserve the full development period. The benefit is familiarity: once you know your shade, amount and timing, the process becomes easier to repeat.

## Choose a professional service when you want artist control

A professional service gives another person responsibility for coverage, spray pattern and the application plan. It is particularly valuable when:

- you cannot reach the back evenly;
- the event includes photography or a revealing neckline;
- you want an artist to assess the appropriate depth;
- previous at-home applications have produced difficult edges;
- you want the result recorded and refined at a later appointment.

The current Jimmy Coco professional range offers a simple depth ladder:

- [Laguna Light/Medium](/products/laguna-ltr-professional-spray-light-medium)
- [Malibu Medium/Dark](/products/malibu-1-ltr-professional-spray-medium-dark)
- [Sunset Dark/Extra Dark](/products/laguna-ltr-professional-spray-light-medium-copy)

A good consultation should translate your desired finish into the appropriate solution and development plan rather than asking you to choose a bottle without context.

## Which route is better before an event?

Neither is automatically better. The best route is the one you have tested and can execute calmly.

Choose at home if you already know how Malibu develops on your skin and can create an even face/body result. Choose a professional if artist-led application removes a genuine difficulty or gives you greater confidence.

Whichever route you choose, trial it before a wedding, major party or photoshoot. Use the [72-Hour Event Tan Plan](/blogs/jimmy-coco-method/72-hour-event-tan-plan) so the final application is not an experiment.

## Can you combine the routes?

Yes, when the products and timing are used appropriately. A customer may receive a professional body spray tan, then maintain facial colour later with the A-List Face Mist. Another may use Malibu at home between professional appointments.

Do not layer consumer and professional products into the same development period without an approved plan from the artist and brand. The formats have different directions.

## Questions to ask yourself

### Can I apply my back and transitions evenly?

If not, a professional service may provide more consistent coverage. If the difficulty is mainly hands and feet, adding The Body Brush may solve the at-home problem.

### Do I want to maintain my face separately?

The A-List Face Mist makes the at-home route particularly flexible for facial maintenance.

### Is this a first trial or a known routine?

Choose the familiar route for a high-stakes event. Test a new route well beforehand.

### Do I want subtle colour or maximum depth?

Both routes offer depth choice. Focus on the intended result and application quality rather than assuming a salon must be darker or home must be lighter.

## What both routes have in common

- Preparation affects evenness.
- The right depth begins with consultation or shade choice.
- Hands, feet, face and transitions need restraint.
- Development and rinse instructions matter.
- More solution is not automatically better.
- Self-tan does not provide sun protection.
