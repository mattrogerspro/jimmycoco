# 04 — Questionnaire Interface and Interaction

## Experience character

The interface should feel calm, focused and premium. It is a consultation, not a game.

## Desktop composition

Use a full-screen or near-full-screen layout with:

- quiet Sunless header;
- progress and exit controls;
- narrow question column;
- spacious answer area;
- optional accurate supporting image;
- persistent Back and Continue actions;
- generous negative space.

Avoid a small modal, dashboard layout or dense survey form.

## Interaction rules

- Selection produces an immediate, clear state change.
- Continue remains disabled until a valid answer is chosen, except where skipping is explicitly allowed.
- Back preserves valid answers.
- Keyboard focus is visible and logical.
- Screen readers receive the question, answer group and selected state correctly.
- Motion is restrained and respects reduced-motion settings.
- Loading or calculation never traps the customer.

## Selected states

Use more than colour alone: border, fill, icon and text treatment may reinforce selection. Maintain strong contrast and avoid delicate beige-on-ivory states.

## Visual assets

When answer imagery is used:

- keep lighting and colour management consistent;
- use real skin texture;
- represent the full intended skin-depth range;
- avoid retouched celebrity imagery as a diagnostic tool;
- include descriptive alternative text or an equivalent text label.

## Transition behaviour

Use a short fade or lateral transition that reinforces progress without creating a carousel-like entertainment effect. Do not animate product bottles or add decorative particles.

## Calculation state

The transition to results may briefly show:

> Matching your desired depth, timing and application preferences…

Keep this under roughly one second where possible. Do not manufacture a long wait to imply complex science.

## Help layers

Contextual help should open beside or beneath the relevant question. It must never erase the customer’s selection or force them into a new page.

## Accessibility

- Minimum 44px touch targets.
- Proper fieldset and legend semantics where applicable.
- No time limit.
- No auto-advancing screen-reader focus without announcement.
- Errors explained in text.
- Browser zoom up to 200% supported.
- Focus returns predictably after help dialogs close.

## Success criteria

The customer can complete the consultation by mouse, keyboard, touch or assistive technology without confusion or lost answers.
