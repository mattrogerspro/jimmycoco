# How Long Should Jimmy Coco Self-Tan Develop?

Jimmy Coco’s current Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé and A-List Face Tanning Mist directions both specify a 6–8 hour development period before rinsing. Treat that window as product instruction, not as a challenge to leave the formula on for as long as possible. The right timing is the timing approved for the formula and the result you want.

Always check the label you are holding. Product directions can change, and professional spray solutions use different instructions from at-home products.

## What “development time” means

Development time is the period in which the tanning ingredients create colour on the outer surface of the skin. The visible guide colour helps you apply evenly, but it is not the same as the final developed tan.

That distinction explains three common surprises:

- the guide can look deeper than the result after rinsing;
- the first rinse may remove a large amount of visible colour;
- the most useful time to judge the result is after the instructed development and rinse, not halfway through.

Do not apply the professional timing to a consumer product or the consumer timing to a professional spray solution.

## Should you choose six hours or eight?

Use the shorter end of the approved window when you want a more restrained result or are testing the formula for the first time. Use the longer end when you want the formula to complete the full approved development period. Shade, amount, starting tone and application evenness also influence what you see; time is not the only control.

If the goal is a deeper tan, do not automatically extend beyond the directions. Choose the appropriate shade or use a later controlled application instead.

## How to plan the 6–8 hour window

### Daytime application

Apply in the morning or early afternoon when you can keep the skin dry, avoid strenuous activity and rinse at the planned time. This can be useful if you prefer not to sleep in guide colour.

### Evening application

Apply early enough that the instructed window fits your routine. Wear loose, dark clothing and protect anything that may come into contact with the guide colour. Do not shorten preparation simply because the day is ending.

### Before an event

Leave enough time for a full application, development, rinse and assessment. The event itself should not be your first test. Use the [72-Hour Event Tan Plan](/blogs/jimmy-coco-method/72-hour-event-tan-plan) to create a calmer schedule.

## What to do during development

- Keep the skin dry.
- Avoid exercise or heat that causes heavy perspiration.
- Choose loose clothing that does not create pressure lines.
- Avoid repeatedly touching or inspecting the guide colour.
- Do not spot-add more product after the application has begun to set unless the approved directions tell you to.

## How to rinse

Follow the directions for the specific Jimmy Coco product. For the A-List Face Mist, the current product page instructs rinsing with water only after 6–8 hours. The guide colour washes away to reveal the developed result.

Keep the first rinse gentle. Do not use it as an opportunity for aggressive exfoliation. Pat the skin dry instead of rubbing it hard with a towel.

## Why longer is not always darker—or better

Once a product’s approved window has passed, leaving the guide on longer does not create unlimited depth. It may simply add inconvenience, transfer risk or dryness. A premium result comes from shade choice, even application, controlled edges and the correct rinse—not from endurance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I rinse Malibu after two hours?

The current Malibu direction is 6–8 hours. Do not substitute express-tan timing from another formula.

### Can I wear makeup while the A-List Face Mist develops?

The product is described as light enough to sit over skincare and under makeup, but the final routine must follow the current directions and your own product compatibility. Avoid disturbing freshly applied mist.

### Can I go into the sun while my tan develops?

Development does not provide sun protection. Use appropriate sun-protection measures and avoid any activity that compromises the application.

Choose the right Jimmy Coco product, apply it evenly, reserve the full approved window and judge the result after rinsing.
