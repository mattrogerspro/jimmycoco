# Replenishment — Entry Trigger, Consent and Suppression

## Entry trigger

A contact becomes eligible when the application reaches the approved replenishment window for a fulfilled product and the sequence has passed all consent, order, product and flow-state checks.

The trigger should be generated by the application’s lifecycle scheduler, not by Resend. Resend delivers the approved message and returns delivery events; it does not own replenishment eligibility.

## Required eligibility checks

Before enrolment, confirm:

- a valid customer and destination address;
- valid marketing consent for the applicable jurisdiction;
- a fulfilled or delivered qualifying order;
- an approved replenishment model for the purchased product;
- no newer order has already replenished the same need;
- the order has not been cancelled, fully refunded or invalidated;
- the customer is not in a relevant subscription flow;
- the product or an approved replacement is available;
- the contact is not suppressed, bounced or complaint-blocked;
- the customer is within global contact-pressure limits;
- no higher-priority lifecycle or service flow currently owns the customer.

## Consent requirements

Check consent at enrolment and immediately before every send.

Store:

- consent state;
- source;
- timestamp;
- form or policy version;
- jurisdiction when required;
- unsubscribe timestamp;
- suppression reason.

A previous purchase does not automatically provide permission for marketing messages.

## Exclusions

Exclude customers when:

- only transactional permission exists;
- the product is a one-time, gift or non-replenishable item;
- the product has no approved timing model;
- the customer has an unresolved complaint, reaction, return or delivery issue tied to the product;
- the order was refunded, cancelled or replaced in a way that invalidates the timing;
- the customer is receiving the product through an active subscription;
- the destination address is suppressed;
- there is insufficient confidence to produce a useful reminder;
- the product is unavailable and no approved alternative exists.

## Immediate exit conditions

Exit when:

- a relevant reorder is completed;
- checkout begins for the relevant item and checkout recovery takes ownership;
- the relevant item enters cart and cart abandonment takes ownership;
- consent is withdrawn;
- the customer asks to stop or pause reminders;
- a refund, return, replacement or support issue opens;
- the product is retired without an approved equivalent;
- the sequence completes.

## Pause conditions

Pause when:

- the relevant item is temporarily out of stock;
- a higher-priority message is scheduled;
- the customer is inside quiet hours;
- current price, currency, variant, URL or stock cannot be validated;
- the customer’s latest order state has not yet settled;
- a newer purchase may match the same need but product mapping is unresolved.

Every paused send must be fully revalidated. Do not release stale content automatically.

## Contact-pressure rules

Default safeguards:

- one active replenishment sequence per replenishment need;
- one behavioural marketing email per day maximum;
- replenishment counts toward the global weekly pressure cap;
- overlapping product windows should be consolidated where useful;
- general campaigns should yield when a high-confidence replenishment message is due, but only within the approved priority framework.

## Pre-send validation

Immediately before rendering, verify:

- consent and suppression state;
- customer identity and email;
- sequence and model version;
- product, variant and quantity;
- current title, price and currency;
- current stock and purchasability;
- valid destination URL;
- approved image asset;
- no newer purchase, cart or checkout state;
- no active subscription shipment;
- no service or support exclusion;
- idempotency key has not been used.

If essential data is missing, suppress or pause. Never invent product details or send a generic reorder CTA to compensate.
