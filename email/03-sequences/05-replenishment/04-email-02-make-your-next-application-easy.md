# Email 02 — Make Your Next Application Easy

## Role

Help the customer maintain their routine by removing practical friction around preparation, application and product availability.

This message should be useful even when the customer is not yet ready to reorder.

## Default timing

Send several days after Email 01, based on the product-level replenishment window and only when no newer purchase, suppression event or higher-priority flow has taken ownership.

## Primary objective

Support continuity of use while giving the customer one clear route to replenish the relevant product.

## Message hierarchy

1. Routine continuity headline
2. Short reminder of the product’s role
3. One practical application or preparation insight
4. Reorder module using current product data
5. One primary CTA
6. Optional low-emphasis delay or preference link

## Content routes

Choose one route from verified order and product context:

- preparation before the next application;
- maintaining an even result;
- face-product continuity;
- body-product continuity;
- replacing a frequently used application tool;
- preserving a professional routine;
- clarifying how the product fits with another previously purchased item.

Do not claim the customer is definitely low or has used the product in a specific way unless that information was explicitly provided.

## Copy guidance

Use language such as:

- `When you are ready for your next application`
- `Keep your routine simple`
- `A useful step before your next tan`
- `Reorder when the timing feels right`

Avoid:

- `You must be nearly out`
- `Your bottle is empty`
- `Do not miss your next tan`
- false countdowns or stock pressure.

## Product module

The replenishment module may show:

- approved source product image;
- exact product and variant last purchased;
- current price and currency;
- current availability;
- quantity selector only when technically safe and supported;
- secure reorder CTA.

If the original variant is unavailable, do not silently substitute another variant. Offer an approved alternative or route to product selection.

## Primary CTA options

- `Reorder my product`
- `Prepare for my next application`
- `View my routine`

Use one primary CTA. A secondary text link may allow the customer to delay reminders or review alternatives.

## Practical guidance module

The supporting tip must be product-specific and approved. It may cover:

- exfoliation timing;
- clean, dry skin preparation;
- application-tool care;
- blending difficult areas;
- development-time planning;
- aftercare that supports an even fade.

Do not include medical or safety claims beyond approved product instructions.

## Asset rules

- Use approved source product imagery only.
- Do not redraw packaging, labels, colour or texture with generative tools.
- Use customer or celebrity imagery only where rights and context are approved.
- Do not alter skin tone, tan depth or body shape.

## Pre-send validation

Confirm:

- consent and suppression state;
- replenishment estimate remains active;
- no newer purchase reset the product clock;
- product and variant are still valid;
- current stock, price and currency;
- destination URL;
- no duplicate send key;
- contact-pressure and quiet-hour compliance.

## Exit and handoff

Exit or transfer when:

- the customer repurchases;
- the customer buys an approved substitute;
- the customer pauses reminders;
- the product becomes unavailable without a valid route;
- consent is withdrawn;
- a service or support state takes priority.

## Success criteria

Measure qualified reorder sessions, repurchase, routine-content engagement, reminder preference changes and margin contribution. Monitor unsubscribes and complaints to ensure the message remains useful rather than presumptive.
