## Two technical traps worth knowing

**Klaviyo clones templates on assign.** `assign_template_to_campaign_message` creates a *new* "Clone of X" and attaches the clone. Editing the library template does **not** change what a campaign or flow sends — you must re-assign to force a fresh clone. Same for flow messages. Orphaned clones from this session: `TBqcPf`, `S3V6fa`, `QVQZuX`.

**Never use `white-space:nowrap` on multi-item rows** inside the 600px wrapper. HTML tables expand to fit non-wrapping content — that's what pushed the trust bar to 731.8px.
