# 06 — Email 05: Complete Your Routine

## Send timing

Approximately seven days after completion, subject to suppression and frequency controls.

## Job to be done

Show the smallest useful preparation and application routine that supports the recommended result without turning the email into a broad product catalogue.

## Core message

> Your matched product is the centre of the routine. These are the only supporting steps that materially improve preparation, application or finish.

## Primary CTA

**BUILD MY ROUTINE**

Destination: a recommendation-aware routine page or cart builder with the matched product and approved complementary items preselected but not automatically added.

## Content structure

1. Reconfirm the recommended hero product.
2. Present no more than three stages:
   - Prepare
   - Apply
   - Perfect or maintain
3. Include one product per supporting stage at most.
4. Explain the functional role of each item in one sentence.
5. Show individual and routine prices only when current and validated.
6. Primary CTA.
7. Quiet option to purchase only the matched product.

## Suggested subject lines

- The routine built around your match
- Prepare, apply, perfect
- Everything you need for your matched result

## Preview text

A concise routine centred on the product selected for you.

## Merchandising rules

- The recommendation must remain the dominant product.
- Complementary products must be compatible and in stock.
- Do not add unrelated best sellers.
- Do not create artificial bundles solely to increase order value.
- Never imply an accessory is mandatory when it is optional.
- Bundle savings must be real, current and clearly calculated.

## Design direction

Use a restrained linear routine module with one large hero product and smaller supporting items. Maintain a clear visual difference between the core recommendation and optional additions.

Product imagery must use approved source assets without generative alteration.

## Dynamic data

- matched product and selected variant;
- approved complementary product IDs;
- stage labels and benefit copy;
- current stock, price and currency;
- routine-builder URL;
- direct matched-product URL.

## Fallbacks

- If a supporting item is unavailable, remove that stage or use an approved substitute.
- If only the matched product is relevant, shorten the email and focus on application confidence.
- Do not show empty or placeholder routine slots.

## Plain-text version

List the matched product first, followed by each optional supporting item, its purpose, price when verified and the routine-builder link.
