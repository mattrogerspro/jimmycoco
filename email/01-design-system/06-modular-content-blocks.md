# Modular Content Blocks

## Purpose

Create a reusable email system that produces variety through composition while keeping implementation efficient and brand-consistent.

## Module library

### 1. Brand header
Compact logo, optional utility link and no website-style navigation overload.

### 2. Editorial hero
Image, short serif headline, supporting sentence and one primary CTA.

### 3. Product hero
Accurate product image, result-led proposition, suitability guidance, price and CTA.

### 4. Expert note
Jimmy portrait or working image, concise professional guidance and signature-style attribution.

### 5. Shade-match result
Customer profile summary, one recommended product, reason for the match and direct purchase action.

### 6. Benefit trio
Three concise benefits displayed horizontally on desktop and stacked on mobile. Use only when each benefit adds distinct value.

### 7. Method steps
A numbered 2–4 step educational sequence for preparation, application or finishing.

### 8. Real-result proof
Unaltered customer result image with product, coats, development time and disclosure.

### 9. Single review
One specific, credible review with concern or result context. Avoid generic testimonial walls.

### 10. Product pair
Two clearly differentiated products with suitability labels and individual actions.

### 11. Routine builder
Prepare, Apply and Perfect modules with one principal product and restrained supporting additions.

### 12. Offer or delivery notice
A compact factual module for verified promotions, thresholds or deadlines.

### 13. Dark closing CTA
Short emotional restatement and one decisive action on deep charcoal.

### 14. Footer
Preferences, support, legal text, social links and unsubscribe in a quiet, readable structure.

## Assembly rules

- Use 3–5 meaningful modules for most campaigns.
- Start with proposition, not navigation.
- Alternate commercial and educational density.
- Do not use every module in one email.
- Avoid two consecutive product grids.
- Keep one dominant story and CTA hierarchy.

## Module metadata

Each production module should document:

- module name and version
- intended lifecycle stages
- required assets
- editable fields
- mobile stacking order
- dark-mode behaviour
- accessibility requirements
- analytics link names

## Success criteria

The module system succeeds when teams can create varied, recognisably Sunless emails without inventing new layouts for every send or allowing the emails to become repetitive template stacks.
