# Sunless VIP and Loyalty Sequence

The VIP and loyalty system recognises sustained customer value without reducing the relationship to discounts or generic status labels.

Its purpose is to reward meaningful loyalty with better service, relevant access, expert support and thoughtful recognition while protecting customer trust, margin and operational credibility.

## Governing principles

1. Loyalty should improve the customer experience, not merely increase message volume.
2. Qualification must use transparent, auditable rules.
3. High value does not justify ignoring consent, complaints or service risk.
4. Benefits must be operationally real before they are communicated.
5. Recognition should be specific and restrained, never overfamiliar.
6. Discounts are optional benefits, not the definition of VIP treatment.
7. Tier changes must be handled respectfully and never as punishment.
8. Product, customer and celebrity imagery must remain faithful to approved source assets.

## Default programme rhythm

The VIP system is event-led rather than a fixed promotional sequence. Core lifecycle moments include qualification, welcome, meaningful access, milestone recognition, renewal review and transition.
