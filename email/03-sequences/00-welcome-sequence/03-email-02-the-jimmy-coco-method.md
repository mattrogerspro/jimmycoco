# Email 02 — The Jimmy Coco Method

## Send timing

Approximately one day after Email 01, subject to contact-pressure and transactional-priority rules.

## Job of the email

Turn Jimmy Coco’s authority into practical product confidence. This is not a biography email. Every part of the story must explain why the customer’s result can look more natural.

## Primary message

> Natural-looking colour comes from judgement, not simply a darker formula.

## Primary CTA

**DISCOVER THE METHOD**

The destination should be a concise website section or editorial page explaining undertone, buildable depth, preparation and controlled application.

## Suggested subject-line directions

- Why professional colour looks different
- The method behind a more natural-looking tan
- Jimmy’s approach: undertone, depth and control
- A better tan is built, not guessed

## Preview-text directions

- Three principles from Jimmy’s professional work.
- Match the tone, build the depth, control the finish.
- The judgement behind every Sunless recommendation.

## Content architecture

1. Minimal header.
2. Approved Jimmy Coco working image or a professionally accurate application detail.
3. Editorial headline and short introduction.
4. Three method principles.
5. Primary CTA.
6. One concise authority statement.
7. Footer and preference controls.

## Three method principles

### 1. Start with tone
Colour should complement the customer’s natural colouring and desired finish rather than impose one universal bronze.

### 2. Build depth deliberately
A controllable, buildable result is easier to personalise than an undifferentiated “darkest possible” promise.

### 3. Control preparation and finish
Evenness, blending and fading depend on method as well as formula.

Do not make scientific or performance claims beyond approved evidence.

## Copy direction

Use Jimmy’s experience as a reason to trust the method, not as celebrity name-dropping. The customer should finish the email understanding one useful idea they can apply.

Suggested body direction:

> Under red-carpet lighting, colour has nowhere to hide. Jimmy’s approach has always been to work with the person’s natural tone, build the depth gradually and control the finish from preparation to final blend.
>
> Sunless translates that professional judgement into a clearer at-home routine.

## Visual direction

- Asymmetrical editorial composition within the email canvas.
- One unaltered approved photograph of Jimmy, if available.
- Three principles presented as simple numbered sections, not heavy icon cards.
- Deep charcoal typography on warm ivory or clean white.
- One matte black CTA.

## Personalisation

If a shade-match result exists, a small contextual line may connect the method to the saved recommendation:

> Your saved match applies these principles to your preferred depth and format.

Do not expose raw quiz answers unnecessarily.

## Failure modes

Reject the email if it:

- becomes a long life story;
- relies on unnamed celebrity associations as proof;
- uses a fake quotation;
- presents three decorative cards with little substance;
- adds pseudo-scientific language;
- alters Jimmy’s approved photograph;
- introduces an unrelated discount.

## Success criteria

- Jimmy’s authority feels relevant to product performance.
- The reader learns three clear principles.
- The email remains concise enough for mobile reading.
- The CTA naturally continues the education journey.
- The message strengthens, rather than delays, purchase confidence.
