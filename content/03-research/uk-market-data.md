## The three numbers the strategy is built on

**Retention.** Only **29% of new salon clients ever rebook** — 71% never return.
Phorest, 2025, **n=716**.

**No-shows.** **Tanning has the highest no-show rate of any treatment category,
at 3.14%**, peaking June–August. Treatwell, 2025. UK no-shows cost the sector
**£1.6bn a year**, at roughly **£39 per miss**. **Pre-payment halves them; only
17% of salons use it.**

**Retail.** Retail is **4% of UK salon revenue against a 15–25% benchmark**
(SalonIQ, 2026), and **70% of clients who don't buy retail say they would like
to** (Phorest).

## Do not use

Recorded so nobody reintroduces them.

- **Any claim that X% of UK salons offer spray tanning.** No such figure exists.
  Several sources appear to give one; none of them survive checking.
- **2015 Mintel penetration data presented as current.**
- **Whito's "£1,000/month average client spend."** Implausible and contradicted
  by the arithmetic — roughly £40 a visit at about five visits a year is an
  order of magnitude out.
