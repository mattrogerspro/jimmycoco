# Shade-Match Questionnaire and Results Experience

This folder defines the complete guided-selling journey that helps customers choose the right Sunless product, shade depth and routine with confidence.

The system must feel like expert guidance from Jimmy Coco translated into a calm, elegant digital consultation—not a generic quiz, lead-generation form or novelty beauty tool.

## Core objective

Reduce uncertainty, recommend one clear best-fit product, explain why it suits the customer and provide a direct route to purchase.

## Non-negotiable principles

- No email gate before results.
- No false precision or pseudo-scientific claims.
- One dominant recommendation, not a confusing product list.
- Explain the recommendation in plain language.
- Preserve customer answers when moving into product pages and cart.
- Every recommendation must be backed by current product data and approved claims.
- The experience must remain useful even when the customer is unsure of an answer.
