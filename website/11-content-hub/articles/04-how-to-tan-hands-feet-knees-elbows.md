# How to Tan Hands, Feet, Knees and Elbows Evenly

The rule for hands, feet, knees and elbows is simple: prepare them carefully, tan them last and use less product than you used on the main body. These areas have more edges, creases and dry texture, so an ordinary full-strength pass can develop too heavily.

Use the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) to complete the broad body areas, then use the small amount remaining on the mitt or blend with [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush). The goal is a soft transition, not identical coverage.

## Why these areas go darker

Self-tan can look stronger where the skin is drier or where product collects. Hands and feet also expose every border: fingers, toes, nails, wrists and ankles. Knees and elbows fold during application, hiding a missed crease until the joint straightens.

That means the solution is not a stronger scrub after the mistake. It is a more deliberate application before the colour develops.

## Prepare the day before

Exfoliate the hands, feet, knees and elbows as part of your normal preparation, paying attention to rough edges without overworking the skin. Complete hair removal early enough for the skin to settle.

On application day, the skin should be clean and dry. If a small dry area needs moisturiser, apply it sparingly to that area and let it absorb. Avoid coating the entire limb in a rich or oily layer immediately before tanning.

## Tan the main body first

Apply [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) to the legs, torso and arms before touching the hands, feet, knees or elbows. This leaves only a small amount of product on the mitt when you reach the precision areas.

Finish one side completely before moving to the other so the wrist and ankle transitions stay consistent.

## How to tan hands

1. Relax the hand and spread the fingers slightly.
2. With almost no fresh product, sweep from the wrist over the back of the hand.
3. Blend each finger lightly, keeping the palm free of tan.
4. Bend the fingers so you can soften the knuckles without filling every crease.
5. Use The Body Brush to blur the wrist line and work around the nail edges.
6. Check the hand from the side as well as from above.

The palm should remain naturally lighter. If product reaches it, clean the area before development rather than waiting for a visible border.

## How to tan feet

1. Complete the leg first.
2. Use residual product to sweep across the top of the foot.
3. Blend from the ankle downward so there is no sharp “sock” line.
4. Use a brush around the outer edges and toes.
5. Keep the sole and the spaces between toes clear.

Feet should look like a continuation of the leg, not a separate application zone.

## How to tan knees and elbows

Apply the main limb with the joint gently bent. This exposes the creases and prevents a pale line when the arm or leg moves. As you finish, straighten the joint and use the mitt or brush to diffuse any heavy edge.

Do not add a fresh pump directly to a knee or elbow. If the surrounding limb needs more coverage, blend toward the joint and let the remaining product do the work.

## The brush technique

The Body Brush has densely packed, ultra-soft bristles and is intended to help blend the Soufflé, particularly around the edges of hands and feet. Use one small amount at a time and work with light, makeup-like motions.

The brush is most useful for:

- softening the wrist and ankle;
- distributing residual product across knuckles;
- blending around the side of the foot;
- correcting a visible edge while the product is still fresh.

Clean the brush according to the approved care instructions and allow it to dry fully before storage.

## Common mistakes

- **Using a fresh full pump on each hand or foot.** Precision areas rarely need the same amount as a leg or arm.
- **Tanning with fingers, knees or elbows locked straight.** Creases may be missed.
- **Taking body colour onto palms and soles.** These areas should remain naturally lighter.
- **Stopping abruptly at the wrist or ankle.** Blend through the transition.
- **Adding more because the guide looks light.** Wait for full development before judging depth.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should my hands match my arms exactly?

They should belong to the same result, but hands naturally read lighter than the main arm. A slightly softer hand is more believable than an opaque match.

### Can I use the Face Brush on my hands?

Use the tool according to its intended instructions. The Body Brush is specifically described for blending body tan around hands and feet.

### What if I see a line while applying?

Blend it immediately with a clean section of the mitt or a lightly loaded brush. Do not wait for development and then try to cover the line with another heavy layer.
