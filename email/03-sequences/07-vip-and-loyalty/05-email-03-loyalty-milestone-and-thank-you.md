# Email 03 — Loyalty Milestone and Thank You

## Role

Recognise a meaningful customer milestone with specific, restrained gratitude and an appropriate benefit or service gesture.

The message should feel earned and human. It must not convert the customer’s history into an overfamiliar or overly detailed surveillance summary.

## Eligible milestones

Use only approved milestones that are both accurate and meaningful, such as:

- first anniversary of the customer relationship;
- a verified repeat-purchase milestone;
- sustained VIP qualification across a review period;
- a meaningful programme anniversary;
- completion of an approved loyalty threshold;
- a customer-selected birthday or occasion where explicit permission exists.

Do not manufacture arbitrary milestones simply to create another send.

## Primary objective

Strengthen loyalty by acknowledging the relationship and delivering a clear expression of value, gratitude or service.

## Message hierarchy

1. Specific but discreet recognition
2. Genuine thank-you
3. One approved benefit, service gesture or useful next step
4. Clear terms when the benefit has conditions
5. Preference and support access

## Recognition rules

Safe language:

- `Thank you for being part of Sunless`
- `A year of Sunless colour`
- `A moment to recognise your continued support`
- `Your loyalty means a great deal to us`

Avoid:

- publishing exact lifetime spend in the email;
- listing every purchase unless the customer explicitly requested an account summary;
- claiming emotional closeness;
- implying that spend determines personal worth;
- language that pressures the customer to maintain status.

## Benefit options

A milestone benefit may include:

- expert shade or routine support;
- priority access;
- an approved gift with purchase;
- complimentary shipping under documented terms;
- a carefully governed reward or credit;
- a relevant educational experience;
- a simple thank-you with no commercial requirement.

Every benefit must be active, funded, fulfilable and supported before communication.

## Subject-line territories

- A thank-you from Sunless
- A moment to celebrate your loyalty
- Your Sunless milestone
- Thank you for another year with us

Do not use a reward-led subject line when the message contains no real reward.

## Visual structure

1. Restrained programme identifier
2. Thank-you headline
3. Milestone statement
4. Optional benefit module
5. One primary CTA
6. Support or preferences
7. Footer

Avoid trophy graphics, gamified progress bars or aggressive status symbolism unless those elements are part of an approved programme design.

## Personalisation

Permitted fields may include:

- first name when reliable;
- programme tier;
- milestone type;
- milestone date or period;
- approved benefit;
- relevant product family or service preference.

Fallback copy must remain natural when any optional field is missing.

## Data validation

Before send, confirm:

- the milestone calculation is correct;
- the customer has not already received the same milestone message;
- current tier and programme state are accurate;
- the benefit is valid in the customer’s market;
- fulfilment and redemption paths work;
- consent and contact-pressure rules permit the send;
- no unresolved complaint, refund or safety issue exists.

## Handoffs

- Redemption should be recorded against the programme benefit ledger.
- Purchase transfers to post-purchase education.
- Cart activity may transfer to cart abandonment.
- Questions or issues transfer to support.
- Preference changes apply across all lifecycle flows.

## Success criteria

Measure benefit redemption, qualified engagement, repeat purchase, service usage, satisfaction, unsubscribe and complaint rates. Evaluate long-term retention rather than relying only on immediate milestone revenue.
