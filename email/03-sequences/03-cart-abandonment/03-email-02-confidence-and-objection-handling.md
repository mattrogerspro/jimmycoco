# Email 02 — Confidence and Objection Handling

## Role

Resolve the most likely reasons the customer paused after creating a cart.

## Default timing

Send approximately 20–24 hours after Email 01 when no purchase, checkout handoff or suppression event has occurred.

## Primary objective

Reduce uncertainty around suitability, application, delivery, returns or payment without repeating the first reminder.

## Objection priority

Select one dominant concern using known cart and journey context:

1. product or shade suitability;
2. application confidence;
3. result depth or development time;
4. delivery timing or cost;
5. returns or exchange confidence;
6. payment or checkout friction.

Do not infer sensitive skin conditions, personal traits or motivations from cart contents alone.

## Message hierarchy

1. Decision-support headline
2. Live cart summary
3. One focused reassurance module
4. One primary CTA to restore the cart
5. Optional customer-support link

## Suitability route

When valid shade-match or browse context exists, explain why the selected product or variant aligns with the customer’s stated preferences. Do not call it a personal match unless a valid recommendation exists.

## Application route

Use concise, accurate steps covering preparation, application and development. Link to deeper guidance rather than turning the email into a long tutorial.

## Delivery and returns route

Display only current market-specific information. Validate thresholds, estimated windows, exclusions and returns language at render time.

## Payment-friction route

If a recoverable checkout error is known, provide a safe return route and support option without exposing internal payment details or implying card failure when the cause is uncertain.

## Subject-line territories

- Everything you need to feel confident
- A little guidance before you decide
- Your Sunless routine, explained
- Questions about your selection?

## CTA

Use `Return to my cart` or `Continue checkout`. A secondary text link may offer `Get product guidance` or `Contact support`.

## Incentives

Do not introduce a discount merely because Email 01 did not convert. Any offer must come from an approved eligibility and margin policy and must be accurately represented in the restored cart.

## Validation

Before send, refresh cart state, consent, stock, price, discount, delivery and returns information, recovery URL and cross-flow ownership.

## Success criteria

Measure restored-cart sessions, checkout starts, support engagement and purchases. Monitor unsubscribe and complaint rates to ensure objection handling remains useful rather than intrusive.
