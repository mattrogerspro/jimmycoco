# Jimmy Coco Content Hub

This folder is the source of truth for customer-facing editorial, organic-search and product-education content for Sunless by Jimmy Coco.

## Scope

The content hub exists to help customers choose, apply and maintain Jimmy Coco tanning products with greater confidence. Customer-facing articles must concentrate on the Jimmy Coco range. Competitor names, competitor products and comparison copy belong only in internal research documents.

## Folder structure

- [`00-competitive-research.md`](00-competitive-research.md) — internal benchmark of ten leading tanning brands and the category opportunities it reveals
- [`01-content-strategy.md`](01-content-strategy.md) — positioning, audience, topic clusters, distribution and measurement
- [`02-editorial-playbook.md`](02-editorial-playbook.md) — writing, evidence, SEO/GEO, image and quality standards
- [`03-marketing-playbook.md`](03-marketing-playbook.md) — conversion, repurposing and campaign deployment system
- [`04-publishing-calendar.md`](04-publishing-calendar.md) — initial twelve-article launch and twelve-week continuation plan
- [`articles/`](articles/) — complete article drafts with production metadata

## Publication status

The initial twelve articles are complete editorial drafts. They are marked `ready_for_editorial_review`, not automatically published. Before production publication, the owner must confirm:

1. product names, URLs, availability, sizes and instructions against the live store;
2. every formulation, ingredient, performance and authority claim;
3. image rights, celebrity references and customer evidence;
4. legal and regulatory wording for the target market;
5. final canonical URL, schema and internal links in the live CMS.

No article may imply that self-tan provides sun protection. Product directions and on-pack warnings override editorial guidance.

## Non-negotiable editorial rule

Articles may answer broad tanning questions, but every commercial recommendation must route to an appropriate Jimmy Coco product, tool, bundle or professional solution. Do not publish competitor round-ups, “best fake tan brands” lists or disguised comparison pages in this hub.
