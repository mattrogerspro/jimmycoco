# Scene 11 — Final Conversion Scene

## Role in the experience

This is the second emotional peak of the homepage and the final major commercial decision point. It must convert accumulated trust, understanding, proof and desire into one confident action.

It follows the bright, instructional routine chapter and must create a decisive cinematic shift in tone.

## Commercial objective

Drive visitors into either the personalised shade-matching journey or a curated bestseller route before the page transitions into lower-priority community and footer content.

## User questions answered

- What should I do now?
- Can the brand help me choose confidently?
- Is there a direct route if I already know what I want?
- Does the experience end with reassurance rather than pressure?

## Core message

> Your most natural tan starts with the right match.

Supporting statement:

> Discover the formula Jimmy would recommend for your skin tone, desired depth and experience level.

## Architectural silhouette

The scene must feel cinematic, dark and memorable.

Suggested silhouette:

- Full-width deep warm charcoal environment
- Large product still life occupying one side or the centre-right
- Large editorial headline with controlled line length
- One dominant CTA and one quieter secondary action
- Small trust or reassurance line beneath
- Intentional darkness and negative space
- Optional Jimmy signature or subtle founder endorsement

This scene must not resemble a dark footer. It is a standalone campaign-quality conversion moment.

## Composition direction

- Use a 12-column grid but allow the product still life to exceed the content grid.
- Anchor copy across approximately columns 2–6.
- Let the principal product arrangement occupy approximately columns 7–12.
- Use strong depth through light, shadow and material contrast.
- Keep the composition asymmetrical and calm.
- Avoid placing all products in one straight catalogue row.
- Preserve a generous clean area around the CTA.

## Product still-life direction

The still life should feel like luxury fragrance or beauty campaign photography.

Use:

- One hero bottle
- One or two supporting products only
- A premium tanning mitt or controlled textural element
- Warm spotlight or narrow window-like illumination
- Deep grounded shadows
- Matte and satin material contrast
- Subtle stone, smoked glass or dark linen surface

Do not use:

- Excessive props
- Water splashes
- Flowers or leaves without relevance
- Glitter
- Bright gold pedestals
- Unrealistic floating products
- Dense product line-ups

## Lighting direction

- Deep charcoal base with warm directional light
- Controlled bronze highlights
- Product labels legible through selective illumination
- Natural fall-off into shadow
- No crushed blacks that erase product geometry
- No orange colour cast
- No nightclub neon
- No theatrical smoke

The lighting should feel expensive, quiet and tactile.

## CTA hierarchy

### Primary

**MATCH MY SHADE**

Supporting reassurance, where operationally accurate:

- Takes 60 seconds
- No email required
- Personal recommendation

### Secondary

**Shop Best Sellers**

The secondary route should be outlined, text-led or lower contrast.

## Trust close

Use one concise proof line beneath the actions, such as:

> 4.8/5 from 1,642 verified customers

Optional supporting phrase:

> Created by celebrity tanning artist Jimmy Coco.

Use only verified data in production.

## Emotional objective

The visitor should feel:

- Calm confidence
- Desire
- Personal relevance
- Relief from choice uncertainty
- Premium value
- Readiness to act

Avoid false urgency, aggressive sales pressure or discount-led messaging.

## Typography

- Large editorial serif headline at approximately 52–64px
- Maximum three lines
- Supporting copy at approximately 17–19px
- Strong cream-on-charcoal contrast
- CTA typography firm and clear
- Optional signature used sparingly as an authentic accent

## Eye-tracking path

1. Illuminated hero product
2. Emotional headline
3. Primary CTA
4. Supporting statement
5. Secondary CTA
6. Trust line

Depending on composition, headline and product may exchange first fixation, but the CTA must remain unmistakable.

## Conversion psychology

- **Peak-End Rule:** creates a memorable final emotional peak.
- **Goal completion:** presents the clearest next action after the full narrative.
- **Choice architecture:** one primary and one secondary route only.
- **Authority:** Jimmy’s recommendation frames the shade match as expert guidance.
- **Risk reduction:** effort and privacy reassurance reduce CTA anxiety.
- **Von Restorff effect:** the dark environment separates the final decision from the pale page above.

## Accessibility and implementation notes

- Ensure cream text and CTA states meet contrast requirements.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus against the dark background.
- Do not place text over complex product reflections.
- Maintain large touch targets.
- Ensure the scene remains readable when images are disabled.
- On mobile, stack copy above product still life or vice versa without losing CTA visibility.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences if product lighting or parallax is animated.

## Common AI failure modes

Reject or regenerate if the model:

- Makes the chapter look like a footer
- Uses a flat dark rectangle with products pasted on top
- Adds too many bottles
- Creates orange or copper colour casts across the whole scene
- Uses nightclub lighting, smoke or neon
- Hides product labels in black shadow
- Adds promotional discount bursts
- Makes the CTA too small
- Adds multiple competing buttons
- Uses glossy black marble clichés
- Distorts packaging or Jimmy’s signature
- Fails to create a strong visual break from the white routine scene

## Success criteria

The scene succeeds when:

- It is unmistakably the homepage’s second emotional peak
- The visitor immediately knows the next action
- Product photography feels campaign-quality
- The dark palette feels warm and luxurious, not gloomy
- The primary CTA dominates without feeling aggressive
- The scene is clearly separate from the footer below
- The experience ends commercially before utility content begins
