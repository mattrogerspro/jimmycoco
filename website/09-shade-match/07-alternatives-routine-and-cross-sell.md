# 07 — Alternatives, Routine and Cross-Sell

## Purpose

Support customers who want a different trade-off and increase average order value without undermining confidence in the primary recommendation.

## Alternative recommendation

Offer one alternative only. It must differ meaningfully from the primary result, for example:

- more gradual and controllable;
- faster development;
- deeper result;
- face-specific rather than combined use;
- or simpler for a first-time customer.

Use explicit comparison copy:

> Prefer a slower, more controllable build? Try [alternative].

Do not present a second “best match” or a broad product carousel.

## Alternative card contents

- Product and selected variant
- One-sentence trade-off
- Price
- View details
- Optional Add to Bag where selection is complete

The alternative must be visually quieter than the primary recommendation.

## Routine architecture

Use the existing Prepare, Apply, Perfect model.

### Prepare

Recommend only where preparation meaningfully supports the result, such as exfoliation or hydration guidance supported by the product instructions.

### Apply

Include the correct mitt, brush or applicator where needed. Make the functional reason explicit.

### Perfect

Offer a finishing or maintenance item only when it genuinely complements the recommended result.

## Cross-sell rules

- Maximum one item per routine stage.
- Never preselect optional products.
- Show individual prices and updated total.
- Explain function, not generic lifestyle benefit.
- Do not hide the primary Add to Bag below a bundle builder.
- Do not imply that the recommended hero product will fail without every accessory.
- Respect product compatibility and stock.

## Bundle action

An **Add Complete Routine** action may be offered only after the user can inspect all included items. It should never replace the primary product purchase action.

## Personalisation limits

Routine recommendations may use application area, experience and main concern. Do not imply a medical or dermatological assessment.

## Real-results handoff

Link to a pre-filtered results view using:

- recommended product;
- selected depth;
- typical number of coats;
- relevant skin-depth range;
- development timing.

Always disclose whether images are customer-submitted, professionally photographed or illustrative.

## Success criteria

Customers retain confidence in the primary match, understand the alternative trade-off and can add only the routine items that have a clear functional role.
