# Email 03 — Proof and Routine Context

## Role

Reinforce confidence by connecting the customer’s selected products to credible results, verified reviews and the routine they support.

## Default timing

Send approximately 48 hours after sequence entry when the cart remains active and no purchase or higher-priority state exists.

## Primary objective

Show why the selected cart makes sense in practice without adding pressure or unrelated recommendations.

## Proof hierarchy

Use the strongest approved evidence connected to the live cart:

1. verified result using a cart product;
2. verified customer review for a cart product;
3. approved professional application guidance;
4. a concise routine explanation showing how the selected products work together;
5. current aggregate review data when substantiated.

## Relevance requirements

Proof must connect directly to at least one cart item or to the complete routine represented by the cart. Do not use unrelated celebrity or customer imagery merely for attention.

Where a before-and-after result is used, provide the available context: product, variant, application count, development time and image-standard disclosure.

## Routine logic

When multiple products form a valid routine, explain their roles in order:

- prepare;
- apply;
- blend or contour;
- develop;
- maintain.

Do not imply that every accessory is mandatory. Distinguish essential products from optional enhancements.

## Message hierarchy

1. Confidence-led headline
2. Compact live cart reminder
3. One dominant proof or routine story
4. One primary cart-recovery CTA
5. Optional link to full application guidance or results

## Subject-line territories

- See how your Sunless routine comes together
- The result behind your selection
- Real results from the products in your cart
- A closer look before you decide

Avoid guarantees, exaggerated transformations and unsupported claims.

## Asset integrity

- Use only approved original product, customer and celebrity source files.
- Never generatively alter people, packaging, skin colour, product colour or labels.
- Do not retouch before-and-after evidence.
- Use descriptive alt text and live-text captions so the proof remains meaningful when images are blocked.

## CTA

Use `Return to my cart` or `Continue checkout`. Keep any results-gallery or tutorial link visually secondary.

## Validation

Refresh the live cart and verify proof provenance, usage rights, review wording, product association, stock, price, currency, discounts, URLs and consent immediately before send.

## Success criteria

Track restored-cart visits, proof-detail views, checkout starts and purchases. Evaluate whether the proof module increases incremental conversion without increasing complaints or unsubscribes.
