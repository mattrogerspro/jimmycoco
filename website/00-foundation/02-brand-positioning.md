# 02 — Website Brand Positioning

## Purpose

This document defines how the Sunless website should position the brand in the customer’s mind. It translates the shared brand DNA into a clear commercial position for ecommerce.

## Positioning statement

Sunless by Jimmy Coco is the premium self-tanning system that gives customers access to the judgement, method and colour control of a Hollywood celebrity tanning artist at home.

The brand should not compete primarily on price, trendiness or product count. It should compete on:

- Professional authority
- Natural-looking results
- Undertone awareness
- Controlled, buildable colour
- Guided product selection
- Confidence in application
- Premium but approachable experience

## Category context

The self-tan category is crowded with brands that often rely on:

- Influencer endorsement
- Generic “glow” language
- One-tone product families
- Heavy discounting
- Aspirational beach imagery
- Before-and-after claims without context
- Product grids that assume the customer already knows what to buy

Sunless must not behave like another version of that category template.

The website should make the customer feel that Sunless understands the technical and emotional reasons self-tan goes wrong, then calmly removes those risks.

## Primary competitive advantage

The primary advantage is not celebrity association by itself.

It is the idea that Jimmy Coco’s professional judgement has been translated into:

- Product formulation
- Shade logic
- Undertone matching
- Layering guidance
- Application method
- Result prediction

The website must repeatedly connect Jimmy’s authority to product decisions. Founder content that does not explain a customer benefit is decorative and should be reduced.

## Customer perception target

After five minutes on the website, the customer should think:

> This brand understands colour, skin and application more deeply than the alternatives, and it can help me choose confidently.

The intended perception is:

- More expert than influencer-led brands
- More human than clinical skincare brands
- More premium than mass-market self-tan
- More useful than luxury brands that rely only on image
- More trustworthy than discount-led ecommerce competitors

## Value hierarchy

### 1. Emotional value

Confidence that the result will look natural and flattering.

### 2. Functional value

A clearer recommendation, controllable colour and professional application logic.

### 3. Symbolic value

Access to Hollywood-level expertise and a polished, aspirational beauty identity.

### 4. Commercial value

Reduced risk of buying the wrong shade, wasting product or avoiding self-tan altogether.

## Positioning pillars

### Professional authority

Jimmy’s credibility should be visible, specific and relevant. Use backstage imagery, method explanation, client context and direct links between professional practice and product design.

Avoid vague authority signals such as “celebrity favourite” without proof or explanation.

### Natural-looking colour

The website should explain what natural-looking means:

- Undertone-aware
- Buildable
- Controlled depth
- Even development
- Believable in daylight
- Designed to avoid flat, orange or one-dimensional results

### Personal guidance

The website should help the customer decide. It should not merely display products.

Guidance should appear through:

- Shade matching
- Product suitability labels
- Skin-tone examples
- Development time
- Number of coats
- Result depth
- Experience-level recommendations

### Luxury through restraint

Premium perception should come from composition, typography, image quality, spacing and confidence—not excess decoration.

### Proof before pressure

The website should prove the method and results before asking the customer to make a high-friction choice.

## Positioning by visitor type

### First-time tanner

Message:

> You do not need to guess. We will guide you to a natural result and show you exactly how to apply it.

### Experienced self-tan customer

Message:

> Get greater control, undertone accuracy and a more professional finish.

### Event-driven customer

Message:

> Choose the right depth and development time with clear preparation and application guidance.

### Premium beauty customer

Message:

> A refined, expert-led system that justifies its price through confidence, precision and result quality.

### Professional customer

Message:

> Access Jimmy’s method, professional products and business-relevant support without weakening the consumer journey.

## Claims discipline

The website should favour specific, supportable claims.

Prefer:

> Designed to build colour in controllable layers.

Over:

> The world’s most advanced tanning technology.

Prefer:

> Matched to your desired depth, skin tone and experience level.

Over:

> Your perfect glow, guaranteed.

Avoid absolute statements unless verified operationally and legally.

## Pricing posture

The design should support premium pricing through:

- Founder expertise
- Product specificity
- High-quality proof
- Guided selection
- Application education
- Premium imagery
- Calm confidence

Do not rely on constant discounting to make products feel attractive. Promotional offers should never dominate the homepage identity.

## What the website must not communicate

- “This is mainly a celebrity merchandise brand.”
- “All products are basically the same shade in different packaging.”
- “You need to be an expert to use this.”
- “The brand is expensive only because it looks luxurious.”
- “The customer must browse everything before understanding what to buy.”
- “The result depends on luck.”

## Positioning test

A successful homepage makes the difference between Sunless and a generic self-tan brand understandable without reading every section.

If the customer remembers only the visual beauty but cannot explain why Sunless should create a better result, the positioning has failed.
