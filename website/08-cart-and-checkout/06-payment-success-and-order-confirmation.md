# Payment Success and Order Confirmation

## Purpose

End the purchase journey with certainty, reassurance and a clear understanding of what happens next.

## Immediate success state

The page must confirm success unambiguously before any secondary content.

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Success icon or restrained confirmation mark
2. **Thank you — your order is confirmed**
3. Order reference
4. Confirmation-email message
5. Delivery summary
6. Ordered items and total
7. Next-step guidance
8. Optional account or routine content

Do not lead with an upsell, newsletter form or celebratory campaign graphic.

## Required information

Show:

- order reference;
- customer email used for confirmation;
- delivery address;
- selected delivery method;
- estimated delivery date or range;
- ordered products, variants and quantities;
- payment total;
- support route;
- tracking expectation.

Sensitive payment details must remain masked.

## Confirmation copy

Use precise language:

> We have received your order and sent confirmation to [email].

> We will send another email when your order is dispatched.

Only state actions that the operational system actually performs.

## Account creation

For guest customers, account creation may be offered after the order has been confirmed.

Frame it as convenience:

> Save your details and view this order more easily next time.

Do not make customers believe an account is required to receive the order.

## Post-purchase guidance

A small application-preparation module may be useful, especially when delivery creates a waiting period. It should be relevant to the purchased product and contain no unverified instructions.

Examples:

- preparation reminder;
- application guide link;
- product-specific development-time reminder;
- shade-match result reference.

## Cross-sell restraint

Do not present a conventional product carousel immediately after payment. Post-purchase selling must never weaken the sense that the transaction is complete.

## Failure boundary

Never show the confirmation page unless the order state is confirmed. If payment status is uncertain, use a dedicated pending state that explains:

- that confirmation is still being checked;
- whether payment may have been authorised;
- that the customer should not resubmit;
- how they will be updated;
- how to contact support.

## Email confirmation consistency

The on-site confirmation and transactional email must agree on:

- order reference;
- products;
- totals;
- delivery address;
- delivery estimate;
- support information.

## Visual direction

- Calm white or warm ivory field
- Generous but controlled whitespace
- One refined success mark
- Clear serif confirmation headline
- Operational detail in sans-serif
- No confetti, flashing animation or oversized promotional photography

## Accessibility

Move focus to the confirmation heading after navigation. Announce success clearly. Ensure the order reference can be selected and copied. Do not encode status through colour alone.

## Success criteria

The customer leaves knowing the order succeeded, where confirmation was sent, what they purchased, when it should arrive and what to do if anything appears incorrect.
