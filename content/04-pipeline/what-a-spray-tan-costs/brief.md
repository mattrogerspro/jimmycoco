## What she gets in the first hundred words

At £30 a tan, the solution costs £2.14 and the total consumables £3.24. The full
loaded cost is around £18.70 with an employed therapist, leaving roughly £11.30
of profit — and solution price is the smallest lever in the whole model.

## Why us, why now

Nothing in the UK answers this with a number in it. The incumbent for the
adjacent commercial query — Sienna X's pricing page — is dated October 2017,
runs ~450 words and contains one figure. Everything else that ranks is either
SalonGeek threads from 2011 or US calculators in dollars.

The one UK page carrying real £ figures ranks #1 for several queries. That is
the entire proof of concept, and it is not a hard page to beat.

Timing: costs changed materially in April 2026 — National Living Wage to £12.71,
employer NIC secondary threshold cut to £5,000, business rates overhauled with
salons excluded from the relief pubs received. 72.6% of the sector expected to
raise prices within three months. She is doing this arithmetic right now.
