# Email 01 — Welcome to VIP

## Role

Recognise the customer’s new status, explain what changes and establish the tone of the programme.

## Trigger

Send after qualification and validation are complete. Do not send while a complaint, refund, chargeback, safety concern or account investigation is active.

## Primary objective

Make the customer feel recognised and clearly explain the benefits now available.

## Message hierarchy

1. Warm recognition
2. Plain explanation of status
3. Two or three real benefits
4. One primary next action
5. Preference and support access

## Subject-line territories

- Welcome to Sunless VIP
- A thank-you from Sunless
- Your Sunless VIP access begins here
- More from your Sunless experience

Avoid language that reveals or dramatises spend.

## Core copy principles

- thank the customer without overfamiliarity;
- explain status in one sentence;
- describe only active, deliverable benefits;
- avoid implying permanent status when review rules apply;
- do not lead with a discount unless the programme is explicitly structured around one.

## Recommended structure

1. Brand header
2. Editorial thank-you
3. VIP status statement
4. Benefit summary
5. Primary CTA: `Explore your VIP benefits`
6. Optional expert-support link
7. Preference and footer modules

## Personalisation

Safe fields may include:

- first name when reliable;
- tier name;
- qualification date;
- available benefits;
- relevant prior product family;
- verified professional status.

Do not mention exact spend, order count or internal score unless the programme explicitly publishes those rules.

## Asset rules

Use refined brand or approved product imagery. Customer, celebrity and product source assets must not be generatively altered.

## Pre-send validation

Confirm:

- current tier and programme version;
- benefit availability;
- consent and suppression state;
- no unresolved service risk;
- links and preference centre;
- correct market and terms;
- no previous welcome send for the same qualification period;
- idempotency key is unused.

## Success criteria

Measure benefit-page visits, preference completion, expert-support engagement, subsequent retained purchase and negative signals. Opens alone must not define success.
