# Writing subjects and previews

The full system lives in `email/04-copy-system/02-subject-line-system.md`, and the branded report at the end of this guide list walks the whole flow. This is the working summary.

## The primary rule

**The subject line must accurately represent the message that follows.** A subject succeeds only when it attracts the right open — not the most opens.

## The seven approved patterns

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| Direct value | "Your shade recommendation is ready" |
| Useful guidance | "How to prepare for an even tan" |
| Relevant reminder | "Time to refresh your colour?" |
| Specific product context | "A closer look at Malibu Beach" |
| Service update | "Your order has been dispatched" |
| Recognition | "Welcome to Sunless VIP" |
| Resolution | "We've updated your delivery" |

## Working rules

- Aim for roughly **25–50 visible characters**; put the most important words first — inboxes truncate unpredictably.
- Sentence case. No ALL CAPS, repeated punctuation, decorative symbols or emoji strings.
- Personalise only when the data is reliable and a natural fallback exists: "Matt, your shade match is ready" must degrade cleanly to "Your shade match is ready".
- Never fake a **RE:** or **FWD:** to simulate a conversation.
- Curiosity is fine with context ("The step most tanning routines miss"); bait is not ("You won't believe this").
- Urgency only when the deadline, stock state or expiry is real, current and recorded.

## The preview is half the proposition

Preview text must **add** information, never repeat the subject. The first 35–60 characters must survive truncation.

> Subject: "Your shade recommendation is ready"
> Preview: "See the depth, undertone and routine selected from your answers."

## Before you call it done

The line matches the content · important words come early · urgency is real · personalisation has a fallback · no surveillance language · no fake reply convention · understandable when truncated · the preview adds something new · service messages stay plain · **you could defend it as honest after the campaign ends.**
