Why your tan goes patchy on day three

Hi {{ first_name|default:'there' }},

Nine times out of ten, when someone tells me their tan has gone blotchy by midweek, the tan isn’t the problem. Something happened before it ever went on.

It’s almost always one of these three. Here’s how I fix all three →

One

You exfoliated on the day, not the day before

Freshly scrubbed skin is still open and slightly damp, and it grabs colour unevenly. Give it a full 24 hours to settle. And avoid oil-based scrubs the night before — the residue sits on the skin and the tan can’t grip it.
What I prep clients with →

Two

You moisturised everywhere instead of nowhere

Barrier cream is for the dry, bony bits only: knuckles, wrists, elbows, knees, ankles and heels. All over the body it thins the colour, and that’s the streaky, faded look that turns up around day three.
Where to barrier-cream, exactly →

Three

Your face and your body are on different clocks

Facial skin renews faster than the skin on your body, so the face fades first and the join along the jaw starts to show. Treat the face as its own job and top it up midweek, rather than re-doing everything.
Keep face and body matched →
