# Email 02 — What Is New and Useful

## Role

Show the customer a genuinely useful reason to reconsider Sunless now.

The email should focus on material improvements, relevant new products, easier routines, better guidance or stronger proof. It must not use “new” as empty decoration.

## Default timing

Send approximately 4–5 days after Email 01 when the customer remains eligible and has not meaningfully reactivated.

## Primary objective

Create renewed relevance by connecting current brand value to the customer’s known history or likely need.

## Approved content routes

Choose one dominant route:

- a new product that meaningfully fits the customer’s previous category;
- an improved application or preparation method;
- a simpler routine;
- updated shade-match guidance;
- new verified results or education;
- an approved successor to a discontinued product;
- an easier reorder or product-comparison experience.

Do not present routine catalogue items as new.

## Message hierarchy

1. Clear statement of what has changed
2. Why it may matter to this customer
3. One product, routine or guidance module
4. One primary CTA
5. Optional secondary link to compare or retake shade match

## Subject-line territories

- A simpler way to find your colour
- What is new at Sunless
- Your routine may have become easier
- A fresh approach to your next result

Avoid unverified superlatives and vague claims such as “our best ever” without substantiation.

## Personalisation rules

Reliable uses include:

- previous product family;
- prior shade-match recommendation;
- face, body or professional category explicitly purchased;
- repeat-purchase history;
- known product discontinuation or successor mapping.

Do not infer that a previous product failed or no longer suits the customer unless they explicitly said so.

## Product and claim validation

Immediately before send, confirm:

- launch or update is still current;
- product is available in the recipient’s market;
- price, currency, stock and variants are accurate;
- comparison claims are approved;
- destination URL resolves;
- all imagery is approved and unaltered.

## Visual structure

1. Header
2. Editorial change statement
3. Dominant product, comparison or guidance module
4. Concise expert explanation
5. Primary CTA
6. Footer

## CTA examples

- `See what is new`
- `Explore the easier routine`
- `Compare the options`
- `Find my match again`

## Success criteria

Measure qualified return sessions, product and comparison engagement, shade-match completion, cart creation and purchase. Treat unsubscribes and complaints as important quality signals, not merely deliverability metrics.
