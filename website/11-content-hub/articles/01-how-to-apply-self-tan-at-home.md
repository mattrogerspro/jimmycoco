# How to Apply Self-Tan at Home: The Jimmy Coco Method

The most reliable at-home tan comes from four controlled stages: prepare the skin, apply the main body areas evenly, use less product around joints and edges, then allow the formula to develop for the stated time before rinsing. The aim is not to make every area equally dark. It is to create one balanced, believable finish.

Jimmy Coco’s at-home system makes each stage clear. [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) provides a visible guide colour and a buildable bronze result; the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) distributes it across larger areas; and [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush) gives greater control around hands, feet and other edges.

## What you need

- Your chosen shade of Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé
- A clean, dry Buff & Glow Mitt
- The Body Brush for precision blending, if desired
- Loose, dark clothing for the development period
- Time to follow the directions without rushing

Do not begin with shade anxiety. If you are undecided, read [Malibu Medium or Dark?](/blogs/jimmy-coco-method/malibu-medium-or-dark) before opening the bottle.

## Step 1: prepare a smooth, clean base

Complete exfoliation and any hair removal well before application so the skin has time to settle. Pay particular attention to areas where dry skin can hold more colour: elbows, knees, ankles, knuckles and the edges of the feet.

On application day, the skin should be clean and fully dry. Avoid leaving an all-over layer of oil, deodorant or rich product that could interrupt an even application. If a dry area needs a small amount of moisturiser, keep it targeted and allow it to absorb.

This preparation is not about scrubbing the skin aggressively. It is about removing the uneven surface that can make the same amount of tan look different from one area to the next.

## Step 2: start with the broad body areas

Shake and use the Soufflé as directed. Place product onto the mitt and work one body section at a time. Begin with the legs, then move to the torso and arms. Use long, overlapping sweeps and keep the product moving until the guide colour looks even.

The guide colour is your map. Look for pale gaps, heavy borders or a sudden change at the wrist, ankle or side of the body. Correct these while the application is still fresh instead of adding another full layer everywhere.

Keep your hand relaxed inside the mitt. Heavy pressure can push too much product into one area; a controlled, gliding motion is easier to blend.

## Step 3: use restraint on hands, feet and joints

Hands, feet, knees and elbows reveal an at-home tan first because their shape, movement and dryness make them less forgiving. Finish these areas after the main body.

Use the small amount of product remaining on the mitt, or blend with The Body Brush. Sweep lightly over the tops of the hands and feet, soften the transition at the wrists and ankles, and blend around—not into—the nails. Bend elbows, knees and fingers as you work so no hard line is hidden inside a crease.

For a detailed technique, use the guide to [tanning hands, feet, knees and elbows](/blogs/jimmy-coco-method/how-to-tan-hands-feet-knees-elbows).

## Step 4: allow the colour to develop

Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé is currently directed to develop for 6–8 hours. During that time, avoid water, perspiration and close-fitting clothing that can disturb the guide colour. Follow the directions on your product, as timing can change by formula or market.

When the development period is complete, rinse as directed. The temporary guide colour will wash away; judge the developed result only after the rinse and once the skin is dry.

## Step 5: build depth with a second considered application

Malibu is buildable. If you want greater depth, assess the fully developed first application before deciding to add more. A second controlled application is more predictable than making the first layer excessively heavy.

Buildability also lets you keep the face, hands and feet slightly softer than the main body. Professional-looking colour has variation; it should not resemble one opaque shade from shoulder to fingertip.

## Five mistakes that make self-tan harder

1. **Rushing preparation.** Uneven texture makes even application look uneven.
2. **Starting with hands or feet.** These areas need the least product and should come last.
3. **Adding more instead of blending.** A visible edge usually needs diffusion, not another pump.
4. **Ignoring the guide colour.** Use it to find gaps before the formula develops.
5. **Judging before the rinse.** The guide is not the final colour.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I use a mitt or a brush?

Use the Buff & Glow Mitt for efficient, even coverage across large body areas. Use The Body Brush when you want more precision around hands, feet and blend edges. Many routines benefit from both rather than forcing one tool to do every job.

### Can I apply another coat immediately?

Follow the product directions. For the most controlled result at home, allow the first application to develop and be rinsed before deciding whether you need more depth.

### Does self-tan protect my skin from the sun?

No. A cosmetic tan does not replace sunscreen or other sun-protection measures.
