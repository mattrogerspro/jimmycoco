# 04 — Before-and-After Presentation

## Purpose

Before-and-after imagery is the most persuasive evidence in the gallery and the area most vulnerable to loss of trust. Presentation must make comparison easy without overstating what the product achieved.

## Core comparison rule

The before and after images should be as comparable as reasonably possible in:

- camera angle;
- subject distance;
- body area shown;
- crop;
- lighting direction;
- colour temperature;
- background;
- exposure;
- pose.

Where they are not directly comparable, state this clearly rather than creating a misleading visual alignment.

## Approved layouts

### Side-by-side

Default format. Best for immediate inspection and accessible labelling.

### Controlled slider

Optional on detail pages only. Always provide visible labels and a static fallback. Never make the slider the only way to understand the result.

### Sequential stack

Useful on narrow mobile screens when side-by-side images become too small. Show Before first, After second, with matching crop dimensions.

## Image integrity

Do not:

- retouch skin tone differently between images;
- alter the hue, saturation or warmth of only one image;
- use beauty filters;
- smooth away texture;
- change body shape;
- remove marks, freckles or natural texture;
- use an AI-generated before or after as customer evidence;
- imply simultaneous capture when images were taken under materially different conditions.

Basic global corrections may be used only to represent the source faithfully and consistently.

## Labelling

Every comparison must visibly identify:

- Before
- After
- Development time or capture timing
- Product and shade used
- Number of coats

Where relevant, add:

- Natural daylight
- Indoor light
- Flash photography
- Professional studio lighting

## Crop discipline

Use a crop that shows the area where colour difference is meaningful. Avoid crops that conceal unevenness or omit the starting condition.

Faces must not be cropped so tightly that natural context disappears. Body images must remain tasteful, brand-appropriate and consented.

## Colour management

- Preserve realistic neutral whites where present.
- Avoid a warm grade applied only to the after image.
- Do not force all results into one bronze hue.
- Retain variation across skin tones and products.
- Export in a colour profile appropriate for modern web browsers.

## Caption template

> Medium neutral starting tone · Tinted Tan Soufflé in Medium · one coat · developed for 6 hours · photographed in indirect daylight.

Use only factually supported details.

## Disclosures

A concise disclosure should be available near the comparison:

> Results vary by starting tone, preparation, application, development time and lighting.

Do not use the disclosure to excuse poor or misleading source imagery.

## AI production rule

AI may be used to design the surrounding interface concept, but not to recreate, retouch or invent customer proof images. Original result photography must be inserted as an unchanged asset during final composition.

## Success criteria

The comparison succeeds when the viewer can inspect the difference easily and has no reason to suspect that presentation choices created the result.
