## Publishing order rules

1. Foundation articles must be live before supporting articles that depend on them.
2. Do not publish a product-led article when its primary product is unavailable without an approved fallback.
3. A professional article must state whether access, delivery or purchase is trade-only where applicable.
4. Add reciprocal internal links when a new supporting article is published.
5. Refresh the four launch pillars after the first 28 days using actual query and behaviour data.

## Launch acceptance criteria

- All twelve initial articles pass editorial and claims review.
- Product links resolve in the target market.
- Article, breadcrumb and visible FAQ data agree.
- Mobile layouts preserve tables and numbered steps.
- Each article has one approved hero and at least two useful instructional assets.
- Analytics capture article view, product click, shade-selector start and professional CTA events.
- XML sitemap and content-hub navigation expose every canonical article.
