# Final AI Collection Page Prompt

Use this prompt to generate a complete desktop collection-page concept after loading the shared brand documentation, website design system, collection-page specifications and approved product references.

## Required references

Attach:

- current Sunless website screenshot;
- approved logo and navigation references;
- approved product-packaging references for every visible SKU;
- approved product photography;
- approved model or result imagery if used;
- relevant collection-page documents;
- shared colour, typography and photography guidance.

## Master prompt

Create one ultra-premium 1700px-wide desktop ecommerce collection page for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**. Use the attached current website as a strict identity reference. This is an evolution, not a redesign. Preserve approximately 95% of the existing visual language: warm ivory, linen, stone and champagne tones; elegant editorial serif headlines; refined sans-serif interface text; matte-black primary actions; natural skin photography; premium beauty restraint; generous but controlled negative space.

Design a commercially effective collection page for **[COLLECTION NAME]** containing **[NUMBER OF PRODUCTS]** products. The page must feel curated, expert-led and luxurious, but products must remain visible, comparable and easy to purchase. Do not create a campaign landing page that hides the product grid.

At the top, include the existing global navigation, a discreet breadcrumb, a clear collection title and a concise 30–55 word benefit-led introduction. Use an editorial split composition only if the attached collection imagery genuinely adds value. Keep the introduction compact enough that the product controls and beginning of the first product row appear within or immediately after the first viewport.

Add one calm guidance route near the introduction reading **“Help Me Choose”** or **“Match My Shade”**, depending on the real destination. Include product count, restrained filter controls and a clear sort control. Use customer-facing filters such as desired result, development time, format, face or body and experience level. Avoid exposing internal catalogue jargon.

Build a disciplined three- or four-column desktop product grid. Every product card must use accurate approved packaging and consistent image proportions. Each card must clearly display:

- product category or format;
- product name;
- one concise result descriptor;
- one suitability cue;
- verified review score and count where available;
- price;
- relevant variant or shade information;
- accurate availability;
- one clear action.

Use **“Add to Bag”** only for products with no unresolved required choice. Use **“Choose Options”** where a required variant must be selected. Keep essential information visible without hover. Optional hover may reveal a secondary product image or restrained quick-add control.

After the first or second product row, introduce one full-width editorial merchandising module that answers a real buying question, such as mousse versus gradual colour, choosing development time, Jimmy’s application method or a verified real-skin result. It should use one dominant image, one concise headline, short supporting copy and one link. It must not resemble a second hero or interrupt the first product row.

Continue the product grid below, followed by concise category education, internal links and delivery or returns reassurance. Keep SEO-supporting content below the commercial grid rather than placing a long essay above the products.

Use a clear invisible 12-column grid, strong alignment, consistent product-card heights and enough tonal contrast to prevent the page becoming one continuous beige block. The opening should feel editorial; the shopping area should feel precise and systematic. Maintain the same button, type, spacing, icon and photography language used throughout the approved Sunless website system.

The intended eye path is collection title → category promise → guidance route → first product row → filters and comparison cues → featured editorial guidance → additional products.

Do not use generic Shopify styling, glassmorphism, floating translucent cards, excessive badges, giant promotional banners, neon beauty colours, hard gradients, tiny grey text, oversized campaign imagery, masonry grids, inconsistent bottle proportions, invented product claims, fake reviews, unsupported discounts or an image-heavy composition that obscures shopping.

All skin colour must look natural and neutral, never orange. Product packaging must match the attached approved references exactly. Use photorealistic, production-ready imagery and believable ecommerce interface detail. The finished page should feel like a Vogue beauty editorial organised with Apple-level clarity and a commercially disciplined luxury retailer.

## Mobile companion prompt

Create a matching mobile collection-page concept at **390px width** using the same products, brand system and commercial hierarchy. Keep the category introduction shallow, show product count and Filter/Sort controls quickly, and use either a readable two-column grid or a richer one-column grid based on the actual card content. Do not shrink essential copy below comfortable reading size. Preserve filters, clear prices, availability and one-handed shopping behaviour. Avoid multiple stacked sticky bars and do not use hover-dependent interactions.

## Approval checks

Reject the output if:

- products are not visible early enough;
- cards do not explain product differences;
- packaging is inaccurate;
- the page looks like a homepage or campaign landing page;
- filters use internal jargon;
- quick add ignores required variants;
- the grid becomes visually repetitive or cluttered;
- fake claims, reviews or discounts appear;
- mobile text or controls are too small;
- the page no longer feels recognisably Sunless by Jimmy Coco.
