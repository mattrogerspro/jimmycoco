# 01 — Gallery Landing Page

## Role

The landing page introduces the proof system and helps visitors find transformations relevant to their own skin, desired depth and preferred product format.

## Hero

Use a restrained editorial opening rather than a large promotional campaign image.

### Suggested headline

> Real skin. Real colour. Real results.

### Supporting copy

> Explore verified Sunless results by starting skin tone, desired depth, product and development time.

### Primary action

**Find results like mine**

This action moves focus to the filters rather than opening another journey.

### Secondary action

**Match my shade**

## Hero composition

- Bright warm-white or pale linen field
- Narrow editorial copy block on the left
- One strong before-and-after transformation on the right
- Product used shown as a small grounded object, not a floating pack shot
- Generous negative space
- No carousel in the hero

## Featured transformation

Immediately below the filter bar, show one larger result selected for relevance, editorial quality or campaign priority.

Include:

- before and after images;
- starting skin profile;
- achieved depth;
- product and shade;
- coats and development time;
- short customer quote;
- `View full result` CTA.

Do not label a paid or selected feature as organic popularity unless supported by data.

## Results grid introduction

Use a concise heading such as:

> Explore every result

Optionally display the active result count and applied filter summary.

## Page rhythm

The landing page should move through:

Editorial openness → guided control → strong proof → dense discovery → reassurance → conversion.

Do not insert unrelated product carousels between every grid row.

## End-of-page module

After sufficient proof, offer:

### Heading

> Ready to find your result?

### Actions

- **Match My Shade**
- Shop Best Sellers

The shade-match route should remain visually dominant.

## Accessibility

- Before and after images require descriptive alt text.
- Filter state and result count must be announced to assistive technology.
- Image labels must not rely on position or colour alone.
- Avoid text embedded inside images.

## Success test

Within ten seconds, a visitor should understand that this is a structured, trustworthy transformation library and know how to narrow it to people like them.
