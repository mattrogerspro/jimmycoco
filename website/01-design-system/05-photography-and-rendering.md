# 05 — Photography and Product Rendering

## Purpose

This document defines the image language for the Sunless website. Photography must create desire while preserving credibility, skin realism and Jimmy Coco’s professional authority.

The website should never rely on generic luxury imagery. Every image must explain, prove or deepen the brand story.

## Core photographic idea

Hollywood expertise in natural light.

The visual world combines:

- Backstage professional access
- Intimate private-suite luxury
- Real, dimensional skin
- Controlled editorial composition
- Warm, tactile materials
- Product accuracy
- Believable at-home attainability

## Skin direction

Skin is the product result and must be treated with exceptional care.

Approved qualities:

- Warm but not orange
- Natural pores and texture retained
- Dimensional highlights
- Believable tonal variation
- Realistic knees, elbows, hands and shoulders where visible
- Healthy, polished finish
- Consistent colour logic across before-and-after images

Avoid:

- Plastic retouching
- Smoothed or airbrushed limbs
- Uniform brown colour
- Excessive oil or wet-look shine
- Artificial HDR
- Skin that appears digitally painted
- Before images deliberately made dull or unflattering

## Lighting language

Primary lighting:

- Soft directional window light
- Diffused daylight
- Gentle warm bounce
- Controlled shadows
- Natural highlight roll-off

Secondary lighting for dark scenes:

- Warm spotlight
- Soft side light
- Restrained specular highlights on packaging
- Deep but readable shadows

Avoid:

- Nightclub lighting
- Neon colour casts
- Hard direct flash as the dominant style
- Overly golden grading
- Flat catalogue lighting in emotional scenes

## Environmental materials

Approved:

- Warm limestone
- Plaster
- Linen
- Travertine
- Brushed metal
- Matte painted surfaces
- Cream upholstery
- Dark timber used sparingly
- Soft curtains
- Backstage mirrors and professional tools where relevant

The environment should feel expensive through material quality, not decoration.

## Model direction

Models should feel:

- Confident
- Relaxed
- Editorial rather than influencer-led
- Diverse in skin tone and age where proof requires it
- Believable as real customers or professional models depending on chapter

Poses should reveal the skin result naturally. Avoid exaggerated glamour posing, implausible body contortion or generic stock-photo laughter.

## Jimmy Coco direction

Jimmy should appear as an active professional, not a passive celebrity endorsement.

Approved actions:

- Applying product
- Assessing skin tone
- Preparing a client
- Working backstage
- Discussing a result
- Holding professional tools
- Reviewing colour in natural light

Jimmy’s appearance, clothing and facial identity must remain consistent with approved references.

Avoid:

- Random invented wardrobe
- Overly formal fashion poses
- Looking directly at camera in every scene
- Anatomically incorrect application
- Product usage that does not make sense

## Product rendering

Product accuracy is non-negotiable.

Every render must preserve:

- Bottle silhouette
- Cap and pump geometry
- Packaging material
- Label hierarchy
- Correct product colour
- Relative size
- Brand mark placement
- Surface finish

Approved render behaviour:

- Soft grounded shadows
- Realistic contact with surfaces
- Controlled reflections
- Slightly enlarged scale for hierarchy
- Occasional overlap with photography where composition requires it

Avoid:

- Impossible floating angles
- Melted or distorted labels
- Generic substitute packaging
- Excessive gloss
- Multiple bottles at inconsistent scales
- Heavy shadow halos

## Photography by chapter

### Hero

Cinematic layered scene with model, Jimmy and product in one coherent environment.

### Recommendation engine

Product-led studio or architectural still life, integrated into a premium interface workspace.

### Hollywood method

Close-up professional details, minimal line imagery or controlled application moments.

### Real results

Documentary proof. Natural daylight, consistent framing, clear skin context and limited styling.

### Best sellers

Clean product renders with restrained surface or floating presentation.

### Jimmy story

Large editorial portrait and authentic backstage reportage.

### Reviews

Human portraits with enough context to feel individual and relatable.

### Routine

Tactile product still life showing sequence and use.

### Final CTA

Luxury still life with dark environment and warm directional light.

## Before-and-after protocol

Where possible, show:

- Similar camera angle
- Similar focal length
- Similar pose
- Similar lighting
- Similar clothing exposure
- Product used
- Number of coats
- Development time
- Skin tone context
- Natural daylight or standardised-lighting note
- Retouching disclosure where applicable

Proof should feel forensic, not manipulated.

## Cropping rules

- Do not crop critical skin areas awkwardly
- Leave enough negative space for text where planned
- Keep faces away from browser or section edges
- Preserve product silhouette
- Avoid placing key details beneath likely UI overlays
- Use intentional partial crops only in editorial scenes

## Image hierarchy

Every scene should have one primary image role:

- Desire
- Authority
- Explanation
- Proof
- Product
- Belonging
- Closure

Do not ask one image to perform every role simultaneously.

## Colour grading

The grade should be:

- Warm-neutral
- Low saturation
- Skin accurate
- Softly cinematic
- Consistent in contrast curve

Different chapters may vary in luminosity and mood, but should remain recognisably part of the same photographic world.

## AI generation protocol

For each generated scene, attach:

- Current homepage reference
- Accurate product references
- Jimmy reference images where relevant
- Approved skin and lighting references
- Adjacent scene thumbnails when available

Prompt for composition before micro-detail. Rebuild typography separately.

## Common AI failures

Reject images that show:

- Extra fingers or malformed hands
- Product labels fused into the bottle
- Spray or mousse applied unrealistically
- Orange skin
- Inconsistent faces
- Generic spa interiors
- Excessive props
- Multiple unrelated light sources
- Impossible reflections
- Hyper-glossy beauty retouching
- Customer imagery that looks like fashion campaign proof rather than real results

## Success criteria

Photography succeeds when:

- Skin looks desirable and believable
- Jimmy’s authority is visible through action
- Products remain accurate
- Each image has a clear narrative role
- The page feels editorial without sacrificing trust
- Different scenes vary in composition but share one visual world
