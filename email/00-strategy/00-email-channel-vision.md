# Email Channel Vision

## Purpose

Email is the owned relationship layer of Sunless by Jimmy Coco. It must translate the same authority, warmth, precision and editorial luxury expressed on the website into a channel designed for guidance, retention and repeat purchase.

Email is not a discount feed. It is not a stream of generic beauty promotions. It is Jimmy's professional method delivered over time.

## Core promise

Every message should help the customer feel more confident about one of four things:

1. Choosing the right product
2. Applying it correctly
3. Achieving a believable result
4. Maintaining or repeating that result

## Strategic role

Email should:

- Reduce uncertainty before purchase
- Continue the shade-match journey
- Recover interrupted shopping intent
- Improve first-use success
- Build complete routines
- Increase replenishment and repeat purchase
- Strengthen Jimmy Coco's authority
- Create a direct customer relationship independent of paid platforms

## Experience principle

The inbox experience should feel like receiving a concise note from an expert beauty editor and professional tanning artist—not opening a mass-market campaign template.

Each email must have:

- One dominant idea
- One dominant action
- Clear relevance to the recipient's current state
- Enough proof or guidance to justify the action
- A calm visual hierarchy

## Channel personality

Email should be:

- Personal without pretending to be one-to-one when it is automated
- Expert without sounding clinical
- Premium without becoming cold
- Commercial without becoming aggressive
- Useful before decorative
- Concise without becoming vague

## Relationship arc

The long-term email relationship should progress through:

> Welcome → Guidance → Recommendation → Confidence → First success → Routine → Replenishment → Loyalty

## Non-negotiables

- No misleading urgency
- No fabricated scarcity
- No unsupported product claims
- No hidden email capture before promised value
- No excessive discount dependence
- No celebrity imagery altered by AI
- No generic template design that conflicts with the website system
- No email whose commercial purpose is unclear

## Definition of success

The email programme succeeds when customers increasingly rely on Sunless for judgement, not merely offers. The strongest signal is not only click-through rate, but repeat purchase driven by confidence, product fit and successful use.
