# Voice, truth and urgency — the guardrails

Three layers of guardrails constrain every line of Sunless copy: how we sound, what we may claim, and when pressure is legitimate. Full detail: `email/04-copy-system/01-brand-voice-for-email.md` and `10-promotional-claims-and-urgency-standards.md`.

## How Sunless sounds

A calm authority in self-tanning: expert without clinical, luxurious without ornamental, warm without over-familiar, **helpful before persuasive**. The central idea every message supports:

> Red-carpet colour. Made effortless at home.

## Vocabulary tiers

| Tier | Words |
|---|---|
| Preferred | colour · glow · finish · depth · undertone · development · routine · natural-looking · even · considered · professional · effortless · refined |
| Use carefully | flawless · perfect · transformative · iconic · exclusive · celebrity · red carpet · instant · guaranteed |
| Avoid unless legally approved | risk-free · miracle · clinically proven · chemical-free · zero streaks · no transfer · lasts X days · works for everyone |

Banned registers: "Hey babe", "Glow bestie", meme language, stacked exclamation marks. Use "I" only when a named person truthfully wrote the message — never simulate a personal note from Jimmy.

## Truth

- Never invent customer counts, awards, results, pricing, delivery times, stock, exclusivity or celebrity association.
- Celebrity and authority references need approval for the exact use, context, market and duration.
- "Selected", "exclusive" and "invited" require a real, documented selection process.
- Unknown facts stay as tokens — `{{approved_price}}`, `[SOURCE REQUIRED]` — and the campaign ships with its gaps listed, not papered over.

## Urgency

Legitimate only when backed by a real, recorded condition: a documented end time, verified inventory, a confirmed cutoff or a benefit expiry.

- Good: "Ends Sunday at 11:59 pm GMT." · "Order by 1 pm for the stated dispatch window."
- Prohibited: evergreen countdowns · "last chance" when an extension is planned · "only a few left" from stale data · "Hurry." · pressure via shame or fear.
- Surveillance language is banned outright: never "We saw you looking" or "Caught you browsing".
