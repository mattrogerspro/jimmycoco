## Email 2 — What to notice in the first result

**Preview:** Assess application, finish and service fit—without overcomplicating the test.  
**Primary CTA:** Email Matthew about the result.  
**Secondary reply:** `RESULT`.

Hi `{{FIRST_NAME}}`,

When you review a first professional result, it helps to separate the product from the whole service experience.

First, consider the application through your regular equipment. Then look at the developed finish in the conditions the client will care about: daylight, photographs and their own mirror. Finally, ask whether the consultation and finish feel natural within your current treatment menu.

Those are more useful questions than trying to decide everything from one number or one photograph. If you would like to talk through the result, reply `RESULT` and Matthew will pick it up personally.
