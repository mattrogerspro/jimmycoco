# Email 03 — How to Apply Your Product

## Role

Deliver the clearest product-specific application guidance at the moment the customer is most likely to need it.

## Default timing

Send on confirmed delivery or the estimated delivery day. If delivery is delayed or failed, pause and allow service communication to take priority.

## Primary objective

Help the customer apply the exact purchased product correctly on the first attempt.

## Message hierarchy

1. Product-specific application headline
2. Preparation confirmation
3. Step-by-step method
4. Quantity, coverage or placement guidance where approved
5. Development or wash-off timing
6. Troubleshooting and support link

## Instruction standards

Every instruction must map to an approved product instruction profile. Do not merge directions from similar-looking products.

The content should state, where applicable:

- how much product to use;
- where to begin;
- application order;
- blending method;
- tool use;
- treatment of hands, feet, elbows, knees, hairline or brows;
- number of coats;
- drying time;
- development time;
- rinse or wash-off process;
- cleaning and storage of tools.

## Format branches

- **Face or contour:** prioritise precision, symmetry, hairline and blending.
- **Body mousse or soufflé:** prioritise section order, even coverage and dry areas.
- **Gradual product:** prioritise buildability and repeated-use expectations.
- **Professional solution:** route to verified trade instructions and equipment guidance.
- **Brush or mitt:** explain compatible product use, technique and cleaning.

## Subject-line territories

- Your step-by-step Sunless method
- How to apply your product with confidence
- Your application guide, from start to finish
- The method behind an even result

## CTA

Primary CTA: `Open the full application guide`.

Video may be linked when approved, captioned and matched to the exact product. Essential directions must remain available in text.

## Visual rules

Use approved product and instructional assets. Do not generate or retouch skin results, alter the product container or fabricate application demonstrations.

## Error prevention

Give priority to the few mistakes that most materially affect the result. Avoid a long fear-based warning list. Use calm language and provide a clear support route.

## Validation

Confirm product, variant, instructions, delivery state, URL, consent and service state immediately before sending.

## Success signals

Application-guide engagement, video completion where available, fewer “how do I use this?” contacts, fewer preventable complaints and stronger result feedback.
