# 05 — Email 04: Real Results Like Yours

## Send timing

Approximately five days after completion, unless the customer has purchased, changed their result or entered a higher-priority recovery flow.

## Job to be done

Build belief through relevant, verified customer evidence rather than generic testimonials.

## Core message

> See how customers using the same product and a similar desired depth achieved their result.

## Primary CTA

**VIEW REAL RESULTS**

## Evidence selection hierarchy

Choose proof using the strongest available match in this order:

1. Same product and variant.
2. Same product and desired depth.
3. Same product and application format.
4. Same product with clearly disclosed differences.

Do not claim that a customer has the same skin tone, undertone or personal characteristics unless that data was explicitly captured, consented and approved for use.

## Content structure

1. Short proof-led headline.
2. Two or three approved result stories maximum.
3. For each result, show live-text metadata where available:
   - product and variant;
   - number of coats;
   - development time;
   - starting context;
   - lighting or image disclosure;
   - verified review excerpt.
4. Primary CTA to the filtered results gallery.
5. Quiet return-to-match link.

## Suggested subject lines

- See real results with your match
- What your recommended product can look like
- Real customers. Your matched product.

## Preview text

Verified results, product details and application context for your recommendation.

## Image integrity

Approved before-and-after, customer and celebrity images must be inserted as unchanged source assets. Do not use generative tools to recreate, retouch, reshape, recolour or improve a person’s face, body or skin result.

Permitted presentation changes are limited to approved responsive sizing and non-destructive layout cropping defined by the asset manifest. When a crop would remove material context, use the uncropped image.

## Claims and disclosure

- Results vary and must be presented as individual outcomes.
- Do not imply the featured outcome is guaranteed.
- Show relevant image, lighting or compensation disclosures.
- Review quotations must be verified and remain within approved usage rights.

## Design direction

Use an editorial proof layout rather than a review carousel screenshot. Keep metadata readable in live text and limit decorative UI. The result imagery should dominate without hiding the customer’s recommendation context.

## Fallback

If no sufficiently relevant verified proof exists, replace the email with a product-specific expert explanation or skip it. Never fill the gap with fabricated testimony or AI-generated customer results.

## Plain-text version

Summarise each result with product, coats, timing, review excerpt and the filtered gallery URL.
