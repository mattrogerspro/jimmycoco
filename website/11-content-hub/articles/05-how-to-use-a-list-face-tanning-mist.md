# How to Use the A-List Face Tanning Mist

Apply the A-List Face Tanning Mist to clean, prepared skin in a light, even layer, keep the hairline and brows tidy, then allow it to develop for 6–8 hours before rinsing with water only. Start with restraint: facial colour is easier to build than to soften after a heavy first application.

[The A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) is a dedicated facial tanner rather than a body formula transferred into a smaller bottle. Its ultra-fine mist is designed to create an even, buildable glow while fitting around a considered skincare and makeup routine.

## What makes the face different?

The face is more visible, washed more frequently and usually has more products layered onto it than the body. Hairline, brows, nostrils, lips and the transition into the neck can also reveal a heavy application quickly.

A professional-looking result therefore uses less product, greater edge control and a dedicated plan for the neck and décolletage.

## Step 1: prepare the skin

Begin with clean skin and remove anything that could create an uneven barrier. Complete any exfoliating treatment well in advance and avoid applying the mist directly after a process that has left the skin irritated.

Follow your normal skincare unless the product direction or your routine indicates otherwise. Let each layer absorb before misting. The A-List Face Mist is described as lightweight enough to wear over skincare and under makeup, but a wet or oily surface can still make any mist harder to distribute evenly.

Keep hair away from the face. Check the brows, hairline and any facial hair so you know where you will soften the edge after application.

## Step 2: apply a light, even mist

Shake and apply exactly as directed on the product. Keep the bottle moving so one area does not receive a concentrated burst. Cover the centre and outer face consistently, then extend the application lightly toward the jaw and upper neck.

Do not repeatedly mist the cheeks simply because they are easiest to reach. The result should be balanced across forehead, cheeks, nose, chin and jaw.

After application, inspect the edges rather than the overall depth. A light first layer may look understated before development; that is preferable to a visible boundary at the hairline.

## Step 3: soften the perimeter

Use clean fingertips or an approved Jimmy Coco application tool only if the product instructions support it. Tidy the brows, hairline and any area where droplets have settled. Blend the jaw transition so face and neck do not become separate colour zones.

If your body is already tanned with [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades), compare the face to the neck and chest in natural light. The face does not need to be equally dark; it needs to belong to the same finish.

## Step 4: leave it to develop

The current A-List Face Mist direction is 6–8 hours. During development, avoid touching, splashing or sweating heavily across the face. If you apply makeup over the mist, use light movements and avoid repeatedly working the same area.

At the end of the development period, rinse with water only as directed. The guide colour washes away, revealing the developed tan.

## Step 5: build only after you have assessed the result

The formula is buildable. If you want more colour, wait until the first application has developed and been rinsed. Assess the face in daylight and check the relationship between forehead, hairline, jaw, neck and chest before repeating.

A lighter application repeated when needed gives you more control than one overly saturated misting session.

## Common mistakes

- Applying over skin that is still wet with another product
- Concentrating too much mist in the centre of the face
- Forgetting the hairline, brows and jaw transition
- Trying to match a deep body tan in one facial application
- Rinsing before the instructed development period
- Treating the guide colour as the final result

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I wear the mist under makeup?

The product is described as light enough to wear over skincare and under makeup. Allow it to settle and use a light application technique so you do not disturb the layer.

### How often should I reapply it?

Reapply as desired after judging the developed colour and how your own cleansing routine affects it. Do not follow a fixed daily schedule merely to chase depth.

### Does it replace sunscreen?

No. Facial self-tan does not provide sun protection. Continue to use appropriate sun-protection measures.

## Create a controlled facial glow

Start with one even application of the [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist), reserve the full 6–8 hour window and build only after you have seen the rinsed result in natural light.
