# Email Colour, Spacing and Surfaces

## Colour role

Email should use the established Sunless warm-neutral palette with stronger contrast than some editorial web scenes require.

## Core palette roles

- Warm ivory: primary body or module background
- Clean white: product and high-readability modules
- Linen/stone: gentle chapter separation
- Champagne: restrained accent, never the main text colour
- Deep charcoal: primary text
- Matte black: primary CTA and decisive commercial moments
- Muted taupe: secondary text where contrast remains sufficient

Exact approved values should be referenced from the shared colour system once finalised.

## Background cadence

Use tonal changes to separate modules without turning the email into stripes. A typical sequence might be:

Warm ivory hero → white product module → linen education module → charcoal closing CTA → quiet ivory footer.

Avoid more than three pale modules with no visible tonal or spacing change.

## Spacing

Spacing should communicate grouping and status:

- 8–12px between tightly related elements
- 16–24px inside compact modules
- 24–32px between headline, body and CTA
- 40–64px around major editorial modules

## Surfaces

Use flat, robust surfaces. Email does not need complex card depth.

Approved:

- subtle 1px borders
- modest corner radius where supported
- white product surfaces on warm backgrounds
- clear full-width tonal modules

Avoid:

- glass effects
- heavy shadows
- gradients used as decoration
- very pale text on beige
- overlapping layers dependent on absolute positioning
- multiple nested cards

## Dark modules

Deep charcoal may be used for a strong final CTA, launch announcement or premium product feature. Keep copy brief, contrast high and buttons visually distinct.

## Dividers

Use whitespace first. Fine rules are acceptable for utility information, order details and footer navigation. Do not divide every module mechanically.

## Success criteria

Colour and surfaces succeed when the email remains recognisably Sunless, each module is legible at a glance and the design still works in clients that simplify advanced styling.
