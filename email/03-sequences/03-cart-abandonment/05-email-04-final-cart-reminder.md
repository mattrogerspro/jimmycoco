# Email 04 — Final Cart Reminder

## Role

Close the recovery sequence with one final accurate return path while preserving trust.

## Default timing

Send approximately four days after sequence entry when the cart remains recoverable and the customer has not purchased, entered checkout recovery or become suppressed.

## Primary objective

Give the customer a final opportunity to resume the live cart without manufactured urgency.

## Message hierarchy

1. Restrained closing headline
2. Current cart summary
3. One concise reassurance point
4. One primary CTA
5. Optional support route

## Subject-line territories

- One last reminder about your Sunless cart
- Your Sunless selection is still available
- Ready when you are
- Continue with your selected routine

Do not use “last chance,” countdown language or stock pressure unless a real, validated commercial condition exists and the wording is approved.

## Cart presentation

Show the current cart only. Remove unavailable items and refresh quantities, variants, prices, currency, discounts and subtotal.

Do not add a large recommendation grid or broaden the message into a campaign.

## Incentive branch

A final-message incentive is optional, never automatic. It may appear only when:

- the contact qualifies under an approved policy;
- margin and promotion rules permit it;
- the incentive is valid for every represented item or exclusions are clear;
- the code or automatic discount is applied correctly in the restored cart;
- expiry wording is real and server-validated;
- a non-incentive holdout is maintained.

Do not invent urgency around an evergreen code.

## CTA

Use `Return to my cart` or `Continue checkout`. The destination must restore the live cart securely.

## End-of-sequence behaviour

After this email:

- mark the sequence complete;
- do not continue repeated cart reminders;
- retain suppression and outcome data;
- allow later lifecycle messaging only under the global contact policy;
- permit a future materially new cart to qualify after the approved cooldown.

## Validation

Immediately before send verify purchase state, checkout ownership, cart revision, stock, prices, discounts, delivery and returns wording, consent, suppression, recovery URL and idempotency.

## Success criteria

Measure incremental recovered orders and margin, not only attributed revenue. Monitor discount dependency, unsubscribes, complaints and repeat abandonment behaviour.
