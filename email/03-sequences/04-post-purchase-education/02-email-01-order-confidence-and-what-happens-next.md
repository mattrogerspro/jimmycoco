# Email 01 — Order Confidence and What Happens Next

## Role

Welcome the customer into ownership, confirm what the education journey will help with and reduce uncertainty immediately after purchase.

This email is separate from the transactional order receipt. It must not duplicate payment, tax, address or legal order-confirmation content.

## Default timing

Send shortly after the order-confirmation event, subject to consent classification, duplicate prevention and local-time policy.

## Primary objective

Make the customer feel confident that they chose well and know what to do before the product arrives.

## Message hierarchy

1. Calm thank-you and ownership acknowledgement
2. Purchased product or routine summary
3. What happens next
4. One useful preparation action
5. Support route

## Suggested content

- concise acknowledgement of the purchase;
- approved product image and exact purchased variant where useful;
- a short statement of the intended result;
- dispatch and delivery wording based only on verified state;
- a simple “before it arrives” preparation note;
- link to the product-specific guide or saved order details;
- clear customer-support path.

## Subject-line territories

- Your Sunless routine starts here
- Everything you need for your first result
- A few steps before your Sunless order arrives
- Your product guide is ready

Do not use “Your order has shipped” unless a dispatch event has occurred.

## CTA

Primary CTA options:

- `See your product guide`
- `Prepare for your result`
- `View your routine`

Do not make another purchase the primary action.

## Multi-product orders

Present the products as one routine in application order. Avoid a large receipt-style grid. Show only information that helps use, preparation or sequencing.

## Returning-customer branch

For repeat purchasers of the same product:

- acknowledge familiarity without claiming usage frequency;
- offer a concise refresher;
- highlight any verified instruction change;
- avoid beginner-heavy copy.

## Asset rules

Use the exact approved product asset. Packaging, labels, shade indicators and product colour must not be AI-redrawn or altered.

## Suppression

Do not send when the order is cancelled, payment has failed, the address is suppressed or an active service problem already owns the customer journey.

## Success signals

Guide views, support avoidance, preparation-content engagement and healthy downstream use. Opens alone are not sufficient.
