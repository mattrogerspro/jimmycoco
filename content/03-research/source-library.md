# Source library

Primary sources worth building on, with how to cite them. Anything cited in an
article should be traceable to an entry here.

`citations` format is `Label | URL` — label is the **source**, not the claim, and
includes the year.

---

## Scientific — DHA and skin

### ACS Omega — DHA browning kinetics
**PMC9753197** · `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9753197/`
Maillard reaction mechanism, pH and temperature effects. **Temperature is the
single most influential variable on browning intensity** — practically
consequential and essentially unknown in the trade.
> `ACS Omega, DHA browning kinetics, 2022 | https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9753197/`

### Draelos — stratum corneum and colour depth
Colour depth is proportional to stratum corneum thickness. Explains dark knees
and elbows, light faces, and why exfoliation works. The single most useful
mechanism in the craft pillar.
⚠ Full citation still needs pinning to a specific paper and year before use.

### Jung et al. — free radical generation under UV
DHA-treated skin generates **>180% more free radicals under UV**. A DHA tan
gives roughly **SPF 3–4**. Basis for `is-a-spray-tan-sun-protection`.
⚠ Full citation needs pinning before use.

## Regulatory

### UK SAG-CS 2024 opinion
The only regulator modelling of spray booth exposure, with margins of safety by
booth type. UK limits: **leave-on self-tan 14%, rinse-off 22.5%**. EU limit
**10%**. The strongest safety asset available and unused by the trade.
⚠ PDF — `WebFetch` could not parse it. Firecrawl. Priority.

### 21 CFR 73.2150 — FDA, DHA
`https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-73`
The actual FDA position. **Contains no numeric cap** — this is the document that
disproves the near-universal "15% FDA limit" claim.

### HSE — COSHH
`https://www.hse.gov.uk/coshh/`
What COSHH requires of a salon; risk assessment; "adequate control" where no
numeric standard exists.

### HSE RR721
Research report relevant to exposure. ⚠ PDF, unparsed. Firecrawl.

### legislation.gov.uk
The enabling legislation for special treatments licensing, which differs by UK
nation and by London borough versus the rest of England. **Still to be
assembled** — blocks the compliance hub.

### BABTAC 2016 PDF
The trade body's most recent indexed asset on the subject. Useful mainly as
evidence of the gap. ⚠ PDF, unparsed.

## Not sources

Other brands' blogs · salon marketing sites · Etsy listings · forum posts · any
figure whose only provenance is that everyone repeats it.

**If a claim's trail ends at another blog, it belongs on the
[claims register](claims-register.md), not in an article.**
