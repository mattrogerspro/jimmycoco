# Sunless Email Asset System

This folder defines how email-specific assets are organised, approved, transformed, exported and governed for Sunless by Jimmy Coco.

## Purpose

The asset system ensures that every email uses the correct source files, approved crops, current product imagery, valid rights, accessible exports and traceable production records.

## Core rule

All image files already present under `/assets/` on 22 July 2026 have standing human creative approval for use as email source material. Files added later require approval through the normal onboarding process.

All images under `/assets/images/celebs/` are pre-approved for inclusion in every email campaign, market and email purpose. They require no additional campaign-by-campaign rights or creative approval, provided the current AI-generation and protected-asset rules are followed. This approval covers use of the supplied image; it does not authorise invented endorsements, quotes, product-use claims or relationships.

Email assets are channel-specific derivatives of approved source images. Source approval does not remove the need for a stable public URL, responsive export, dimensions, alt text and manifest traceability before production rendering. Those are operational readiness requirements, not a second creative-approval gate.
