The Shade & Undertone Guide

Jimmy's method for natural, undertone-true colour — the same judgement he uses on set, in one page. Read the undertone, choose the depth, develop with care. Colour that looks warm and believable in daylight, never orange, never flat.

01

Read the undertone

Check the inside of the wrist in daylight

Cool

Veins look blue or purple. Silver jewellery flatters. Skin can burn before it tans.

Jimmy's direction

Lean to a violet-based formula to keep colour fresh and stop it turning grey or dull.

Neutral

Veins are blue-green; silver and gold both suit. Skin tans steadily and evenly.

Jimmy's direction

The most forgiving base — a classic brown formula reads true. Build depth in layers.

Warm

Veins look green; gold jewellery flatters. Skin tans easily and rarely burns.

Jimmy's direction

Lean to a green/olive base to balance natural warmth and avoid an orange turn.

02

Choose the depth

04

Develop & rinse

  • Let colour develop 6–8 hours (or per product) before the first rinse.
  • Rinse in lukewarm water only until it runs clear — no soap yet.
  • Colour continues to deepen for 24 hours; judge the true result the next day.
  • For events, tan 1–2 days ahead so colour settles naturally.
05

Prep & aftercare

  • Exfoliate 24 hrs before; skip oils and heavy moisturiser on the day.
  • Barrier-cream the hands, knees, elbows & ankles to stop grabbing.
  • Loose, dark clothing after; avoid long showers, sweat & swimming early on.
  • Moisturise daily to fade evenly — the take-home range extends colour a week+.

Jimmy's rule

“Match the base to the undertone, then build in layers. Natural colour is control, not depth — you can always add, you can't easily take away.”
