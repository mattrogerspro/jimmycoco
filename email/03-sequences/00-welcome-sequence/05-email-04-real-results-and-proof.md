# Email 04 — Real Results and Proof

## Send timing

Approximately five days after enrolment.

## Job of the email

Replace abstract promises with credible, relevant evidence while preserving the integrity of real customer imagery.

## Primary message

> See the product, routine and development context behind the result.

## Primary CTA

**EXPLORE REAL RESULTS**

## Suggested subject-line directions

- Real skin. Real routines. Real results.
- See what different Sunless routines create
- The details behind the glow
- Results you can understand, not just admire

## Preview-text directions

- Product, coats, development time and starting context.
- Browse verified results by desired depth.
- No digitally manufactured skin-tone changes.

## Content architecture

1. Short proof-led introduction.
2. Two or three verified result stories maximum.
3. For each: unchanged approved image, product used, selected depth or variant, coats, development time and a concise customer concern.
4. One primary CTA to the full results gallery.
5. Optional contextual link to the subscriber’s saved match.
6. Disclosure and footer.

## Proof selection

Choose results that are:

- approved for the intended usage;
- representative of different customer needs rather than tokenistic diversity;
- accompanied by sufficient routine metadata;
- relevant to the recipient’s known desired depth or product interest where available;
- free from misleading lighting, retouching or cropping.

## Image-integrity rule

Customer, creator and celebrity photographs must be inserted as source assets. Do not use generative processing to change faces, skin colour, body shape, background, lighting, texture or product appearance. Any layout crop must follow the approved asset usage record and must not misrepresent the result.

## Copy direction

Proof captions should be factual and concise.

Example:

> Medium buildable result · two light coats · developed for the stated time · applied with the recommended preparation routine.

Do not write certainty claims such as “you will look exactly like this.”

## Visual direction

- Photography-led but not image-only.
- Clear metadata beside or beneath each image.
- Consistent image treatment without forcing incompatible source images into deceptive before/after symmetry.
- Minimal decorative elements.
- One matte black CTA after the proof set.

## Personalisation

When the subscriber has a saved recommendation, prioritise one result created with the same product or a genuinely comparable routine. Label the relationship accurately:

> A result created with your recommended product.

Do not claim “your exact result.”

## Failure modes

Reject the email if it:

- uses altered people or synthetic before-and-after images;
- omits product and routine context;
- relies on star ratings without provenance;
- cherry-picks only extreme results;
- shows more than three competing proof stories;
- confuses celebrity association with product-result evidence;
- uses fake urgency or an unrelated offer.

## Success criteria

- Each result is understandable, not merely aspirational.
- Source-image integrity is preserved.
- The reader can connect evidence to a product and method.
- The full gallery CTA is obvious.
- Disclosures remain readable on mobile and with images blocked.
