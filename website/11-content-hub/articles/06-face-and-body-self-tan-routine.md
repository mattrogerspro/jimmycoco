# Face Tan and Body Tan: How to Build One Seamless Result

Use a dedicated facial product for the face and Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé for the body, then connect the two with a light transition across the jaw, neck and décolletage. Do not try to make the face and body equally dark in one session. A seamless result is coordinated, not uniform.

Jimmy Coco’s [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) and [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) share a current 6–8 hour development window, making them practical to plan as one routine while still treating facial skin differently.

## Choose the body depth first

Decide whether Malibu Medium or Dark best matches your desired body result. If you want a soft, everyday bronze or more room to build, begin with Medium. Choose Dark when you want greater depth and are comfortable controlling hands, feet and blend edges.

The body shade becomes the anchor. The face should support it, but not necessarily duplicate it.

## Why the face should be treated separately

The face is cleansed more often and carries skincare, makeup and sun protection. It also has more visual landmarks: brows, hairline, jaw and the centre of the face. A mist lets you apply a fine, lighter layer and repeat it later if needed.

Using a dedicated facial product also makes maintenance easier. You can refresh the face without reapplying the entire body routine.

## The complete sequence

### 1. Prepare face and body

Complete exfoliation and hair removal early enough for the skin to settle. On application day, begin with clean, dry body skin and an absorbed facial skincare routine.

### 2. Apply Malibu to the body

Use the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) for legs, torso and arms. Keep the body application even, then finish hands, feet, knees and elbows with residual product or [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush).

Bring the body formula lightly across the upper chest if that area will be visible. Avoid creating a hard stop at the base of the neck.

### 3. Apply the A-List Face Mist

Mist the face lightly and evenly, then soften the hairline, brows and jaw. Extend a controlled amount onto the upper neck only as needed to connect the face and chest.

### 4. Check the three transitions

Inspect:

- jaw to neck;
- neck to décolletage;
- wrists to hands.

These boundaries matter more than making the centre of every area exactly the same shade.

### 5. Develop and rinse

Both current consumer formulas specify 6–8 hours. Follow the exact directions on each product. The A-List Face Mist currently instructs a water-only rinse. Do not assume the same rinse method for every formula without checking its label.

## How to balance face and body depth

Use this simple hierarchy:

| Area | Intended visual weight | Application approach |
|---|---|---|
| Torso and limbs | Main bronze depth | Full, even body application |
| Décolletage and neck | Transition | Controlled blend with no hard boundary |
| Face | Slightly softer, buildable finish | Fine mist; assess before repeating |
| Hands and feet | Softest edges | Residual product and precision blending |

This variation mirrors how natural colour appears and helps the finished result remain believable in daylight.

## Makeup and the final finish

Once the tan has fully developed and been rinsed, judge the face without trying to correct it immediately with heavy bronzer. Look at the face beside the neck and shoulders. Makeup should refine the finish, not conceal a mismatched base.

If the face is too light, a later light application of the A-List Face Mist is more controlled than another full body session. If the body is lighter than intended, reassess the Malibu shade and application only after the first result is complete.

## Common mistakes

- Applying the body formula heavily to the face for an exact match
- Stopping body colour sharply at the collarbone
- Forgetting the back and sides of the neck
- Misting the cheeks repeatedly while neglecting forehead and jaw
- Making hands as dark as the forearms
- Adding more before the first result has developed

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I apply both products in the same session?

Their current development windows align, so a coordinated session is practical. Follow each product’s directions independently.

### Should my face be lighter than my body?

A slightly softer face often looks more natural and leaves room for makeup. The important point is that face, neck and body transition smoothly.
