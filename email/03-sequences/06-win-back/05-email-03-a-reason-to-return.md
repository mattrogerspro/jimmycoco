# Email 03 — A Reason to Return

## Role

Provide the strongest appropriate reason for the customer to re-engage, using value, relevance or an approved commercial incentive without undermining margin or training customers to wait for discounts.

## Default timing

Send approximately 5–7 days after Email 02 when the customer remains eligible and has not purchased, entered cart recovery or changed communication preferences.

## Primary objective

Convert renewed interest into a meaningful action: product exploration, shade-match completion, cart creation or purchase.

## Value hierarchy

Use the strongest valid route in this order:

1. A highly relevant product or routine solution
2. Improved convenience or easier decision support
3. Verified proof or expert guidance tied to the customer’s previous category
4. Early access, bundle value or loyalty benefit when genuinely available
5. A controlled incentive when approved by commercial policy

## Incentive governance

An incentive may appear only when:

- the customer segment is eligible;
- expected margin remains acceptable;
- the code, value, market, expiry and exclusions are validated immediately before send;
- the customer has not already received or used an equivalent incentive inside the cooldown window;
- the offer does not conflict with another active promotion;
- holdout testing can measure incremental value.

Do not create fake urgency, false scarcity or invented expiry dates.

## Subject-line territories

Without incentive:

- A reason to revisit your Sunless routine
- Your easiest route back to colour
- Ready when you are

With an approved incentive:

- A welcome-back benefit for your next routine
- A little extra value when you return
- Your approved Sunless welcome-back offer

Avoid percentage or price claims in the subject line unless the offer is fully validated and permitted.

## Message hierarchy

1. Clear value proposition
2. One relevant product, routine or guidance route
3. Optional approved benefit
4. Terms or exclusions in readable live text
5. One primary CTA

## CTA examples

- `Return to my routine`
- `Find my best match`
- `Explore the collection`
- `Use my welcome-back benefit`

## Product selection

Prefer one primary product or one tightly related routine. Do not use an indiscriminate product grid simply because the customer is lapsed.

If the previous product is unavailable, use only an approved successor or route to guidance. Never imply equivalence without merchandising approval.

## Pre-send validation

Confirm:

- consent and flow ownership;
- no purchase, active cart, checkout or support case;
- product and variant availability;
- current price and currency;
- incentive eligibility and remaining validity;
- unique or shared code behaviour;
- destination URL;
- terms visibility;
- idempotency key unused.

## Success criteria

Measure incremental purchase and contribution margin against holdout, not only attributed revenue. Also monitor code leakage, discount dependency, unsubscribe, complaint and post-purchase return rates.
