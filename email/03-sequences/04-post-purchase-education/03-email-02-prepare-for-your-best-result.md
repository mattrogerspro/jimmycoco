# Email 02 — Prepare for Your Best Result

## Role

Give the customer the practical preparation steps that make application easier and the result more even.

## Default timing

Send after dispatch or approximately one to two days before expected delivery. Never imply confirmed delivery when only an estimate exists.

## Primary objective

Help the customer complete the right preparation at the right time without overwhelming them.

## Content hierarchy

1. Preparation headline
2. Product-specific timing window
3. Three to five essential preparation steps
4. What to avoid immediately before application
5. One primary CTA to the full guide

## Guidance rules

Instructions must come from the approved product guide. They may cover:

- exfoliation timing;
- hair-removal timing;
- clean, dry skin;
- moisturising specific dry areas;
- removal of deodorant, fragrance, oils or makeup where relevant;
- tools needed for the purchased format;
- patch-test or safety wording when required by approved product guidance.

Do not create medical advice or add contraindications that are not part of approved instructions.

## Product branching

- **Face product:** prioritise clean skin, skincare compatibility and precise application areas.
- **Body product:** prioritise exfoliation, dry areas and application tools.
- **Contour product:** explain preparation for controlled placement.
- **Application tool only:** explain cleaning, pairing and technique without implying a tan product was purchased.
- **Professional solution:** use the approved trade path rather than consumer instructions.

## Subject-line territories

- Prep now for a smoother Sunless result
- Your pre-tan checklist
- The steps that make the difference
- Before you apply: a simple preparation guide

## CTA

Primary CTA: `View the preparation guide`.

A secondary support link may be included, but do not add unrelated product promotion.

## Visual system

Use a compact editorial checklist, approved instructional imagery and live text. Essential instructions must not exist only inside an image.

## Validation

Before send confirm exact product, likely delivery timing, approved preparation profile, locale, consent and absence of delivery or support problems.

## Success signals

Preparation-guide engagement, fewer avoidable application questions and stronger downstream first-use satisfaction.
