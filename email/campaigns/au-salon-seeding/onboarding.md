## Notes

- **Trigger:** any positive reply / sample request / booked call. Move the contact out of the cold sequence so no further cold touches fire.
- **The one ask:** the postal address (to send the sample) — everything else follows the trial.
- **Keep the premium posture:** "no lock-in, no pressure" reassures without discounting.
- **Follow-ups after this** belong to a proper account/onboarding flow (sample-received check-in, post-call trade-terms summary, first-order confirmation) — out of scope for the cold sequence, easy to add next.
