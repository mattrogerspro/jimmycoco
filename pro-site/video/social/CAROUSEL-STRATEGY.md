# Jimmy Coco Pro carousel strategy

## Why the formats differ

The two platforms share the same campaign truth but not the same reading
behaviour.

Instagram receives a seven-page, 4:5 sequence designed for quick swiping:

1. A pattern-breaking cover that can stand alone in the feed
2. A single commercial reframe
3. Three proof points that are easy to scan
4. One large number or result
5. A practical decision
6. A low-friction route that removes certification anxiety
7. One destination-specific CTA

LinkedIn receives an eight-page PDF document. It adds a commercial-problem page
before the reframe, makes assumptions explicit and keeps the explanatory copy
slightly denser. This makes it useful to a salon owner or operator rather than
looking like an Instagram advert uploaded to LinkedIn.

## Evidence behind the choices

- LinkedIn officially recommends converting document posts to PDF, using a
  single consistent page size, flattening layered PDFs and adding a clear
  document title. The platform accepts PDFs up to 100MB and 300 pages:
  <https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a519831>
- LinkedIn carousel-ad guidance supports a two-to-ten-card story and recommends
  1080px source images. The organic documents here use eight pages, keeping the
  narrative within that familiar interaction length:
  <https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a427022/carousel-image-ads-advertising-specifications>
- A 2025 analysis of almost 700,000 Instagram and TikTok posts found Instagram
  carousels achieved 13% higher reach and roughly 50% more engagement than Reels
  in that dataset. This is directional evidence, not a performance guarantee:
  <https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/>
- Buffer's account-level analysis found its carousels produced a slightly higher
  engagement rate and twice as many saves as other formats. Again, this is a
  reason to test carousels alongside video—not a reason to stop using video:
  <https://buffer.com/resources/do-instagram-carousels-get-more-engagement/>

## Recommended publishing order

Use the same commercial idea on the two platforms in the same week, but do not
publish them at the same time. Instagram should lead with the visual version;
LinkedIn should follow 48–72 hours later with the evidence-led document.

| Week | Campaign | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | This is not a £60 bottle | Immediate Pro order |
| 2 | Test it on a real client | Free trial |
| 3 | From Hollywood to your booth | Free trial |
| 4 | Training and certification | Free trial |
| 5 | The tan ends. The sale does not | Pro + retail order |
| 6 | The result after they leave | Free trial |
| 7 | Launch content included | Immediate Pro order |
| 8 | The Salon Launch Pack | Immediate order |
| 9 | Your treatment room fits in your boot | Mobile Pro order |
| 10 | Test before you carry stock | Mobile free trial |
| 11 | Turn followers into bookings | Mobile free trial |
| 12 | Mobile does not mean budget | Mobile Pro order |

If a 12-week run is too slow, publish two themes each week. Keep at least 48
hours between posts aimed at the same audience.

## What to measure

Judge the decks by commercial movement, not likes alone.

1. Saves and shares on Instagram
2. Profile visits and website taps
3. LinkedIn document opens, page progression and downloads where available
4. Sessions carrying the supplied `organic-carousel` UTM tags
5. Trial-form starts and completed trial requests
6. Product-page visits and completed order requests
7. Cost per qualified trial or order if the post is later promoted

Keep the first six weeks as a controlled learning period. Do not change the
cover, slide count, caption and CTA all at once. First compare trial-led against
order-led themes. Then test one alternative cover hook on the two highest-traffic
topics.

## Non-negotiable publishing checks

- Keep the assumptions and disclosures attached to every financial claim.
- Recheck current prices and fulfilment terms before publishing.
- Do not describe certification as automatic, guaranteed or required to order.
- Use only approved celebrity photography and the exact endorsement wording for
  which permission exists.
- Confirm the tracked destination resolves correctly on mobile before every
  post goes live.
