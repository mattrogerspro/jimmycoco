# 00 — Executive Summary

## Purpose

The Sunless Creative Director Bible defines one coherent experience across every homepage chapter, product story, campaign, visual asset and AI-generated concept.

It is not merely a style guide. It is the operating system for the brand’s digital expression.

## What Sunless is

Sunless is not simply another self-tanning cosmetics brand. It is the productisation of Jimmy Coco’s professional tanning methodology: celebrity-level colour judgement, undertone awareness, layering technique and confidence translated into an elegant at-home experience.

The physical product is the formula. The emotional product is confidence.

## Brand promise

> Red-carpet colour. Made effortless at home.

The homepage should leave the visitor believing:

> This is the closest I can come to receiving a professional Hollywood tan without leaving home.

## Strategic objective

The experience must increase:

- Immediate comprehension
- Trust in Jimmy’s authority
- Confidence in shade selection
- Desire for the finished result
- Perceived product value
- Ease of purchase
- Conversion rate
- Average order value
- Repeat-purchase confidence

## Design position

This project is an evolution, not a redesign.

The existing Sunless identity already contains valuable equity:

- Warm ivory and champagne tones
- Editorial serif typography
- Refined sans-serif supporting copy
- Natural, glowing skin photography
- Jimmy’s backstage authority
- Matte black calls to action
- Premium beauty restraint

These elements should remain immediately recognisable. The work improves the system through:

- Stronger visual hierarchy
- Distinct editorial chapters
- Better guided decision-making
- More specific proof
- Larger, more readable typography
- Greater variation in composition
- More deliberate negative space
- Clearer purchase pathways

## Core commercial problem

The existing homepage is visually polished but behaves too often like a long series of similarly weighted ecommerce sections. At distance, multiple chapters merge into one continuous ivory rectangle.

The improved experience must transform the page from:

> Hero → cards → cards → products → cards → reviews

into:

> Arrival → authority → guidance → understanding → proof → desire → confidence → purchase

## Non-negotiable test

When viewed at 10% zoom, with blurred vision or from across the room, the homepage must resolve into 10–12 visibly distinct chapters. Each chapter must possess a unique silhouette, density, background tone, image scale and emotional purpose.

If the page still appears to be one continuous cream block, the design has failed.

## Production method

Generate and refine each homepage scene independently. Use the common design-system documents and the approved reference assets every time. Assemble the final page only after each scene has passed the visual, commercial and consistency checks.

## Definition of success

The finished homepage should feel like a luxury editorial experience that naturally concludes in a purchase—not an ecommerce template disguised with premium photography.
