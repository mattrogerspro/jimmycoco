Internal systems report · Email production platform

The production system: how everything connects.

Six systems cooperate to take a campaign from a one-sentence request to a monitored, compliant send: Claude with the campaign skill, the GitHub repository, Vercel, Resend, Supabase — and the human approver who holds every consequential key. This report maps the whole machine: who owns what, how the pieces talk to each other, the three flows that move work through the system, and the locks that make an accidental send impossible.

Flow 2 · The send gauntlet — what every single email must pass

When (and only when) sending is enabled, Vercel's cron calls the worker every 15 minutes with a bearer secret. Each due message then has to survive six gates in api/_lib/engine.js before Resend is ever contacted:

1
Live modeEnvironment

EMAIL_LIVE_MODE must be on, or the worker exits before touching a single enrollment.

2
Atomic claimSupabase

A database RPC locks the enrollment to exactly one worker — no double processing.

3
Registry enabledRepository

The campaign must be enabled: true in the send registry. New campaigns never are.

4
SuppressionSupabase

Unsubscribes, complaints and bounces block the send — written by the feedback loop below.

5
FrequencyPolicy

A minimum contact gap (16 hours) between marketing touches, or the send reschedules itself.

6
IdempotencyLedger

A unique key per campaign, step and contact — a duplicate can never send twice.

✓
Send→ Resend

sendTemplateEmail with the registry's template ID, at 10am in the contact's own timezone.

Failures retry three times, then park as needs_attention. Lifecycle events pass a triple campaign gate — registry enabled and a database-level enabled flag and suppression.
