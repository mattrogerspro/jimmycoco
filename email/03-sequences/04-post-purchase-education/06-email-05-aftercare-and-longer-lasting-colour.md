# Email 05 — Aftercare and Longer-Lasting Colour

## Role

Help the customer maintain the result, support a more even fade and understand when the routine should be refreshed.

## Default timing

Send approximately five to seven days after delivery or expected first use, adjusted by product format and development profile.

## Primary objective

Improve the ownership experience after application without prematurely turning the message into a replenishment promotion.

## Content hierarchy

1. Simple aftercare headline
2. Three to five approved maintenance actions
3. What can shorten or disrupt the result
4. How to support an even fade
5. When to consider refreshing the colour
6. One primary CTA to the aftercare guide

## Approved guidance areas

Depending on the product, guidance may include:

- moisturising routine;
- shower temperature and duration;
- gentle cleansing;
- drying by patting rather than aggressive rubbing;
- swimming, exfoliation or hair-removal timing;
- treatment of high-friction areas;
- gradual top-up or repeat-application timing;
- safe tool cleaning and product storage.

All advice must come from approved product documentation. Do not create dermatological claims.

## Product branches

- **Face products:** focus on skincare compatibility and controlled refresh.
- **Body products:** focus on moisture, friction and even fading.
- **Contour products:** focus on maintaining subtle placement and avoiding uneven build-up.
- **Gradual products:** explain build-and-maintain rhythm.
- **Professional solutions:** use trade-specific aftercare instructions.
- **Tools only:** focus on cleaning, drying and storage.

## Subject-line territories

- Keep your Sunless colour looking its best
- A simple aftercare routine
- How to support a smoother, more even fade
- Your result after day one

## CTA

Primary CTA: `View the aftercare guide`.

An optional replenishment or complementary-product link may appear only when it is genuinely relevant, current and subordinate to education.

## Cross-sell restraint

Do not recommend an item the customer already purchased. Do not present a complete routine as mandatory when the purchased product works independently. Any recommendation must explain its practical role.

## Validation

Check product profile, likely-use timing, order and support state, stock for any optional recommendation, consent and destination links.

## Success signals

Aftercare-guide use, fewer fading or maintenance questions, stronger satisfaction, appropriate repeat use and lower negative feedback.
