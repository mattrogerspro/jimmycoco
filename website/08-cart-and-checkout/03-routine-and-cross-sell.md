# Routine and Cross-Sell

## Purpose

Increase average order value by helping customers complete a useful tanning routine without interrupting checkout confidence.

## Governing rule

Cross-sell must feel like professional guidance, not opportunistic merchandising.

## Approved logic

Recommend only products that fill a clear missing role:

- Prepare — exfoliation or skin preparation
- Apply — tanning mitt or application tool
- Perfect — face product, finishing product or maintenance item

Do not recommend a duplicate format, an incompatible shade or an item already in the cart.

## Placement

### Mini-cart

Maximum one add-on.

### Full cart

Maximum two add-ons, preferably presented as one compact routine-completion module beneath cart lines rather than between them.

### Checkout

No conventional cross-sell after the customer enters payment. A highly relevant one-click add-on may be tested before payment only if it does not reset entered details, obscure totals or introduce surprise costs.

## Recommendation content

Every recommendation must show:

- accurate product image;
- product name;
- price;
- concise reason for inclusion;
- clear Add control;
- stock state.

Example:

> **Velvet Tanning Mitt**  
> Helps apply mousse evenly while protecting hands.

## Personalisation hierarchy

Use, in order:

1. compatibility with the product in cart;
2. missing routine stage;
3. prior shade-match result;
4. customer purchase history, where consent and data allow;
5. general best-seller status.

Never describe a recommendation as personalised when it is merely a generic bestseller.

## Bundle logic

Where an approved bundle exists, show:

- included products;
- exact bundle saving;
- whether existing cart lines will be replaced;
- final bundle price;
- one confirmation action.

Do not silently replace individual items.

## Visual hierarchy

Cross-sell must be visually quieter than:

- the cart contents;
- final total;
- checkout CTA;
- delivery information.

Avoid carousels, countdowns, red urgency labels and large promotional imagery.

## Measurement

Track:

- recommendation viewed;
- add-on added;
- add-on removed;
- bundle accepted;
- checkout completion after exposure;
- revenue per cart;
- abandonment change.

A cross-sell that raises add-on clicks but reduces completed orders is unsuccessful.

## Success criteria

The recommendation should answer, “Is there one useful thing I have forgotten?” without making the customer reconsider the entire order.
