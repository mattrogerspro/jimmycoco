# Product Page — Gallery and Media System

## Purpose

The gallery must answer practical buying questions while preserving premium art direction. Every asset should help the visitor understand the exact product, texture, scale, application or result.

## Required media sequence

1. **Primary pack shot** — exact packaging, unobstructed and immediately recognisable.
2. **Alternate product angle** — cap, pump, applicator or closure detail.
3. **Texture image** — formula colour and consistency shown accurately.
4. **In-hand scale image** — establishes realistic bottle size.
5. **Application image** — correct product usage on real skin.
6. **Finished-result image** — visible outcome on an appropriate model.
7. **Method or short video** — concise demonstration when available.
8. **Packaging detail** — label, included accessories or bundle contents where relevant.

The order may change by product type, but the first asset should normally show the exact item being purchased.

## Desktop presentation

Use one dominant media frame with a restrained thumbnail rail or a considered editorial grid. The gallery should feel generous and tactile, not like a crowded marketplace carousel.

Recommended behaviours:

- Zoom for genuine packaging and texture detail
- Previous and next controls with clear accessible labels
- Media count or position indicator where useful
- Video identified before activation
- Thumbnails large enough to distinguish content

## Mobile presentation

Use horizontal swipe with visible pagination. Preserve image aspect ratios and avoid excessively tall imagery that forces the purchase information far below the fold.

## Photography standards

- Warm, neutral daylight
- Accurate formula and packaging colour
- Real skin texture
- Soft grounded shadows
- No orange colour cast
- No excessive gloss or artificial HDR
- No impossible floating bottles
- No inaccurate product proportions
- No invented accessories

## Product consistency

Packaging must match the approved canonical reference for:

- Bottle silhouette
- Cap, pump or spray geometry
- Label position
- Logo proportions
- Material finish
- Product volume
- Formula colour where visible

AI-generated concepts are not authoritative packaging references.

## Finished-result imagery

Every result asset should have supporting metadata elsewhere on the page:

- Product used
- Number of coats
- Development time
- Model skin context
- Lighting condition
- Whether the image is retouched or colour adjusted

Do not imply one universal outcome across all skin tones.

## Video rules

Video should demonstrate useful information, not autoplay as decorative movement. Keep sound off by default, provide captions, and ensure all important instructions also exist as text.

## Alt-text pattern

Describe the content and commercial purpose:

> Sunless [product name] bottle shown front-on against warm ivory background.

> Model applying one pump of [product name] mousse to the lower leg using a tanning mitt.

Avoid keyword stuffing and decorative descriptions that omit the product.

## Loading and performance

- Prioritise the first product image.
- Reserve dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Lazy-load non-primary media.
- Deliver responsive image sizes and modern formats.
- Do not load all high-resolution gallery assets before interaction.

## Failure modes

Reject galleries that:

- lead with an unrelated lifestyle image;
- crop the product label or bottle;
- repeat near-identical pack shots;
- use tiny thumbnails;
- conceal video controls;
- distort skin colour;
- present AI-generated packaging as factual product photography;
- make the finished result impossible to compare with the product being sold.

## Success criteria

The gallery succeeds when visitors can inspect the exact product, understand its physical form, see how it is applied and visualise the likely finished result without leaving the purchase zone.
