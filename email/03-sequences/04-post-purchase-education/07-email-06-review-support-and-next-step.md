# Email 06 — Review, Support and Next Step

## Role

Close the educational journey by checking whether the customer received the intended support, inviting honest feedback and routing them to the correct next step.

This message must never prioritise a review over an unresolved problem.

## Default timing

Send approximately 10–14 days after confirmed or estimated delivery, or later when the product requires more time for meaningful use.

## Primary objective

Learn from the customer’s actual experience, make support easy and transition satisfied customers carefully into retention.

## Eligibility

Send only when:

- the relevant item was delivered or reasonably expected to be delivered;
- enough time has passed for meaningful use;
- no open support, delivery, refund, return or safety case exists;
- the order has not been cancelled or fully refunded;
- the address remains eligible;
- the customer has not already submitted a review for the item during the current order cycle.

## Message hierarchy

1. Experience check-in
2. Clear support route
3. Honest feedback or review invitation
4. Optional product-specific next step
5. Preference or education link

## Satisfaction routing

Where the interface asks for an experience response, all choices must remain honest and accessible.

- **Needs help:** open the support path and suppress review promotion.
- **Not yet used:** delay the request and offer the application guide.
- **Used but unsure:** offer troubleshooting or expert guidance.
- **Happy with the result:** invite a verified review without requiring positivity.

Do not use review gating that filters unhappy customers away from the review process while directing only satisfied customers to public review channels.

## Subject-line territories

- How did your first Sunless result go?
- We’re here if you need help
- Tell us about your Sunless experience
- Your result, your honest feedback

## CTA hierarchy

Primary CTA should match the customer state:

- `Get product support`
- `Share honest feedback`
- `Review your product`
- `Reopen the application guide`

Do not use multiple equal-weight CTAs.

## Review standards

- State that honest feedback is welcome.
- Do not offer undisclosed compensation for positive reviews.
- Identify the exact product and order association.
- Prevent duplicate requests and duplicate submissions where practical.
- Preserve review provenance and moderation policy.

## Next-step logic

A subordinate next step may include:

- saving the routine;
- learning when to replenish;
- viewing a genuinely complimentary product;
- updating email preferences;
- joining an approved loyalty or VIP programme.

Do not assume the customer needs replenishment based solely on elapsed time.

## Asset and proof rules

Do not ask customers to upload imagery without a clear consent, usage-rights and privacy process. Customer photos must never be retouched or generatively altered for proof use.

## Success signals

Support resolution, useful feedback, verified review completion, preference updates, healthy retention and low complaint or unsubscribe rates.
