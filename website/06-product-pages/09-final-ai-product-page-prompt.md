# Final AI Product Page Prompt

Use this prompt only after loading the shared brand system, website design system, product-page architecture and approved product references.

---

Create one ultra-premium **desktop product page concept** for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**, using the attached current Sunless website, approved product photography, packaging references, Jimmy Coco references and skin-result imagery as strict factual and visual inputs.

This is an **evolution, not a redesign**. Preserve approximately 95% of the established Sunless identity: warm ivory, linen, stone and champagne neutrals; elegant editorial serif headlines; refined sans-serif body copy; matte-black purchase actions; natural skin photography; quiet luxury; generous negative space; and Jimmy Coco’s professional Hollywood authority.

The page must feel like a luxury editorial beauty experience organised with Apple-level clarity, but it must remain unmistakably a **commercial product page**. The visitor must immediately understand what the product is, what result it creates, who it suits, what it costs, which option is selected and how to purchase it. Do not create a brochure that hides the buying process.

## Canvas and structure

Design a 1700px-wide desktop page using an invisible 12-column grid.

Create a visually distinct long-form sequence:

1. Above-the-fold purchase zone
2. Result-at-a-glance facts
3. Editorial mechanism and formula story
4. Suitability and expected result
5. Real skin results
6. Professional application method
7. Formula and product detail
8. Reviews organised by customer concern
9. Prepare / Apply / Perfect routine
10. Final purchase reminder

Alternate density, image scale and background tone so the page reads as a sequence of editorial chapters rather than one continuous cream rectangle.

## Above-the-fold purchase zone

Use a refined two-part composition:

- Left 7 columns: one dominant, accurate product image with a restrained thumbnail rail and supporting texture, scale, application and finished-result media.
- Right 5 columns: product identity and purchase controls.

The purchase panel must show, in this order:

- Product category eyebrow
- Exact product name
- One concise, factual result statement
- Verified rating and review count
- Price and size
- A clear suitability summary
- Shade, depth, formula or size selection where relevant
- Stock status
- One dominant matte-black **ADD TO BAG — [PRICE]** button
- Concise dispatch, delivery and returns reassurance
- A quiet **Check My Match** route for uncertain customers

Do not hide price or Add to Bag below the fold. Do not make editorial links compete with purchase.

## Product and packaging accuracy

Use the approved product reference exactly. Preserve bottle silhouette, cap or pump geometry, label position, logo proportions, material finish, product volume and formula colour. Do not invent packaging, accessories, shades, claims, prices or ingredients.

The primary media image must show the exact item being sold, not an unrelated lifestyle scene. Use realistic shadows and physically credible scale. No floating bottles, impossible reflections, warped labels or generic white-label packaging.

## Suitability and result communication

Make the result concrete and easy to scan. Include factual values for:

- Expected depth
- Undertone behaviour
- Suitable skin context
- Finish
- Development time
- Buildability
- Experience level

Use direct language such as:

> Creates a deep, neutral bronze with controlled warmth and buildable intensity.

Also show when another product may be more suitable. Do not imply one identical result for every skin tone.

## Real results

Create a photography-dominant proof chapter using authentic, realistic skin imagery. Each result must visibly connect to metadata such as product used, number of coats, development time and lighting context. Preserve real skin texture. Do not exaggerate before-and-after differences with incompatible lighting, saturation or aggressive retouching.

## Application method

Present a calm professional ritual:

**Prepare → Apply → Develop → Maintain**

Use concise numbered actions, accurate product and mitt imagery, practical timing and one useful Jimmy Coco professional tip. Do not use a generic four-card template or decorative beauty language in place of instructions.

## Reviews

Show one dominant customer story and a smaller review stream organised around genuine concerns such as fair skin, first-time use, deep colour, transfer, even fading and event preparation. Include verified status and relevant product variant. Do not invent quotations or hide lower ratings.

## Routine cross-sell

Create a restrained **Prepare / Apply / Perfect** ritual using the current product and no more than two materially relevant supporting products. Explain why each item improves the result. Make combined price and bundle contents explicit. Do not create a generic related-products carousel.

## Typography and interaction

- Large editorial serif product and chapter headings
- Refined sans-serif product facts and controls
- Strong contrast and readable body copy
- Clear selected, unavailable and error states for variants
- At least 44px interaction targets
- Minimal motion implied through elegant transitions, not visual gimmicks
- Production text should ultimately be rebuilt as live UI

## Visual direction

Use cinematic natural daylight, warm neutral environments, real skin texture, champagne textiles, soft stone surfaces and restrained material depth. Maintain sufficient tonal separation so the page does not become a flat beige wash.

The page should feel premium, human and professionally art-directed—not clinical, neon, influencer-led, marketplace-like or generically spa-themed.

## Do not create

- Generic Shopify product-page styling
- Glassmorphism
- Excessive floating cards
- Tiny product information
- Hidden price or purchase controls
- Multiple equally dominant CTAs
- Unlabelled swatches
- Unsupported scientific or performance claims
- Fake reviews
- Invented product packaging
- Repeated four-card rows
- Decorative gradients
- Oversized lifestyle imagery that pushes purchase below the fold
- A brochure without an obvious sales pipeline

## Intended eye path

Product image → product name → visible result → rating → suitability → selected option → price → Add to Bag → reassurance → proof → method → reviews → routine → final purchase.

## Success criteria

The concept succeeds only when:

- The product and result are understood within five seconds.
- The page remains recognisably Sunless by Jimmy Coco.
- The purchase action is immediate and unmistakable.
- The visitor can judge suitability without guessing.
- Product packaging remains accurate.
- Proof feels authentic and contextualised.
- The editorial chapters increase confidence rather than delaying purchase.
- The page can scale across the Sunless product catalogue without requiring a unique cinematic story for every SKU.

Generate a photorealistic, production-ready desktop product-page concept with strong commercial hierarchy, luxury restraint and clear ecommerce functionality.
