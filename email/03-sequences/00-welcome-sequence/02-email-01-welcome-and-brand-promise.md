# Email 01 — Welcome and Brand Promise

## Send timing

Immediately after confirmed eligibility.

## Job of the email

Acknowledge the subscription, establish the core Sunless promise and provide one immediately useful next step.

## Reader state

The subscriber may know very little about the brand. They may be interested in self-tan but anxious about orange colour, streaking, transfer, choosing the wrong depth or achieving an even result.

## Primary message

> Professional Hollywood colour, made easier to choose and apply at home.

## CTA logic

### Subscriber has no saved shade match
Primary CTA: **FIND MY MATCH**

### Subscriber has a saved shade match
Primary CTA: **VIEW MY MATCH**

### Subscriber has a known product interest but no result
Primary CTA may remain **FIND MY MATCH**. Do not prematurely force a product recommendation from a single browse event.

## Suggested subject-line directions

- Welcome to a more natural-looking tan
- Your best tan starts with the right match
- Welcome to Sunless by Jimmy Coco
- Let’s find the colour that works for you

Avoid exaggerated transformation claims, false familiarity and immediate discount dependence.

## Preview-text directions

- Jimmy’s professional approach, translated for home.
- Start with your undertone, desired depth and routine.
- A clearer way to choose natural-looking colour.

## Content architecture

1. Minimal brand header.
2. Warm editorial hero using an approved source image or accurate product still.
3. Short welcome headline.
4. Two concise paragraphs explaining the value of Jimmy’s judgement.
5. Primary CTA.
6. Three reassurance points.
7. Quiet footer and preferences.

## Reassurance points

Use only verified language, such as:

- Buildable colour rather than one fixed result.
- Guidance based on desired depth and application preference.
- Professional method explained in simple steps.

## Copy direction

The email should feel personally guided without pretending Jimmy wrote an individual message. It should be warm, specific and concise.

Example body direction:

> Welcome to Sunless. Jimmy Coco built his career creating natural-looking colour for people who needed to feel confident under unforgiving cameras and lights. The same judgement now guides the way you choose, apply and build your colour at home.
>
> Start with a few simple questions and we’ll point you toward the most suitable result.

## Visual direction

- Warm ivory field.
- One strong editorial image, not a collage.
- Large serif headline with email-safe fallback.
- Matte black CTA.
- Generous but inbox-efficient spacing.
- No more than one product image unless the saved recommendation is being shown.

## Personalisation

Permitted:

- first name with neutral fallback;
- saved shade-match status;
- source-aware CTA;
- region-appropriate shipping or policy footer.

Do not insert unverified skin-tone assumptions or sensitive inferred traits.

## Failure modes

Reject the email if it:

- leads with a discount before explaining value;
- contains multiple competing product CTAs;
- resembles a generic cosmetics welcome template;
- claims a personal recommendation where none exists;
- uses altered celebrity or customer imagery;
- hides unsubscribe or preference controls.

## Success criteria

- The subscriber understands what makes Sunless distinctive.
- The main CTA is obvious within seconds.
- The message remains useful without an incentive.
- The email renders coherently with images blocked.
- Plain text communicates the same primary message and link.
