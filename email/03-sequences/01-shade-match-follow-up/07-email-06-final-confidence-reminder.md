# 07 — Email 06: Final Confidence Reminder

## Send timing

Approximately nine days after completion, only when no purchase, cart, checkout, newer result or suppression event has occurred.

## Job to be done

Close the sequence calmly by returning the customer to their recommendation and resolving the final decision without manufactured urgency.

## Core message

> Your recommendation is still available whenever you are ready. Review it, adjust it or ask for help.

## Primary CTA

**RETURN TO MY MATCH**

## Secondary actions

Use quiet text links only:

- Change my answers
- Ask for help

## Content structure

1. Concise reminder of the matched product and variant.
2. One-sentence rationale.
3. Current product image and verified price where available.
4. One concise confidence cue: relevant review, application ease or control benefit.
5. Primary CTA.
6. Answer-change and support links.
7. Preference and unsubscribe controls.

## Suggested subject lines

- Your Sunless match is still here
- Ready when you are
- One last look at your personalised match

## Preview text

Review your recommendation, adjust your answers or ask us for help.

## Promotional policy

A discount is not the default purpose of this email. Include an incentive only when:

- it is part of an approved commercial programme;
- eligibility is deterministic and auditable;
- the terms and expiry are accurate;
- the same customer would receive the offer through the approved campaign rules;
- the offer does not create false urgency.

Do not invent countdowns, low-stock warnings or expiry dates.

## Design direction

This should be the quietest message in the sequence:

- generous whitespace;
- one product image;
- brief live text;
- one strong button;
- no large product grid;
- no crowded proof wall.

## Exit state

After this send, mark the sequence complete. The customer may remain eligible for ordinary campaigns, replenishment or behaviour-triggered flows according to consent and contact-pressure rules, but must not receive further messages framed as part of this match follow-up.

## Plain-text version

Include the matched product, rationale, result URL, retake URL, support address and preference links.
