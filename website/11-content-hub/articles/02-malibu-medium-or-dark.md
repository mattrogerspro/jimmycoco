# Malibu Medium or Dark? How to Choose Your Jimmy Coco Shade

Choose Malibu Medium when you want a softer, sun-kissed bronze, are newer to self-tan or prefer to build colour gradually. Choose Malibu Dark when you already feel confident applying self-tan and want a deeper bronze result. Your natural skin tone matters, but the finish you want and the way you apply matter too.

Both shades use the Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé format and are designed to develop over 6–8 hours. The important decision is not “How dark can I go?” It is “Which starting point gives me the control I want?”

## Quick shade guide

| Choose | Best starting point | Result goal | Application approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Malibu Medium** | Fair to medium starting tones, first-time users or anyone wanting restraint | Soft, fresh, everyday bronze | Begin with one even application; reassess after full development |
| **Malibu Dark** | Medium to deeper starting tones or experienced tanners seeking more depth | Deeper, more defined bronze | Apply one controlled layer; keep hands, feet and face lighter |

This is guidance, not a rule based on complexion alone. A fair-skinned customer may choose Dark for a deliberately deeper result, while a deeper-skinned customer may use Medium to add subtle warmth and even-looking radiance.

## Start with the result, not the shade name

“Medium” and “Dark” describe the intended depth of the developed tan. They do not grade beauty, confidence or suitability. Before choosing, decide which of these outcomes is closest to what you want:

- a quiet everyday lift;
- a polished holiday bronze;
- a deeper event result;
- a controllable base that can be built later.

If you are between two outcomes, start with Medium. It is easier to deepen a controlled result than to make an over-strong first application look lighter.

## Consider your experience level

A deeper guide colour can be easy to see, but a deeper developed result also makes missed edges and heavy areas more noticeable. If you are learning, Medium gives you room to practise the Jimmy Coco method: broad areas first, precision areas last, then a full 6–8 hour development before you judge the colour.

Experienced tanners may prefer Dark because they already know how much product their skin needs and how to blend wrists, ankles, hands and feet. Technique remains more important than speed.

## Think about the event and the lighting

For a first application before a wedding, party or photographs, do not use the event itself as your trial. Test the chosen shade far enough in advance to see it in daylight, indoor light and with your intended clothes or makeup.

For photography, the most believable result is not automatically the darkest. Face, neck, shoulders, arms and hands should read as one finish. If your body shade is deeper, use a dedicated facial product such as the [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) in light, buildable applications rather than forcing body product to create the same depth everywhere.

## One coat or two?

Malibu is described as buildable, so the number of applications can change the final depth. One even application of Medium may be enough for an everyday bronze. If you want more depth, wait until the first application has developed and been rinsed before deciding what the skin actually needs.

This creates two useful routes:

- **Medium + controlled repeat:** more gradual control over depth;
- **Dark + one considered application:** a deeper starting point without unnecessary layering.

Do not assume two coats are automatically better. More colour is only useful when it remains even and believable.

## Keep the edges softer

Whichever shade you choose, apply less product to hands, feet, elbows, knees and the perimeter of the face. These areas should transition into the main colour, not match it as an opaque block.

Use the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) for the body, then use the product remaining on the mitt or [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush) to soften the edges. The deeper the shade, the more valuable this restraint becomes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Medium only for fair skin?

No. Medium is also suitable for anyone who wants a subtle, controllable result, regardless of starting tone.

### Is Dark only for experienced users?

No, but a deeper result makes precise blending more important. A first-time user choosing Dark should apply carefully, finish hands and feet last and avoid rushing the development and rinse stages.

### Can I mix Medium and Dark?

Do not invent a mixing ratio. Use one approved shade as directed unless Jimmy Coco publishes a specific mixing method for these products.

### Does a darker self-tan provide more sun protection?

No. Neither shade replaces sunscreen or other sun-protection measures.

## Choose with control

Select [Malibu Medium or Dark](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) according to the result you want today. If you are uncertain, begin with Medium, perfect the application, and build only after you have seen the fully developed colour.
