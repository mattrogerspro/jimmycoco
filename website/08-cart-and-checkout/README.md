# Cart and Checkout System

This folder defines the complete Sunless by Jimmy Coco purchase journey from add-to-bag through order confirmation.

The objective is to preserve luxury, confidence and clarity while removing every avoidable source of checkout friction.

## Files

- `00-purchase-flow-architecture.md` — end-to-end purchase journey and governing rules
- `01-mini-cart.md` — add-to-bag confirmation and cart drawer
- `02-full-cart.md` — full cart architecture and behaviour
- `03-routine-and-cross-sell.md` — restrained routine-building and add-on logic
- `04-shipping-delivery-and-returns.md` — delivery confidence, thresholds and returns communication
- `05-checkout-experience.md` — checkout structure, forms, payment and validation
- `06-payment-success-and-order-confirmation.md` — success state and post-purchase reassurance
- `07-mobile-cart-and-checkout.md` — mobile-specific purchase behaviour
- `08-final-ai-cart-checkout-prompts.md` — final image-generation prompts

## Core principle

Checkout is not the place to re-sell the entire brand. It is the place to make the customer feel certain, protected and ready to complete the purchase.

The visual experience must remain recognisably Sunless, but decorative expression should reduce as commitment increases. Clarity, legibility, error prevention and trust take priority over editorial flourish.
