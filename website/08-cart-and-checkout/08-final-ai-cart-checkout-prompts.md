# Final AI Cart and Checkout Prompts

Use these prompts with the shared brand references, website design system, approved product imagery and the relevant cart or checkout specification. Generated concepts establish architecture and art direction; all production text, prices, controls and payment interfaces must be rebuilt as live UI.

## Prompt 01 — Desktop mini-cart

Create one ultra-premium desktop ecommerce mini-cart for **Sunless by Jimmy Coco**, using the approved website references as the strict visual identity. Show the existing product page dimmed behind a refined right-side cart drawer. Preserve the warm ivory, linen, champagne and charcoal palette, editorial serif accents, refined sans-serif operational typography and matte-black CTA language.

The drawer must clearly confirm **“Added to your bag”** and show one accurate Sunless product thumbnail, complete product name, selected shade or variant, volume, price, quantity control and remove action. Beneath the product, show a clearly labelled subtotal and one dominant full-width CTA reading **“CHECKOUT SECURELY”**, with a quieter **“Continue Shopping”** action. Include a restrained delivery-threshold message and no more than one relevant application accessory recommendation beneath the checkout action.

The design must feel calm, precise and commercially obvious. Use fine rules and disciplined spacing instead of multiple floating cards. No promotion carousel, no confetti, no oversized lifestyle imagery, no glassmorphism, no urgency countdown, no cluttered trust badges and no ambiguous variant information. Production-ready desktop UI, approximately 1700px wide.

## Prompt 02 — Desktop full cart

Create one premium desktop **Your Bag** page for Sunless by Jimmy Coco. Use a restrained two-column composition: cart contents on the left and a sticky order summary on the right. Maintain the approved warm-neutral visual identity while making price, product selection and checkout completely unambiguous.

Show two or three accurate product lines with clear product imagery, names, selected shades or variants, size, price, quantity controls and remove actions. The order summary must show subtotal, discount only when applicable, delivery status and a strongly emphasised final total. Include one dominant matte-black **“CHECKOUT SECURELY”** CTA. Place accepted-payment and returns reassurance quietly beneath it.

Below the cart lines, include one compact routine-completion module recommending no more than two genuinely compatible products, each with a concise reason. Do not turn the page into a product gallery. Use an elegant serif page title, operational sans-serif content, white or warm ivory fields, fine charcoal dividers and generous but controlled spacing. No floating dashboard cards, no surprise charges, no promotional takeover and no decorative hero image.

## Prompt 03 — Desktop checkout

Create one ultra-refined desktop checkout screen for Sunless by Jimmy Coco. This is a transactional interface, not an editorial landing page. Preserve the approved brand palette and typography but reduce decorative expression in favour of clarity, confidence and completion.

Use a reduced header with the Sunless logo, a secure-checkout cue, support access and a discreet return-to-cart route. Structure the main page as a clear checkout form on the left and a persistent order summary on the right. Organise the form into **Contact**, **Delivery** and **Payment** groups with visible labels, generous input sizing, clear selected states and no placeholder-only fields.

Show guest checkout by default, an email field, delivery address, delivery methods with price and estimated dates, billing-address control, supported payment choices and a final matte-black CTA reading **“PAY £XX.XX”**. The order summary must show accurate product thumbnails, variants, quantities, subtotal, delivery and final total. Include precise returns and payment reassurance near commitment, not a wall of trust badges.

Use bright white and warm ivory surfaces, restrained serif headings, refined sans-serif form content, subtle rules and clear focus states. No broad navigation, no campaign banner, no account gate, no hidden delivery cost, no decorative photography, no excessive cards and no tiny legal text.

## Prompt 04 — Mobile cart and checkout

Create a production-ready mobile ecommerce sequence for Sunless by Jimmy Coco shown as three adjacent phone screens: **mini-cart**, **full cart** and **checkout**. Preserve the approved warm ivory palette, editorial serif accent and matte-black CTA language while simplifying the interface for touch and small screens.

The mini-cart must show Added to Bag confirmation, product image, exact selected variant, subtotal and a full-width Checkout Securely CTA. The full cart must use a clear one-column hierarchy with quantity and remove controls, visible delivery status and a sticky checkout action that does not cover content. The checkout screen must use single-column labelled fields, an expandable order summary, delivery methods, mobile-friendly payment choices and a final CTA showing the exact payment amount.

All touch targets must be generous. Ensure no horizontal scrolling, hidden totals, keyboard obstruction, card-grid clutter or compressed desktop layout. Use realistic mobile safe areas, legible text and clean operational hierarchy. No glassmorphism, no tiny labels, no ambiguous icons, no promotional popups and no intrusive cross-sell.

## Prompt 05 — Order confirmation

Create one calm, premium desktop order-confirmation page for Sunless by Jimmy Coco. The first message must be unmistakable: **“Thank you — your order is confirmed.”** Show a refined success mark, order reference, confirmation-email message, delivery address, delivery estimate, ordered items and final total.

Use a bright white or warm ivory background with generous controlled whitespace, one elegant serif confirmation headline and refined sans-serif operational detail. Include a quiet link to application guidance and an optional post-purchase account-creation invitation below the complete order information. Do not lead with products, newsletter signup or an upsell carousel.

The page must feel reassuring, conclusive and human. No confetti, no celebratory animation, no ambiguous pending status, no oversized marketing photography and no cluttered card dashboard.

## Universal negative prompt

Reject any concept containing generic Shopify styling, excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism, neon beauty colours, orange skin, invented product packaging, hidden totals, surprise delivery charges, forced account creation, tiny form labels, placeholder-only fields, ambiguous payment status, fake security seals, countdown timers, aggressive scarcity, unrelated product carousels, desktop layouts squeezed into mobile frames, or decorative choices that weaken checkout clarity.
