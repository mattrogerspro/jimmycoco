# The Jimmy Coco Three-Step Tanning Routine

A professional-looking self-tan does not need a complicated shelf. It needs three clear stages: prepare an even base, apply the chosen formula with control, then perfect the face, hands, feet and transitions that make the result believable.

The Jimmy Coco range turns those stages into a practical system. The formula creates the colour; the mitt handles broad coverage; the brushes provide precision; and the face mist lets facial colour be maintained separately.

## The complete routine at a glance

| Stage | Purpose | Jimmy Coco role |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Prepare** | Create a clean, even surface | Buff & Glow Mitt used according to its approved three-in-one functions |
| **2. Apply** | Build the main face and body colour | Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé, A-List Face Tanning Mist and Buff & Glow Mitt |
| **3. Perfect** | Soften edges and coordinate the finish | The Body Brush, The Face Brush and controlled reapplication |

## Step 1: prepare

Preparation solves texture before colour makes it visible. Complete exfoliation and hair removal early enough for the skin to settle. Concentrate on elbows, knees, ankles, knuckles and the edges of the feet without treating the skin aggressively.

On application day, begin with clean, fully dry body skin. Allow facial skincare to absorb. Keep heavy oils, deodorant and wet product away from areas you are about to tan unless the approved directions specify otherwise.

Check the tools as carefully as the skin:

- the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) should be clean and dry;
- [The Body Brush](/products/the-body-brush) should be free from old product;
- [The Face Brush](/products/the-face-brush) should have soft, clean bristles;
- the formula should be current and stored as directed.

Preparation is complete when you can apply without stopping to find a product, wash a tool or change clothes.

## Step 2: apply

### Choose the body result

Select [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) in Medium for a softer or more gradual starting point, or Dark for greater depth. Apply to the legs, torso and arms with the Buff & Glow Mitt, working one body section at a time.

Use the guide colour to identify pale gaps and heavy borders. Blend an edge before adding more product.

### Add facial colour separately

Apply the [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) in a fine, even layer. Keep the hairline, brows and jaw controlled, and connect the face to the upper neck rather than creating an exact opaque match.

### Respect the development period

The current Malibu and A-List Face Mist directions specify 6–8 hours. Keep the skin dry, avoid heavy perspiration and wear loose clothing. Rinse each formula according to its own directions.

## Step 3: perfect

Perfection does not mean adding more colour everywhere. It means refining the boundaries.

### Hands and feet

Finish with residual product from the mitt, then use The Body Brush to soften wrists, ankles, knuckles and the outer edges of the feet. Keep palms and soles clear.

### Knees and elbows

Blend with the joint gently bent so product does not miss a crease or collect at one edge. Do not place a fresh full pump directly onto a joint.

### Face and hairline

Use the mist lightly. If the approved product instructions support brush blending, The Face Brush’s densely packed, ultra-soft bristles give control around the hairline. Avoid making an unsupported tool/formula combination merely because it looks convenient.

### Final assessment

After development and rinsing, inspect the result in daylight. Check face to neck, wrist to hand and ankle to foot. Build only after the first result is complete.

## Common routine mistakes

- Treating preparation as a rushed shower immediately before tanning
- Using the same amount on legs, hands, feet and face
- Applying more product instead of blending the guide colour
- Ignoring the neck and décolletage transition
- Leaving no uninterrupted 6–8 hour development window
- Building another layer before seeing the rinsed first result
- Using self-tan as a substitute for sun protection

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need both a mitt and a brush?

The mitt is the efficient tool for broad body coverage. A brush adds control at the edges. Use both if hands, feet and transitions are where your application usually loses precision.

### Should I tan face and body every time?

No. The face is cleansed more often and may need a separate refresh. Keep the finish coordinated rather than following one fixed schedule for every area.

### What is the best first Jimmy Coco routine?

Start with one Malibu shade and the Buff & Glow Mitt. Add the A-List Face Mist for a dedicated facial route and a brush when precision is a genuine need.
