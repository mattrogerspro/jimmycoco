# Homepage Narrative Architecture

## Narrative objective

The homepage must move the customer from aspiration to confident purchase without feeling like a conventional sales funnel.

The commercial sequence is:

1. Create desire
2. Establish authority
3. Reduce choice uncertainty
4. Explain the mechanism
5. Prove the result
6. Curate the products
7. Humanise the expertise
8. Differentiate from alternatives
9. Resolve remaining objections
10. Build the routine
11. Close emotionally and commercially

## Approved chapter order

### 01 — Navigation and utility
Provide calm orientation, consumer shopping routes and a visibly privileged path to shade matching.

### 02 — Hero: red-carpet colour at home
Create the initial emotional promise through model imagery, Jimmy’s presence, product visibility and one dominant guided-shopping CTA.

### 03 — Integrated authority strip
Confirm customer approval, editorial recognition and Jimmy’s professional status without creating a crowded floating overlay.

### 04 — Shade recommendation engine
Become the principal buying mechanism. Ask a small number of clear questions and produce one primary recommendation plus one alternative.

### 05 — Why Hollywood looks different
Explain the mechanism: undertone matching, controlled layering and buildable colour.

### 06 — Real skin, real results
Provide forensic proof with product used, skin tone, number of coats, development time, lighting context and verification.

### 07 — Curated best sellers
Serve ready-to-buy visitors with four clearly differentiated products. Each product must state who it suits and what result it creates.

### 08 — Jimmy Coco editorial story
Connect founder authority to formula and method decisions. This is not biography for its own sake.

### 09 — Professional comparison
Make the difference between Sunless and generic one-tone self-tan legible in seconds.

### 10 — Customer stories by concern
Resolve objections using relatable customers: fair skin, first-time use, deep colour, even fading, transfer and event readiness.

### 11 — Complete the routine
Build average order value through a clear three-stage ritual: Prepare, Apply, Perfect.

### 12 — Final conversion scene
Create the second emotional peak. Reintroduce the personalised match and provide a direct bestseller route.

### 13 — Community, newsletter and footer
Offer secondary engagement without weakening the final purchase moment.

## Emotional progression

- **Arrival:** This is beautiful and relevant to me.
- **Authority:** This expert is credible.
- **Relief:** I do not need to guess which product to choose.
- **Understanding:** I understand why the result looks more natural.
- **Belief:** The proof feels authentic.
- **Desire:** I can picture the result on myself.
- **Confidence:** The product and method are clear.
- **Belonging:** People like me achieve this result.
- **Commitment:** I know what to buy and what happens next.
- **Satisfaction:** The page closes with reassurance rather than pressure.

## Conversion hierarchy

### Primary conversion
Complete the shade match and purchase the recommended product.

### Secondary conversion
Shop a curated bestseller.

### Tertiary conversions
- Explore real results
- Learn Jimmy’s method
- Read customer stories
- Build a routine
- Join the email programme

Tertiary actions must never visually compete with the primary route.

## Storytelling constraints

- Trust must precede major commitment.
- Education must be concise and visually legible.
- Proof should appear before the broad product gallery.
- Founder storytelling must reinforce product value.
- Cross-sell should appear only after product confidence.
- The page must end with a decisive commercial moment, not an Instagram grid.

## Delete test

Every chapter must answer one question that has not already been resolved. If removing a chapter would not reduce comprehension, confidence, desire, trust or conversion, merge or remove it.

## Peak-End rule

The hero is the first emotional peak. The final dark CTA is the second. The experience must not end on low-value utility content. Community and footer content should follow the final CTA quietly and with reduced visual weight.
