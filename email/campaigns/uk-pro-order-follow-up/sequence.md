## Email 3 — The conversation after the mirror

**Preview:** Retail should help a client maintain a result they already value.  
**Primary CTA:** Discuss the retail edit.  
**Secondary reply:** `RETAIL`.

Hi `{{FIRST_NAME}}`,

The natural retail moment is often after the client has seen the result and asks how to keep it looking considered until their next appointment.

That is why the retail conversation should begin with client care, not a crowded product stand. A focused aftercare edit can be relevant where it supports the client's routine: an application mitt, a controlled top-up or a complete glow routine.

Trade pricing and retail contribution depend on the individual partnership and order quantity. Reply `RETAIL` if you would like Matthew to talk through what might be relevant for your setting.
