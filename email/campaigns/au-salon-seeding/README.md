# AU Salon Seeding — Cold Outreach

**Goal:** seed AU spray-tan salons in winter so they're ready before summer.
**Audience:** AU spray-tan salon owners & managers.
**Offer / hook:** pro solution + retail range; email 1 offers a **free sample** + shade guide.
**Channel:** Email + WhatsApp (plain-text cold + branded HTML). ESP: Resend.
**Status:** Draft · positive replies → [`../au-salon-account-flow/`](../au-salon-account-flow/)
