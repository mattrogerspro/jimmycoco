# 01 — Brand DNA

## Brand essence

Professional Hollywood colour, translated into effortless at-home confidence.

## Core idea

Sunless gives customers access to Jimmy Coco’s judgement—not merely his products.

The brand should feel as though the customer is being personally guided by a trusted celebrity tanning artist who understands skin, undertones, preparation, layering and finish.

## Functional value

- Natural-looking colour
- Undertone-aware shade selection
- Buildable depth
- Professional application logic
- Clearer product choice
- Premium at-home convenience

## Emotional value

- Confidence
- Control
- Reassurance
- Aspiration
- Belonging
- Relief from fear of getting self-tan wrong

## Brand personality

### Quietly authoritative
Sunless never needs to shout. Expertise is demonstrated through specificity, proof and restraint.

### Warm
The brand is human, tactile and flattering. It should never feel cold, clinical or sterile.

### Editorial
The experience should feel art-directed, composed and culturally aware rather than promotion-led.

### Reassuring
Customers often fear orange tones, streaking, transfer, dryness, poor fading and choosing the wrong depth. The brand should calmly resolve these concerns.

### Precise
Product suitability, undertone, development time, number of coats and expected result should be explained clearly.

### Aspirational but attainable
Hollywood is the authority cue; effortless at-home use is the accessibility cue.

## Brand tensions

The strongest expression of Sunless balances the following tensions:

- Hollywood / home
- Professional / effortless
- Luxury / warmth
- Editorial / commercial
- Aspirational / believable
- Expert-led / customer-centred
- Minimal / informative

Removing either side weakens the brand.

## What Sunless is not

- A loud influencer beauty brand
- A generic white-label tanning company
- A clinical skincare laboratory
- A discount-driven cosmetics retailer
- A trend-led neon beauty brand
- A conventional Shopify catalogue
- A celebrity vanity project

## Verbal character

Copy should be:

- Direct
- Elegant
- Specific
- Reassuring
- Sensory where useful
- Free from inflated beauty clichés

Prefer:

> Matched to your undertone and desired depth.

Over:

> Unlock your ultimate glow journey.

Prefer:

> Builds natural colour in controllable layers.

Over:

> Revolutionary radiance technology.

## Customer takeaway

After interacting with the brand, the customer should think:

> They understand exactly what makes self-tan look natural, and they can help me choose the right result.
