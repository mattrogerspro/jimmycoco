# Email 03 — Find Your Right Result

## Send timing

Approximately three days after enrolment.

## Job of the email

Reduce choice anxiety by helping the subscriber distinguish desired depth, format and application style.

## Primary message

> The right product starts with the result you want and the way you prefer to apply it.

## Primary CTA

- No completed shade match: **FIND MY RIGHT RESULT**
- Completed shade match: **REVIEW MY RECOMMENDATION**

## Suggested subject-line directions

- Light glow, deeper bronze or gradual build?
- Find the Sunless result that fits you
- Choose by result, not guesswork
- Your ideal tan starts here

## Preview-text directions

- A simple guide to depth, format and development time.
- Match the finish to your routine.
- Your saved recommendation is ready when you are.

## Content architecture

1. Clear headline and short framing copy.
2. Three result routes: subtle, medium/buildable, deeper/event-ready.
3. Brief format guidance: mousse, lotion/soufflé, face-specific or professional format where relevant.
4. One primary CTA.
5. Optional saved-match panel.
6. Quiet reassurance and footer.

## Result routes

Each route should explain:

- visible result;
- likely customer intent;
- degree of buildability;
- typical routine commitment;
- link into the shade-match journey rather than directly asserting a perfect SKU.

Do not imply that skin tone alone determines the desired depth.

## Copy direction

Use plain, useful language. Avoid beauty jargon and vague names without explanation.

Example direction:

> Some customers want a subtle everyday warmth. Others want a more visible bronze for an event. The best choice also depends on whether you prefer a quick application, gradual control or face-specific precision.

## Visual direction

- Light, diagnostic composition.
- Three routes may use restrained columns on desktop but must stack clearly on mobile.
- Use approved result or product imagery only.
- Do not alter customer skin colour to manufacture differences between routes.
- Keep the CTA visually dominant over individual route links.

## Personalisation branches

### Saved recommendation available
Show the product name, selected variant, concise “why it fits” sentence and a link back to the result.

### Quiz started but incomplete
Use **CONTINUE MY MATCH** and resume at the saved step.

### No quiz activity
Use the full educational route and primary quiz CTA.

## Failure modes

Reject the email if it:

- treats shade selection as a simplistic fair/medium/deep skin classification;
- shows digitally recoloured versions of the same person;
- presents too many products;
- uses unverified development-time claims;
- hides the guided recommendation behind category links;
- turns into a catalogue grid.

## Success criteria

- The subscriber understands the principal choice dimensions.
- The email reduces rather than adds complexity.
- Returning quiz users resume without losing progress.
- Saved recommendations are accurate and clearly explained.
- The primary action remains obvious on mobile.
