# The 72-Hour Jimmy Coco Event Tan Plan

For an important event, prepare the skin roughly three days before, apply the final tan about two days before, then use the remaining day to rinse, assess and let the finish settle. Do not make the night before the event your first trial of a new product or shade.

This 72-hour plan uses Jimmy Coco products only: [Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades) for the body, the [A-List Face Tanning Mist](/products/face-mist) for facial colour and the [Buff & Glow Mitt](/products/buff-glow-mitt) with a brush for controlled application.

## Before the 72-hour window: run a trial

If the event matters enough to plan hair, makeup or clothes, it matters enough to trial the tan. Test your selected Malibu shade well in advance. See it in natural daylight, indoor evening light and photography. Wear a neckline similar to the event outfit so you can assess the neck, shoulders and décolletage.

A trial answers questions that no general guide can settle for you:

- Is Medium or Dark the right depth?
- Does one application create the finish you want?
- How does your face routine balance the body?
- How does the colour look with your makeup and clothes?
- How long does the result remain at its best on your skin?

## 72 hours before: prepare

Complete exfoliation and any hair removal early enough for the skin to calm before application. Pay particular attention to elbows, knees, ankles, knuckles and feet, but avoid aggressive last-minute scrubbing.

Check that your mitt and brushes are clean and fully dry. Confirm the products are in date, the bottle has enough for the routine and the current directions still match your plan.

Lay out loose, dark clothing for the development period. A calm setup reduces the temptation to rush one side of the body or skip the precision areas.

## 48 hours before: apply

Begin on clean, fully dry body skin. Apply Malibu Tinted Tan Soufflé to the legs, torso and arms with the mitt. Finish hands, feet, knees and elbows with residual product or The Body Brush.

Apply the A-List Face Mist in a lighter, even layer, then soften the hairline, brows and jaw. Check the neck and décolletage as one transition.

Both consumer products currently specify a 6–8 hour development period. Reserve that time fully. Avoid water, tight clothing and heavy perspiration, then rinse each product as directed.

## 24 hours before: assess, do not panic

Look at the result in daylight after the guide colour has been rinsed away. Check the overall balance before focusing on a tiny area close to the mirror.

Ask:

- Does the face belong to the same finish as the neck and body?
- Are the wrists and ankles softly blended?
- Do hands and feet look lighter and believable?
- Is the depth right with the event outfit?

If the trial and first application were properly planned, the final day should not require a complete second tan. Avoid aggressive exfoliation, long hot soaking and untested correction tricks. If a small adjustment is needed, use only an approved, familiar method.

## Event day: protect the finish

Keep the shower gentle and pat the skin dry. Avoid last-minute treatments or tight clothing that creates friction across freshly tanned areas. Apply makeup after assessing the face and décolletage together.

Self-tan does not provide sun protection. If the event is outdoors, continue to use appropriate sun-protection measures and allow any product to settle without rubbing the tan unnecessarily.

## Timing by event type

| Event | What to prioritise |
|---|---|
| Wedding | Trial well in advance; check against dress neckline, makeup and photography |
| Party or red-carpet-style event | Balance shoulders, arms, hands, face and décolletage in flash and indoor light |
| Holiday | Plan the rinse before travel; remember the cosmetic tan offers no sun protection |
| Photoshoot | Prioritise believable daylight/flash balance over maximum depth |

## Common event-tan mistakes

- Using a new shade for the first time the night before
- Leaving no time for the full development and rinse
- Applying the face as heavily as the body
- Wearing tight clothing during development
- Booking exfoliating or oil-heavy treatments after the tan
- Assuming darker colour will photograph better
- Forgetting that hands, feet and décolletage will be visible

## Frequently asked questions

### Is 48 hours before the event always correct?

It is a useful planning framework, not a substitute for your product directions or trial experience. Your own result, event time and routine may require adjustment.

### Should I apply another full coat the day before?

Only if your trial showed that this is the correct, repeatable method. Do not improvise an extra full layer because the rinsed result initially feels subtle.

### When should I tan for a morning event?

Work backwards from the event and protect the complete 6–8 hour development window plus the rinse and assessment day. Avoid compressing the routine into the final night.

## Make the final day uneventful

Choose your [Malibu shade](/products/tinted-tan-souffle-malibu-beach-choose-from-two-shades), complete a trial and use the 72-hour plan so the event day is about wearing the result—not waiting for it to develop.
