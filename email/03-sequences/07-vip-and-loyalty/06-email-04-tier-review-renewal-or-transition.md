# Email 04 — Tier Review, Renewal or Transition

## Role

Communicate a VIP tier review clearly and respectfully, whether the customer is renewing, moving upward, moving to another level or leaving the current tier.

Tier communication must never feel punitive. A change in commercial status is not a judgment of the customer.

## Trigger

Send only after the programme engine has completed an auditable review and the resulting state is final enough to communicate.

Possible outcomes:

- tier renewed;
- tier upgraded;
- tier maintained through a grace period;
- tier transitioned to a different level;
- VIP status paused because the programme changed;
- customer moved out of the current VIP tier.

## Primary objective

Explain the current status, preserve trust and provide a useful next step without pressuring the customer to spend.

## Renewal route

For a renewed tier:

- thank the customer;
- confirm the tier and review period;
- summarise the benefits that remain active;
- link to the programme overview or account area;
- avoid suggesting guaranteed permanent status.

## Upgrade route

For an upgrade:

- explain the new level plainly;
- state when benefits begin;
- highlight only benefits that are operationally live;
- avoid excessive celebration tied to spending.

## Grace-period route

When a grace period applies:

- state that existing access continues temporarily;
- explain the review date;
- do not frame the grace period as a countdown to spend;
- provide support for customers who believe the status is incorrect.

## Transition or downgrade route

Use neutral language such as:

- `Your programme access is changing`
- `An update to your Sunless VIP benefits`
- `Your current VIP period has ended`

Explain:

- the effective date;
- which benefits change;
- whether unused benefits remain valid;
- what standard services remain available;
- how the customer can query the decision.

Do not use loss-framed language, shame, urgency or spend targets designed to provoke a purchase.

## Programme-change route

When Sunless changes or closes the programme, distinguish that clearly from a customer-specific tier decision. Explain benefit deadlines, outstanding credits and support routes accurately.

## Subject-line territories

- An update to your Sunless VIP status
- Your VIP access has been renewed
- Welcome to your new VIP level
- Important information about your VIP benefits

Use direct language. Do not hide a transition behind a misleading celebratory subject line.

## Visual structure

1. Programme identifier
2. Clear status headline
3. Effective date and concise explanation
4. Current or changed benefits
5. One primary account or support CTA
6. Preferences and footer

## Data requirements

Validate before send:

- current and previous tier;
- review decision and reason code;
- effective date;
- grace-period state;
- available and expiring benefits;
- unused reward treatment;
- market-specific terms;
- support escalation route;
- consent and service-message classification.

A tier notice may have a service component, but marketing content must not be inserted into a service message without the appropriate permission and review.

## Dispute and support handling

Any reply disputing status must create or route to a support case. Automated messaging should pause while the review is open.

## Success criteria

Measure successful account access, benefit understanding, support contacts, correction rate, unsubscribe, complaint rate and retention after transition. A low immediate revenue outcome is not a failure when the message preserves trust and prevents confusion.
