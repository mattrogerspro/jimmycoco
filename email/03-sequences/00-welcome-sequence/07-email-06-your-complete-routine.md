# Email 06 — Your Complete Routine

## Send timing

Approximately ten days after enrolment, provided the recipient has not purchased or moved into a higher-priority lifecycle journey.

## Job of the email

Close the welcome sequence by showing how preparation, application and finish work together. Build confidence and, where relevant, average order value without turning the email into an accessory catalogue.

## Primary message

> Better results come from a simple, controlled routine.

## Primary CTA

- Saved recommendation available: **BUILD MY ROUTINE**
- No recommendation available: **FIND MY STARTING POINT**

## Suggested subject-line directions

- The three steps behind a better result
- Prepare. Apply. Perfect.
- Your simplest route to an even finish
- Complete the routine, not the clutter

## Preview-text directions

- The essential steps before, during and after application.
- A practical routine built around your chosen result.
- Preparation and blending matter as much as the formula.

## Content architecture

1. Short routine-led headline.
2. Three stages: Prepare, Apply, Perfect.
3. One essential action or product per stage.
4. Contextual primary CTA.
5. Brief confidence checklist.
6. Closing brand note and preference controls.

## Stage guidance

### Prepare
Explain exfoliation, dry-area care and timing using approved application guidance. Avoid overloading the email with a full tutorial.

### Apply
Show the recommended product and appropriate tool. Explain controlled, even application in one or two concise points.

### Perfect
Cover blending, face precision or finishing only where relevant to the selected product and routine.

## Merchandising rule

The email may recommend complementary products only when they solve a real application need. Limit the routine to the minimum credible set. Do not bundle unrelated products simply to increase order value.

## Personalisation

Where a saved recommendation exists, build the routine around that product and selected variant. Where a purchase has already occurred, suppress this email and route to post-purchase instructions for the actual purchased items.

## Visual direction

- Linear three-stage composition.
- Accurate source product images.
- Clear stage labels and compact instructional copy.
- One dominant CTA after the routine.
- No more than three product-focused visual modules.

## Confidence checklist

Possible approved points:

- Read the product-specific development guidance.
- Use light, controlled layers.
- Blend hands, feet and dry areas carefully.
- Follow the approved aftercare advice.

Do not publish generic instructions that conflict with individual product directions.

## Failure modes

Reject the email if it:

- recommends accessories with no clear purpose;
- conflicts with product instructions;
- creates a six- or eight-product routine;
- uses generated or inaccurate packaging;
- sends after purchase instead of the appropriate post-purchase guidance;
- introduces pressure or false scarcity as the closing message.

## Success criteria

- The reader understands the complete routine in seconds.
- Cross-sell is restrained and useful.
- Product-specific instructions remain authoritative.
- The welcome journey ends with confidence and a clear next step.
- Non-buyers transition into normal marketing according to engagement and preference state.
